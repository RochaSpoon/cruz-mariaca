## Images

Each `image.find_url` opens a Wikimedia Commons search for the named public-domain work. Pick the file, confirm the license tag says public domain, then paste its direct URL into `image.file_url`. Photos of 2D public-domain paintings are public domain in the U.S.; avoid photos of sculptures or buildings unless the photo itself is licensed for reuse.
