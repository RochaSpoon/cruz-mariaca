## The short version

You are not your appearance. The Catechism says "the human body shares in the dignity of 'the image of God'" (CCC 364), and Scripture says God looks at the heart (1 Samuel 16:7).

## What the Church teaches

"The human body shares in the dignity of 'the image of God': it is a human body precisely because it is animated by a spiritual soul, and it is the whole human person that is intended to become, in the body of Christ, a temple of the Spirit" (CCC 364).

Life and health "are precious gifts entrusted to us by God. We must take reasonable care of them" (CCC 2288).

But "if morality requires respect for the life of the body, it does not make it an absolute value." The Catechism rejects "a neo-pagan notion that tends to promote the cult of the body, to sacrifice everything for its sake, to idolize physical perfection" (CCC 2289).

The Lord told Samuel that man looks at appearances, but the Lord looks at the heart (1 Kings 16:7, in the Douay-Rheims numbering).

The psalmist praises God because he is fearfully and wonderfully made (Psalm 138:14, in the Douay-Rheims numbering).

## Scripture

> "I will praise thee, for thou art fearfully magnified: wonderful are thy works, and my soul knoweth right well."
*Psalms 138:14 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims calls 1 Samuel "1 Kings" and numbers this psalm 138. In other Bibles it is Psalm 139.

## Why this matters today

Feeds are full of edited bodies. The Catechism's warning against idolizing "physical perfection" is aimed at this (CCC 2289).

Caring about your health is not vanity. The Church asks for reasonable care and not obsession (CCC 2288-2289).

## What's at stake

If your worth is your appearance, you will lose it with time. The Church says your worth comes from being made in God's image.

## How to fight it

- **Say one true good thing** about your body each day, such as what it lets you do.
- **Unfollow** accounts that make you feel worse.
- **Do not comment** on other people's bodies.
- **If it takes over your thinking,** talk to someone and see the lesson on eating disorders.
