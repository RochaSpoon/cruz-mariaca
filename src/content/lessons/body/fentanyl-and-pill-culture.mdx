## The short version

One counterfeit pill can kill. The Catechism says drug use except on strictly therapeutic grounds is a grave offense, and calls trafficking "direct co-operation in evil" (CCC 2291). Never take a pill that was not prescribed to you.

## What the Church teaches

"The use of drugs inflicts very grave damage on human health and life. Their use, except on strictly therapeutic grounds, is a grave offense" (CCC 2291).

The Catechism says trafficking in drugs is "direct co-operation in evil" (CCC 2291).

"Life and physical health are precious gifts entrusted to us by God. We must take reasonable care of them" (CCC 2288).

"Abuse of... medicine" is listed as a failure of temperance (CCC 2290).

If someone you know is using, you may have a duty to speak, and the Catechism says we can share in sin by "not hindering" it when we have an obligation (CCC 1868).

## Scripture

> "Or know you not, that your members are the temple of the Holy Ghost, who is in you, whom you have from God; and you are not your own? For you are bought with a great price. Glorify and bear God in your body."
*1 Corinthians 6:19-20 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit and that you were bought at a price.

## Why this matters today

Counterfeit pills can contain deadly drugs, and the person taking them may not know. This lesson does not give medical details. Public health agencies do.

If you or someone you love is in danger, call emergency services at once.

## What's at stake

This is a matter of life and death. The Church says life is a gift to be protected.

## How to live it

- **Take only** what a doctor prescribed to you.
- **Tell a trusted adult** if a friend is using.
- **Call for help** in an emergency.
- **Pray for those** in addiction.
