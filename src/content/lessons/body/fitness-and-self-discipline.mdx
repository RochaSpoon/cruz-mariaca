## The short version

Training the body can be a spiritual discipline. Paul uses the athlete as a model (1 Corinthians 9:24-27), while the Catechism warns against making a cult of the body (CCC 2289).

## What the Church teaches

Paul writes that everyone who competes in the games exercises self-control in all things (1 Corinthians 9:25).

He tells Timothy that bodily training is of some value, but that godliness is valuable in every way (1 Timothy 4:8).

The Catechism says temperance "ensures the will's mastery over instincts" (CCC 1809), and gives "the practice of the moral life" a place for discipline.

But the Catechism says morality "does not make it an absolute value." The Church rejects the idolizing of "physical perfection and success at sports" (CCC 2289).

Care for the body means reasonable care (CCC 2288).

## Scripture

> "Know you not that they that run in the race, all run indeed, but one receiveth the prize? So run that you may obtain. And every one that striveth for the mastery, refraineth himself from all things: and they indeed that they may receive a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible one. I therefore so run, not as at an uncertainty: I so fight, not as one beating the air: But I chastise my body, and bring it into subjection: lest perhaps, when I have preached to others, I myself should become a castaway."
*1 Corinthians 9:24-27 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "But avoid foolish and old wives' fables: and exercise thyself unto godliness. For bodily exercise is profitable to little: but godliness is profitable to all things, having promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come."
*1 Timothy 4:7-8 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul compares the Christian life to a race, and says the prize is not a perishable crown.

## Why this matters today

The gym can be good: it builds discipline, health and friendships. It can also become an idol (CCC 2289).

Obsession, over-training and the use of dangerous substances run against the Catechism's call for temperance (CCC 2290).

## What's at stake

Discipline in one area helps in others. A body you control is a better servant of the spirit.

## How to live it

- **Train regularly,** and offer it up.
- **Rest** as seriously as you train.
- **Avoid shortcuts** that damage you.
- **Keep it in proportion** to prayer, family and work.
