## The short version

Gambling is not always a sin, but it becomes wrong when it deprives someone of what they need, and the passion for it "risks becoming an enslavement" (CCC 2413).

## What the Church teaches

"Games of chance (card games, etc.) or wagers are not in themselves contrary to justice. They become morally unacceptable when they deprive someone of what is necessary to provide for his needs and those of others" (CCC 2413).

"The passion for gambling risks becoming an enslavement" (CCC 2413).

"Unfair wagers and cheating at games constitute grave matter, unless the damage inflicted is so slight that the one who suffers it cannot reasonably consider it significant" (CCC 2413).

The Church also says debts must be paid (CCC 2410), and warns against the love of money (CCC 2445).

## Scripture

> "For they that will become rich, fall into temptation, and into the snare of the devil, and into many unprofitable and hurtful desires, which drown men into destruction and perdition. For the desire of money is the root of all evils; which some coveting have erred from the faith, and have entangled themselves in many sorrows."
*1 Timothy 6:9-10 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says those who want to become rich fall into temptation and many harmful desires.

## Why this matters today

Sports betting apps are on every phone, with bonuses and instant bets. They are designed to make it easy and constant.

A small bet among friends is different. The line is when it harms you or your dependents, or when you cannot stop (CCC 2413).

## What's at stake

Losing money you needed is a form of harm to your family, and chasing losses is a trap.

## How to live it

- **Set a limit** and keep to it.
- **Delete the app** if you cannot.
- **Never bet** money you need.
- **Get help** early. Many places have problem gambling helplines.
