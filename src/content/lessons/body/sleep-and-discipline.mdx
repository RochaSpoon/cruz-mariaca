## The short version

Rest is part of God's design. The Catechism says "human life has a rhythm of work and rest" (CCC 2184), and Jesus told his disciples to rest a while (Mark 6:31).

## What the Church teaches

"Just as God 'rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had done,' human life has a rhythm of work and rest" (CCC 2184).

The Lord's Day helps everyone "enjoy adequate rest and leisure to cultivate their familial, cultural, social, and religious lives" (CCC 2184).

Jesus tells his disciples, "Come apart into a desert place, and rest a little" (Mark 6:31).

The psalm warns that it is vain to rise before light and to eat the bread of sorrow, since God gives sleep to his beloved (Psalm 126:2, Douay-Rheims numbering).

The Catechism lists "abuse of food, alcohol, tobacco, or medicine" under intemperance (CCC 2290). Chronic lack of sleep makes self-command harder, but the Church does not say more than that.

## Scripture

> "It is vain for you to rise before light, rise ye after you have sitten, you that eat the bread of sorrow. When he shall give sleep to his beloved,"
*Psalms 126:2 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "And the apostles coming together unto Jesus, related to him all things that they had done and taught. And he said to them: Come apart into a desert place, and rest a little. For there were many coming and going: and they had not so much as time to eat. And going up into a ship, they went into a desert place apart."
*Mark 6:30-32 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims numbers this psalm 126. In other Bibles it is 127.

## Why this matters today

Phones and late nights cost many people sleep. This lesson has no medical claims, and a doctor is the person to ask if your sleep is a problem.

Rest is not laziness. The Church treats it as a duty and a gift (CCC 2184-2185).

## What's at stake

If you never stop, you cannot hear anything. Rest makes room for prayer.

## How to live it

- **Set a bedtime** and keep the phone out of reach.
- **Keep Sunday** as a rest day.
- **Pray at night** before you sleep.
- **See a doctor** if you cannot sleep.
