## The short version

Scripture says it is better for two to be together than one, and tells believers to confess their sins to one another (Ecclesiastes 4:9; James 5:16). The Church's own form of this is Confession, and many Catholics also add a trusted friend.

## What the Church teaches

The Church does not require a formal accountability partner. It does teach that the Christian life is lived with others and that Confession is the sacrament of healing (CCC 1446).

The Catechism says self-mastery can be helped "at times by the support of disinterested friendship" (CCC 2359).

It says "the virtue of chastity blossoms in friendship" (CCC 2347).

A friend is not a replacement for Confession. Sacramental absolution comes from a priest.

## Scripture

> "It is better therefore that two should be together, than one: for they have the advantage of their society: If one fall he shall be supported by the other: woe to him that is alone, for when he falleth, he hath none to lift him up. And if two lie together, they shall warm one another: how shall one alone be warmed? And if a man prevail against one, two shall withstand him: a threefold cord is not easily broken."
*Ecclesiastes 4:9-12 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Confess therefore your sins one to another: and pray one for another, that you may be saved. For the continual prayer of a just man availeth much."
*James 5:16 (Douay-Rheims)*

Ecclesiastes says that two are better than one, and a threefold cord is not easily broken. James says to confess your sins to one another and pray for one another.

## Why this matters today

Secrecy gives a habit power. Telling someone you trust often weakens it.

Pick a friend who is steady, faithful to the Church and kind. Meet regularly. Do not use the friendship for shame.

## What's at stake

Secrecy makes a habit harder to fight. Having someone ask the direct question helps.

## How to live it

- **Ask** one trusted Catholic friend of your sex.
- **Agree** on what you will share and how often.
- **Keep** going to Confession.
- **Pray** for each other.
