## The short version

AI romantic or sexual companions are a counterfeit of love. The Catechism says pornography "immerses all who are involved in the illusion of a fantasy world," and Magnifica Humanitas asks that we recognize others "as a face not merely as a function" (CCC 2354; Magnifica Humanitas 114).

## What the Church teaches

Pornography "immerses all who are involved in the illusion of a fantasy world" (CCC 2354).

Chastity "tolerates neither a double life nor duplicity in speech" and unites the person (CCC 2338).

Jesus says that lust in the heart is adultery (Matthew 5:28).

Pope Leo XIV writes that the quality of a civilization is its "ability to recognize the other as a face not merely as a function" (Magnifica Humanitas 114).

A chatbot is a program, not a person. It cannot give itself, and cannot be hurt or loved back.

## Scripture

> "But I say to you, that whosoever shall look on a woman to lust after her, hath already committed adultery with her in his heart."
*Matthew 5:28 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus is speaking about desire that treats a person as an object. A chatbot is not a person at all.

## Why this matters today

Many people use these companions because they are lonely. That is real, and the answer is people and community. Tell someone.

Sexual chatbots belong with pornography in the Church's thinking. They are designed to be agreeable and never to say no.

## What's at stake

If the relationship asks nothing of you, it cannot make you more loving.

## How to live it

- **Delete** the app.
- **Reach** out to a real person.
- **Tell** a trusted friend or priest.
- **See** a counselor if loneliness is heavy.
