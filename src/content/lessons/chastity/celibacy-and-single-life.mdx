## The short version

Single life can be lived chastely, by choice or by circumstance. The Catechism says some "profess virginity or consecrated celibacy," and others "live in the way prescribed for all by the moral law, whether they are married or single" (CCC 2349).

## What the Church teaches

"People should cultivate [chastity] in the way that is suited to their state of life. Some profess virginity or consecrated celibacy which enables them to give themselves to God alone with an undivided heart in a remarkable manner. Others live in the way prescribed for all by the moral law, whether they are married or single" (CCC 2349).

"From the very beginning of the Church there have been men and women who have renounced the great good of marriage to follow the Lamb wherever he goes" (CCC 1618).

Some "forgo marriage in order to care for their parents or brothers and sisters, to give themselves more completely to a profession, or to serve other honorable ends" and can "contribute greatly to the good of the human family" (CCC 2231).

Paul says the unmarried man is concerned about the things of the Lord, how he may please the Lord (1 Corinthians 7:32-35).

Jesus says some are eunuchs for the sake of the kingdom of heaven, and that whoever can accept this teaching should accept it (Matthew 19:12).

## Scripture

> "But I would have you to be without solicitude. He that is without a wife, is solicitous for the things that belong to the Lord, how he may please God. But he that is with a wife, is solicitous for the things of the world, how he may please his wife: and he is divided. And the unmarried woman and the virgin thinketh on the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and in spirit. But she that is married thinketh on the things of the world, how she may please her husband. And this I speak for your profit: not to cast a snare upon you; but for that which is decent, and which may give you power to attend upon the Lord, without impediment."
*1 Corinthians 7:32-35 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul writes that the unmarried can attend to the Lord without distraction. He also says marriage is good (1 Corinthians 7:38).

## Why this matters today

Many single people feel their life is on hold. The Church says a single life lived for God is a real vocation (CCC 2349).

Many are single without choosing it. That can be painful, and the Church asks for patience and community.

## What's at stake

If a life is only complete in a couple, then a single person is a failure. The Church says no.

## How to live it

- **Invest** in friends and family.
- **Serve** in your parish.
- **Pray** about your vocation.
- **Be** honest about loneliness.
