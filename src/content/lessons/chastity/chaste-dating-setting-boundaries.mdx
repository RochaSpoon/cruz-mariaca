## The short version

The Catechism says engaged couples should "reserve for marriage the expressions of affection that belong to married love" (CCC 2350). How couples set that line is a matter of prudence, and they should set it together before temptation.

## What the Church teaches

"Those who are engaged to marry are called to live chastity in continence. They should see in this time of testing a discovery of mutual respect, an apprenticeship in fidelity, and the hope of receiving one another from God. They should reserve for marriage the expressions of affection that belong to married love. They will help each other grow in chastity" (CCC 2350).

The means: "self-knowledge, practice of an ascesis adapted to the situations that confront him, obedience to God's commandments, exercise of the moral virtues, and fidelity to prayer" (CCC 2340).

Paul says each should know how to possess his own vessel in holiness and honor, not in the passion of lust (1 Thessalonians 4:4-5).

The Song of Songs says not to stir up or awaken love until it is ready (Song of Songs 2:7).

The Church does not give a list of what is allowed. The Catechism gives the aim and leaves the details to conscience, and to a priest or mentor.

## Scripture

> "That every one of you should know how to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour: Not in the passion of lust, like the Gentiles that know not God:"
*1 Thessalonians 4:4-5 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims calls the Song of Songs the Canticle of Canticles. The refrain about not waking love before it pleases is repeated in that book.

## Why this matters today

Many couples find that the line gets blurry in the moment. Agreeing on it in advance, and having someone to ask, helps.

If you have crossed the line, do not give up. See the lesson on starting again.

## What's at stake

You are working toward giving yourself fully to someone. The Church says the preparation matters.

## How to live it

- **Talk** about limits when you are calm.
- **Avoid** being alone in places that make it hard.
- **Pray** together.
- **Go** to Confession if you fall.
