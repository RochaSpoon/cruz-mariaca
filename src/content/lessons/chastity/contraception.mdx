## The short version

The Church teaches that every marital act must stay open to life. Contraception (pills, condoms, IUDs, sterilization for that purpose, withdrawal) deliberately blocks that. It's grave matter. Natural Family Planning (NFP), which spaces births for serious reasons by abstaining during fertile times, is morally different, and the Church allows it.

## What the Church teaches

Pope Paul VI taught that "each and every marital act must of necessity retain its intrinsic relationship to the procreation of human life" (*Humanae Vitae* 11). He condemned "any action which either before, at the moment of, or after sexual intercourse, is specifically intended to prevent procreation" (*HV* 14). The Catechism repeats this as "intrinsically evil" (CCC 2370).

Sex has two meanings that God joined: union (bonding the spouses) and procreation (openness to children). Contraception splits them on purpose.

## History

Until 1930, every major Christian body taught that contraception was wrong. The Anglican Lambeth Conference was the first to allow it in limited cases (Resolution 15, 1930). Pius XI responded that same year with *Casti Connubii* reaffirming the ban. Most Protestant churches followed Lambeth in the decades after. The Catholic Church is the one that didn't change.

## Paul VI's predictions

In *Humanae Vitae* 17 (1968), Paul VI warned that widespread contraception would lead to:
1. More marital infidelity and a general lowering of morality
2. Men losing respect for women and treating them as instruments of pleasure
3. Governments imposing birth control on populations

Look at the decades since and judge for yourself.

## Why this hits hard today

Most Catholics in the U.S. use contraception, and many have never heard why the Church teaches against it. Hormonal birth control is handed to teenage girls for acne and cramps without much discussion of side effects. Some forms can also act after fertilization, which raises the question of early abortion.

## What's at stake

Contraception is grave matter. A married couple using it with full knowledge and consent is committing mortal sin and needs Confession. Beyond that, it changes how spouses see each other and children, from gift to problem to be managed.

## What to do

- **Learn NFP.** Modern methods (Creighton, Marquette, Sympto-Thermal, Billings) are science-based and, used correctly, very effective.
- **If you're engaged,** take an NFP course before your wedding.
- **If you've used contraception,** confess it and talk with your spouse about stopping.
- **Medical needs:** Hormones used to treat a real medical condition, not to prevent conception, are judged differently (*HV* 15). Talk to a priest and a pro-life doctor.
