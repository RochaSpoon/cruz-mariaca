## The short version

Fornication is sex between two unmarried people. Cohabitation is living together in a sexual relationship without being married. Both are grave sins. Being in love, being engaged, or "planning to get married someday" doesn't change that.

## What the Church teaches

"Fornication is carnal union between an unmarried man and an unmarried woman. It is gravely contrary to the dignity of persons and of human sexuality" (CCC 2353). The Catechism says "free unions" and "trial marriages" offend the dignity of marriage and destroy the very idea of the family (CCC 2390-2391). It says engaged couples should keep continence and reserve the expressions of affection that belong to married love for marriage (CCC 2350).

## Scripture

> "Fly fornication. Every sin that a man doth, is without the body; but he that committeth fornication, sinneth against his own body."
*1 Corinthians 6:18 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Marriage honourable in all, and the bed undefiled. For fornicators and adulterers God will judge."

## Why this matters

Sex says with your body, "I give you all of myself, forever." Marriage is when you say that with words and vows. Sex outside marriage makes the body say something the person hasn't promised. That's why it's a lie, and why it hurts so much when it ends.

## Why this hits hard today

Most couples now live together before marriage. Hookup culture treats sex as entertainment. "Test drive before you buy" sounds logical. But a person isn't a car, and love isn't a trial period. People shouldn't have to audition for a lifetime commitment.

## What's at stake

Scripture lists fornicators among those who "shall not obtain the kingdom of God" (Galatians 5:21). Cohabiting Catholics who are sexually active are living in an ongoing state of grave sin and should not receive Communion until they confess and change the situation. There are also consequences on earth: pregnancies outside of a stable commitment, broken hearts, soul ties that follow you into marriage, and comparisons that poison your future spouse.

## If you're in this situation now

- **Talk to a priest.** He's heard it before, and he'll help you figure out next steps.
- **Stop the sexual relationship** and go to Confession.
- **If you're living together,** the clearest path is to move apart before the wedding or to marry in the Church soon. Many parishes have marriage prep that welcomes cohabiting couples and helps them do this right.
- **If you're dating,** set physical boundaries together, out loud, before you're in a heated moment. Avoid being alone in bedrooms or late at night.
