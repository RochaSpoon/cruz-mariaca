## The short version

Masturbation is the deliberate stimulation of the genitals to get sexual pleasure. The Church teaches it is "an intrinsically and gravely disordered action" (CCC 2352). Sex is meant to be a gift of yourself to your spouse, open to life. Masturbation turns that gift inward. It's sex with no one.

## What the Church teaches

"The deliberate use of the sexual faculty, for whatever reason, outside of marriage is essentially contrary to its purpose" (CCC 2352, citing *Persona Humana* 9). It is grave matter.

The same paragraph says that to judge a person's responsibility, you have to take into account "affective immaturity, force of acquired habit, conditions of anxiety or other psychological or social factors that lessen, if not even reduce to a minimum, moral culpability" (CCC 2352).

Both halves matter. The act is gravely wrong. Your guilt depends on how free you were. Only a priest in Confession can help you sort that out, so bring it there. Don't decide on your own that it "doesn't count."

## Scripture

> "For this is the will of God, your sanctification; that you should abstain from fornication; That every one of you should know how to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour."
*1 Thessalonians 4:3-4 (Douay-Rheims)*

## Why this hits hard today

The world says masturbation is healthy, normal, even recommended. Health classes often teach it that way. So many Catholics feel crazy for struggling. You're not crazy. You're being asked to live a virtue the culture has stopped believing in. It almost always travels with porn, fantasy, and sexting, so fighting one means fighting all of them.

## What's at stake

Grave matter, freely chosen, is mortal sin. The habit also trains you in self-gratification, which carries straight into marriage, where love requires you to put your spouse first.

## How to fight it

- **Confess it plainly.** "I committed impurity with myself, about __ times."
- **Don't sleep with your phone.** Most falls happen at night, alone, with a screen.
- **Go to bed when you're tired.** Lying awake bored is a trap.
- **Get up immediately when tempted.** Change rooms, turn on the lights.
- **Move your body.** Regular exercise helps with stress and restlessness.
- **Guard your thoughts.** You can't stop a thought from showing up. You can refuse to entertain it.
- **Don't be scrupulous.** Involuntary arousal, dreams, and nocturnal emissions are not sins.
- **Be patient.** Many saints fought for years. Persistence, not perfection, is what God asks.
