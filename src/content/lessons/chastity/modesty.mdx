## The short version

Modesty is the virtue that protects what's intimate. It means not putting your body (or someone else's) on display to be used. It's not shame about the body. It's respect for it. It applies to everyone: men and women, clothes, social media, speech, and the way you look at others.

## What the Church teaches

"Purity requires modesty, an integral part of temperance. Modesty protects the intimate center of the person. It means refusing to unveil what should remain hidden" (CCC 2521). It "protects the mystery of persons and their love" (CCC 2522). It "inspires a way of life which makes it possible to resist the allurements of fashion and the pressures of prevailing ideologies" (CCC 2523).

The Catechism admits that the specific forms of modesty vary between cultures, but says that everywhere, modesty is the intuition of the spiritual dignity proper to man (CCC 2524).

## The saints and notable figures

St. John Paul II taught that modesty isn't fear of the body. It's a natural defense against being treated as an object of use. It protects a person until someone can respond to the whole person and not just the body (*Love and Responsibility*, ch. 3).

St. Francis de Sales told his readers to dress neatly and suitably to their state in life, not carelessly and not vainly, and to avoid vanity, peculiarity and fancifulness. He says to "always be neat" and to keep to what is simple, and that modesty in dress depends on age, season, condition and the occasion (*Introduction to the Devout Life*, Part III, ch. 25).

## Why this hits hard today

- **Social media rewards immodesty.** The more skin, the more likes. "Thirst traps" are designed to make strangers lust after you.
- **OnlyFans and similar sites** turn immodesty into income. Many teens see it as normal, or even as a way to take control.
- **Men aren't exempt.** Shirtless gym selfies, crude talk about women, and showing off for attention are immodesty too.
- **Modesty of the eyes.** Jesus puts responsibility for lust on the one looking (Matthew 5:28). No outfit ever excuses lust, harassment, or assault. Modesty is something you choose for yourself, not something you police in other people.

## What's at stake

Deliberately dressing or posting in order to arouse others is a sin against chastity and can be grave, because it leads others into sin (CCC 2284-2287 on scandal). Selling sexual images of yourself is grave matter. More broadly, immodesty teaches you that your worth is your body, which is a lie that leaves people empty.

## Practical guidelines

- **Ask: why am I wearing or posting this?** To look good and appropriate, or to get looks?
- **Dress for the occasion.** Mass requires more care than the beach. You're going to meet God.
- **Before you post:** Would you be comfortable if your grandmother, your future spouse, and Jesus saw it? They all will, eventually.
- **Guys:** Watch your mouth about women. Don't share or ask for sexual images. Ever.
- **Guard your eyes** whatever anyone else is wearing.
- **Don't shame people.** Modesty is a virtue you live, not a weapon you use on others.
