## The short version

The Church allows spouses to space births for just reasons by using infertile periods. The Catechism says periodic continence "is in conformity with the objective criteria of morality" (CCC 2368, 2370).

## What the Church teaches

"For just reasons, spouses may wish to space the births of their children. It is their duty to make certain that their desire is not motivated by selfishness but is in conformity with the generosity appropriate to responsible parenthood" (CCC 2368).

"Periodic continence, that is, the methods of birth regulation based on self-observation and the use of infertile periods, is in conformity with the objective criteria of morality" (CCC 2370).

These methods "respect the bodies of the spouses, encourage tenderness between them, and favor the education of an authentic freedom" (CCC 2370).

The Church rejects "every action which, whether in anticipation of the conjugal act, or in its accomplishment, or in the development of its natural consequences, proposes, whether as an end or as a means, to render procreation impossible" (CCC 2370).

## Scripture

> "And God created man to his own image: to the image of God he created him: male and female he created them. And God blessed them, saying: Increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it, and rule over the fishes of the sea, and the fowls of the air, and all living creatures that move upon the earth."
*Genesis 1:27-28 (Douay-Rheims)*

God blesses man and woman and calls them to be fruitful. The Church reads this as the basis for responsible and generous parenthood.

## Why this matters today

People often ask what the difference is between NFP and contraception. The Church's answer is the nature of the act: one respects the structure of the marital act, the other acts to render it infertile.

NFP requires learning, patience and cooperation between spouses. Many dioceses offer courses.

## What's at stake

The teaching is about both openness to life and self-gift. It is not a license for selfishness, and it does not demand as many children as possible.

## How to live it

- **Take** a course from a certified NFP instructor.
- **Talk** honestly as spouses about your reasons.
- **Ask** your priest if you are unsure.
- **Be** patient. Learning takes time.
