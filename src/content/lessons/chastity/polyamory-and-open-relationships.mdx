## The short version

The Church teaches that married love is exclusive and faithful. The Catechism says it "cannot be an arrangement 'until further notice'" (CCC 1644-1646).

## What the Church teaches

"The love of the spouses requires, of its very nature, the unity and indissolubility of the spouses' community of persons, which embraces their entire life" (CCC 1644).

"Polygamy is contrary to conjugal love which is undivided and exclusive" (CCC 1645).

"By its very nature conjugal love requires the inviolable fidelity of the spouses. This is the consequence of the gift of themselves which they make to each other. Love seeks to be definitive; it cannot be an arrangement 'until further notice'" (CCC 1646).

The Catechism says polygamy "directly negates the plan of God" because it is "contrary to the equal personal dignity of men and women who in matrimony give themselves with a love that is total and therefore unique and exclusive" (CCC 2387).

Adultery is a sin against fidelity even when a partner consents. The Catechism does not make an exception for agreed arrangements (CCC 2380-2381).

## Scripture

> "Who answering, said to them: Have ye not read, that he who made man from the beginning, made them male and female? And he said: For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife, and they two shall be in one flesh. Therefore now they are not two, but one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let no man put asunder."
*Matthew 19:4-6 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus says the two become one flesh and refers to the beginning.

## Why this matters today

Some people say honesty between all adults makes non-monogamy acceptable. The Church answers that marriage is a total gift and that sex outside it is a sin.

It also holds that the person who feels pulled to this lifestyle is loved and called to chastity like everyone else.

## What's at stake

The question is whether love can be divided without being diminished. The Church says total self-giving cannot be shared out.

## How to live it

- **Be honest** with yourself and your partner about what you want.
- **Talk** with a priest.
- **Choose** fidelity.
- **Get** counseling if your marriage is struggling.
