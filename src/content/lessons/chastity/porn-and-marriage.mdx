## The short version

The Catechism says pornography "offends against chastity because it perverts the conjugal act, the intimate giving of spouses to each other" (CCC 2354).

## What the Church teaches

"Pornography consists in removing real or simulated sexual acts from the intimacy of the partners, in order to display them deliberately to third parties. It offends against chastity because it perverts the conjugal act, the intimate giving of spouses to each other" (CCC 2354).

"Conjugal love involves a totality... it demands indissolubility and faithfulness in definitive mutual giving" (CCC 1643).

"By its very nature conjugal love requires the inviolable fidelity of the spouses" (CCC 1646).

The Catechism says that pornography "immerses all who are involved in the illusion of a fantasy world" (CCC 2354).

## Scripture

> "You have heard that it was said to them of old: Thou shalt not commit adultery. But I say to you, that whosoever shall look on a woman to lust after her, hath already committed adultery with her in his heart."
*Matthew 5:27-28 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus extends the commandment against adultery to the lustful look.

## Why this matters today

Many spouses say porn is harmless private entertainment. The Church says it is a betrayal of the marital gift, even when done alone.

If your spouse is struggling, the first step is honesty and compassion. If you are, the first step is telling someone.

## What's at stake

Porn can hurt a spouse, and it can turn the marriage into one of two people serving themselves.

## How to live it

- **Tell** your spouse or a priest.
- **Use** filters and accountability.
- **Go** to Confession.
- **Seek** counseling if the pattern is hard to break.
