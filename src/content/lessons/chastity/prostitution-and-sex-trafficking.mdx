## The short version

Prostitution "is a social scourge," and trafficking turns people into merchandise. The Catechism says the sins involved are grave, and that destitution, blackmail and pressure can reduce the guilt of those who are used (CCC 2355, 2414).

## What the Church teaches

"Prostitution is a social scourge. It usually involves women, but also men, children, and adolescents" (CCC 2355). Where children are involved there is "the added sin of scandal" (CCC 2355).

"While it is always gravely sinful to engage in prostitution, the imputability of the offense can be attenuated by destitution, blackmail, or social pressure" (CCC 2355).

"The seventh commandment forbids acts or enterprises that... lead to the enslavement of human beings, to their being bought, sold and exchanged like merchandise" (CCC 2414).

Pornography feeds demand. The Catechism says it "offends against chastity" and injures all who take part (CCC 2354).

"Rape is the forcible violation of the sexual intimacy of another person" and "an intrinsically evil act" (CCC 2356).

## Scripture

*Proverbs 31:8-9 (Douay-Rheims)*

Proverbs says to open your mouth for the dumb and for the cause of all the children who pass.

## Why this matters today

Many people in prostitution did not choose it freely. The Church makes a distinction between the person who sells under pressure and the one who buys or profits.

If you suspect trafficking, call the police or a national hotline.

## What's at stake

Demand creates supply. The Church asks people to look at their part.

## How to live it

- **Learn** the signs of trafficking.
- **Do not buy** sex or sexual content.
- **Support** survivors' organizations.
- **Report** what you suspect.
