## The short version

The Catechism says Penance is for "those who, since Baptism, have fallen into grave sin," and offers "a new possibility to convert" (CCC 1446). Purity is not lost forever.

## What the Church teaches

"Christ instituted the sacrament of Penance for all sinful members of his Church: above all for those who, since Baptism, have fallen into grave sin... It is to them that the sacrament of Penance offers a new possibility to convert and to recover the grace of justification" (CCC 1446).

"Jesus' look of infinite mercy drew tears of repentance from Peter" (CCC 1429).

"Self-mastery is a long and exacting work. One can never consider it acquired once and for all" (CCC 2342).

"Chastity is a moral virtue. It is also a gift from God, a grace, a fruit of spiritual effort" (CCC 2345).

Jesus does not condemn the woman, and tells her to go and sin no more.

## Why this matters today

Some people feel they are "used goods." The Church says the sacraments offer a real fresh start, not a lesser version.

You may fall again. The Catechism says self-mastery is never acquired "once and for all" (CCC 2342), and it also says to start again.

## What's at stake

Despair is a greater danger than the fall. The Church asks you to return.

## How to live it

- **Go** to Confession.
- **Be honest** and specific.
- **Avoid** the places and apps that lead you back.
- **Find** a spiritual friend.
