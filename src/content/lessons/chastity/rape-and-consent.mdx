## The short version

Rape is "the forcible violation of the sexual intimacy of another person." The Catechism calls it "an intrinsically evil act" that "causes grave damage that can mark the victim for life" (CCC 2356).

## What the Church teaches

"Rape is the forcible violation of the sexual intimacy of another person. It does injury to justice and charity. Rape deeply wounds the respect, freedom, and physical and moral integrity to which every person has a right. It causes grave damage that can mark the victim for life. It is always an intrinsically evil act" (CCC 2356).

"Graver still is the rape of children committed by parents (incest) or those responsible for the education of the children entrusted to them" (CCC 2356).

Sexual acts are meant to be a free gift. Sexuality "is realized in a truly human way only if it is an integral part of the love by which a man and a woman commit themselves totally to one another" (CCC 2361).

Deuteronomy says that if a man finds a betrothed girl in the field and seizes her, only the man shall die and the girl is not guilty (Deuteronomy 22:25-27).

The Catechism says the victim of an assault is not at fault. Freedom and consent are central to the dignity of the person (CCC 2356).

## Scripture

> "But if a man find a damsel that is betrothed, in the field, and taking hold of her, lie with her, he alone shall die: The damsel shall suffer nothing, neither is she guilty of death: for as a robber riseth against his brother, and taketh away his life, so also did the damsel suffer: She was alone in the field: she cried, and there was no man to help her."
*Deuteronomy 22:25-27 (Douay-Rheims)*

The law protects the victim and puts the guilt on the attacker.

## Why this matters today

If you were assaulted, it was not your sin. Tell someone, seek medical help and report to the police if you can. This lesson is not legal or medical advice.

Many people do not understand consent. The Church holds that the person's freedom must be respected, and that the body is not a thing to be used.

## What's at stake

This is a crime and a grave sin. The victim is not to be blamed.

## How to live it

- **Believe** survivors.
- **Never** pressure anyone.
- **Get** medical and legal help if it happened to you.
- **Call** a crisis line.
