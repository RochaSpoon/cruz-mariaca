## The short version

The Church teaches that marriage is between a man and a woman and that persons with same-sex attraction "must be accepted with respect, compassion, and sensitivity" (CCC 1601, 2357-2359).

## What the Church teaches

"The matrimonial covenant, by which a man and a woman establish between themselves a partnership of the whole of life, is by its nature ordered toward the good of the spouses and the procreation and education of offspring" (CCC 1601).

Regarding homosexual acts, the Catechism says "they close the sexual act to the gift of life. They do not proceed from a genuine affective and sexual complementarity. Under no circumstances can they be approved" (CCC 2357).

It also says the people involved "must be accepted with respect, compassion, and sensitivity. Every sign of unjust discrimination in their regard should be avoided" (CCC 2358).

"Homosexual persons are called to chastity" (CCC 2359).

The passage presents marriage as man and woman becoming one flesh. Jesus cites it in Matthew 19.

## Why this matters today

The Church does not claim that same-sex attracted people choose their attraction. The Catechism says its genesis "remains largely unexplained" (CCC 2357).

The Catechism names "disinterested friendship," prayer and sacramental grace as supports, and says this call is hard (CCC 2358-2359). Those who find the teaching difficult deserve patience and respect.

Civil marriage law and the sacrament are different matters. The Church's lesson here is about the sacrament and natural marriage.

## What's at stake

The teaching on marriage and the commandment to love every person go together in the Catechism. Neither should be dropped.

## How to live it

- **Treat** everyone with respect and without mockery.
- **Learn** the teaching from the Catechism itself.
- **Offer** friendship.
- **Find** a priest or ministry that is faithful and kind.
