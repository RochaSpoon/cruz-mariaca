## The short version

Sending nude images is a sin against modesty and chastity, and it can have lasting consequences. The Catechism says modesty means "refusing to unveil what should remain hidden," and pornography "does grave injury to the dignity of its participants" (CCC 2521, 2354).

## What the Church teaches

"Modesty protects the intimate center of the person. It means refusing to unveil what should remain hidden" (CCC 2521).

Pornography "does grave injury to the dignity of its participants (actors, vendors, the public), since each one becomes an object of base pleasure and illicit profit for others" (CCC 2354).

Sharing or forwarding someone's image makes you a participant. We answer for sins we cooperate in "by ordering, advising, praising, or approving them" (CCC 1868).

Paul says to flee fornication and that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:18-20).

Once sent, an image cannot be controlled. Where the person is a minor, there may also be legal consequences, and you should check your local law.

## Scripture

> "Fly fornication. Every sin that a man doth, is without the body; but he that committeth fornication, sinneth against his own body. Or know you not, that your members are the temple of the Holy Ghost, who is in you, whom you have from God; and you are not your own? For you are bought with a great price. Glorify and bear God in your body."
*1 Corinthians 6:18-20 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says you are not your own, and that you were bought at a price.

## Why this matters today

Many people send images under pressure, or because they trust the other person. The Church's concern is for the person who is pressured.

If an image of you was shared, you did not commit that sin. Tell an adult you trust, and report it to the platform.

## What's at stake

An image can follow you for years, and can be used against you.

## How to live it

- **Do not send** and do not forward.
- **Say no** to pressure.
- **Delete** what you receive and tell an adult if it involves a minor.
- **Go** to Confession if you did.
