## The short version

If you were abused, you did not sin. The Catechism says abuse "compounds" the offense and that the young "will remain scarred" (CCC 2389). Vos Estis Lux Mundi (2019) requires clerics to report abuse.

## What the Church teaches

"Connected to incest is any sexual abuse perpetrated by adults on children or adolescents entrusted to their care. The offense is compounded by the scandalous harm done to the physical and moral integrity of the young, who will remain scarred by it all their lives; and the violation of responsibility for their upbringing" (CCC 2389).

The Catechism says that "external pressures" can "diminish the voluntary and free character of the offense" for the person who suffers it (CCC 1860). A victim is not guilty.

Pope Francis's 2019 motu proprio Vos Estis Lux Mundi says that "whenever a cleric or a member of an Institute of Consecrated Life or of a Society of Apostolic Life has notice of, or well-founded motives to believe that" abuse has been committed, "that person is obliged to report promptly the fact to the local Ordinary" (Vos Estis Lux Mundi, Art. 3 §1).

It also requires dioceses to set up "public, stable and easily accessible systems for submission of reports" (Vos Estis Lux Mundi, Art. 2 §1).

Reporting to church authorities does not replace reporting to civil authorities. If someone is in danger, call the police or child protection services.

## Scripture

> "But he that shall scandalize one of these little ones that believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone should be hanged about his neck, and that he should be drowned in the depth of the sea."
*Matthew 18:6 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus says it would be better for a man who scandalizes one of these little ones to have a millstone hung around his neck and be drowned in the depths of the sea.

## Why this matters today

Many survivors carry shame that is not theirs. The Church says the guilt belongs to the abuser and to those who covered it up.

The Church has failed survivors in the past. The reporting norms exist because of that.

## What's at stake

Silence protects abusers. Telling someone is the first step.

## How to live it

- **Tell** an adult you trust.
- **Report** to the police and child protection services.
- **Find** a counselor who works with survivors.
- **Know** that you did nothing wrong.
