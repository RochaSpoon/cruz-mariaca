## The short version

The Church teaches that sex in marriage has two inseparable meanings, union and the transmission of life (CCC 2360-2363; Humanae Vitae 12).

## What the Church teaches

"Sexuality is ordered to the conjugal love of man and woman. In marriage the physical intimacy of the spouses becomes a sign and pledge of spiritual communion" (CCC 2360).

"Sexuality... concerns the innermost being of the human person as such. It is realized in a truly human way only if it is an integral part of the love by which a man and woman commit themselves totally to one another until death" (CCC 2361).

"The acts in marriage by which the intimate and chaste union of the spouses takes place are noble and honorable." Sexuality "is a source of joy and pleasure" (CCC 2362).

"The spouses' union achieves the twofold end of marriage: the good of the spouses themselves and the transmission of life. These two meanings or values of marriage cannot be separated without altering the couple's spiritual life and compromising the goods of marriage and the future of the family" (CCC 2363).

Paul VI wrote that this teaching rests on "the inseparable connection, established by God, which man on his own initiative may not break, between the unitive significance and the procreative significance which are both inherent to the marriage act" (Humanae Vitae 12).

## Scripture

> "Wherefore a man shall leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife: and they shall be two in one flesh."
*Genesis 2:24 (Douay-Rheims)*

The verse says a man leaves his father and mother and cleaves to his wife, and they become one flesh. Jesus quotes it (Matthew 19:5).

## Why this matters today

A common view is that sex is a private pleasure with no wider meaning. The Church says it is a way of giving yourself and, potentially, of giving life.

Many people find this old-fashioned. The Church's claim is that it protects the dignity of both persons.

## What's at stake

If sex means whatever each person wants it to mean, it can be used as a means. The Church says it is a gift of self.

## How to live it

- **Learn** the reasons, not only the rules.
- **Read** Humanae Vitae 8-12.
- **Talk** honestly with someone you trust.
- **Pray** for your future marriage, or your present one.
