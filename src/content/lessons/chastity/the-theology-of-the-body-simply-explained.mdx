## The short version

John Paul II taught that the body is not just a machine. The Catechism says "the human body shares in the dignity of 'the image of God'," and that man and woman each image God's power and tenderness (CCC 364, 2335).

## What the Church teaches

"The human body shares in the dignity of 'the image of God': it is a human body precisely because it is animated by a spiritual soul, and it is the whole human person that is intended to become, in the body of Christ, a temple of the Spirit" (CCC 364).

"Sexuality affects all aspects of the human person in the unity of his body and soul. It especially concerns affectivity, the capacity to love and to procreate, and in a more general way the aptitude for forming bonds of communion with others" (CCC 2332).

"Each of the two sexes is an image of the power and tenderness of God, with equal dignity though in a different way" (CCC 2335).

John Paul II developed this in a long series of Wednesday audience talks given between 1979 and 1984, which are collected as the Theology of the Body.

Dignitas Infinita says "the body participates in that dignity as it is endowed with personal meanings, particularly in its sexed condition" (Dignitas Infinita 60).

## Scripture

> "And God created man to his own image: to the image of God he created him: male and female he created them. And God blessed them, saying: Increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it, and rule over the fishes of the sea, and the fowls of the air, and all living creatures that move upon the earth."
*Genesis 1:27-28 (Douay-Rheims)*

The audiences begin from Genesis, where man and woman are made in God's image and given to each other.

## Why this matters today

Many people treat the body as a tool or as a costume. The Church says it expresses the person.

This lesson is a short summary. The audiences themselves are long and the reader should look at them.

## What's at stake

If the body means nothing, what you do with it means nothing. The Church says it means a great deal.

## How to live it

- **Read** CCC 2331-2336.
- **Look up** the audiences on vatican.va.
- **Ask** what your body says about you.
- **Thank** God for it.
