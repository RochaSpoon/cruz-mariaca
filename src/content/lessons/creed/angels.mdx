## The short version

Angels are real, created spirits with intelligence and will, who serve God. The Catechism calls their existence "a truth of faith" (CCC 328).

## What the Church teaches

"The existence of the spiritual, non-corporeal beings that Sacred Scripture usually calls 'angels' is a truth of faith." The Catechism says "the witness of Scripture is as clear as the unanimity of Tradition" (CCC 328).

Augustine is quoted: "'Angel' is the name of their office, not of their nature." By nature they are spirit; by what they do, they are angels (CCC 329).

They are "purely spiritual creatures" with "intelligence and will," "personal and immortal" (CCC 330). They were created through and for Christ (CCC 331).

They appear throughout salvation history, announcing and serving God's plan (CCC 332). The Church's liturgy joins with them to adore God (CCC 335).

"From infancy to death human life is surrounded by their watchful care and intercession. 'Beside each believer stands an angel as protector and shepherd leading him to life'" (CCC 336).

Some angels fell. The Church teaches that Satan "was at first a good angel, made by God" (CCC 391).

## Scripture

> "See that you despise not one of these little ones: for I say to you, that their angels in heaven always see the face of my Father who is in heaven."
*Matthew 18:10 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Are they not all ministering spirits, sent to minister for them, who shall receive the inheritance of salvation?"
*Hebrews 1:14 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus says that children's angels "always see the face of my Father." The Catechism quotes the tradition that "beside each believer stands an angel as protector and shepherd leading him to life" (CCC 336).

## Why this matters today

Angels in popular culture are cherubs, ghosts or a vague spiritual force. The Church says they are persons, with minds and wills, and not dead people who earned wings (CCC 330).

Some Christians think talk of angels is a distraction from Christ. The Catechism puts Christ at the center of the angelic world (CCC 331).

## What's at stake

If angels are decoration, the unseen world is empty and you are alone in it. The Church says the unseen world is full and ordered around Christ, and that you are not alone.

## How to fight it

- **Learn the Guardian Angel prayer.** It is short.
- **Do not treat angels as fortune tellers.** They are servants of God, not of your curiosity.
- **Notice the angels at Mass.** The Holy, Holy, Holy is theirs (CCC 335).
- **Be careful with the other side.** The Church says the devil is real and fallen (CCC 391).
