## The short version

"Outside the Church there is no salvation" means all salvation comes from Christ through his Body, the Church. It does not mean everyone else is damned (CCC 846-848).

## What the Church teaches

The Catechism restates the old saying in positive form: "all salvation comes from Christ the Head through the Church which is his Body" (CCC 846).

The Second Vatican Council taught that the Church "is necessary for salvation," because Christ is "the one Mediator and the unique way of salvation." Someone who, "knowing that the Catholic Church was made necessary by Christ," refused to enter or remain in it "could not be saved" (Lumen Gentium 14).

The same document says: "Those also can attain to salvation who through no fault of their own do not know the Gospel of Christ or His Church, yet sincerely seek God and moved by grace strive by their deeds to do His will as it is known to them through the dictates of conscience" (Lumen Gentium 16).

The Catechism says this affirmation "is not aimed at those who, through no fault of their own, do not know Christ and his Church" (CCC 847).

It adds that even so, "the Church still has the obligation and also the sacred right to evangelize all men" (CCC 848).

## Scripture

> "Neither is there salvation in any other. For there is no other name under heaven given to men, whereby we must be saved."
*Acts 4:12 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour, Who will have all men to be saved, and to come to the knowledge of the truth."
*1 Timothy 2:3-4 (Douay-Rheims)*

Acts 4:12 says there is no other name by which we must be saved, and 1 Timothy 2:4 says God wishes all to be saved. The Church holds both.

## Why this matters today

Many people think the Church says only Catholics go to heaven. The Council says the opposite for those who through no fault of their own do not know Christ (Lumen Gentium 16).

Others think this teaching makes missionary work pointless. The Catechism says the opposite: it makes evangelizing an obligation (CCC 848).

## What's at stake

If salvation does not need Christ, the cross was not needed. If God abandoned everyone outside the visible Church, he would not be the God the Church describes.

## How to live it

- **Read Lumen Gentium 14-16** and see what it actually says.
- **Do not judge the state of anyone's soul.** The Church does not claim to know.
- **Take the call to share the Gospel seriously,** and do it gently.
- **Remember that the Church also warns Catholics** who neglect grace (Lumen Gentium 14).
