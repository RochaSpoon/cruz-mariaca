## The short version

The Church says it was by apostolic Tradition that she discerned which books belong in the Bible (CCC 120). The Catholic Old Testament has 46 books, and most Protestant Bibles have fewer.

## What the Church teaches

"It was by the apostolic Tradition that the Church discerned which writings are to be included in the list of the sacred books." This complete list is called the canon (CCC 120).

The Catechism says the canon includes 46 books for the Old Testament (45 if Jeremiah and Lamentations are counted as one) and 27 for the New (CCC 120).

The Catholic list includes Tobit, Judith, 1 and 2 Maccabees, Wisdom, Sirach and Baruch. They are in the Catechism's list of Old Testament books (CCC 120).

Scripture is inspired. "God is the author of Sacred Scripture," and he used human authors who were "true authors" (CCC 105-106). The books "firmly, faithfully, and without error teach that truth which God, for the sake of our salvation, wished to see confided to the Sacred Scriptures" (CCC 107).

The Old Testament remains "an indispensable part of Sacred Scripture," because the Old Covenant "has never been revoked" (CCC 121).

## Scripture

> "All scripture, inspired of God, is profitable to teach, to reprove, to correct, to instruct in justice,"
*2 Timothy 3:16 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "But the souls of the just are in the hand of God, and the torment of death shall not touch them. In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die: and their departure was taken for misery: And their going away from us, for utter destruction: but they are in peace."
*Wisdom 3:1-3 (Douay-Rheims)*

The second reading is from Wisdom, one of the books most Protestant Bibles leave out. The Catechism's list of 46 Old Testament books includes them (CCC 120).

## Why this matters today

Protestants and Catholics share the 27 New Testament books and most of the Old Testament, and most Protestant Bibles leave out the seven books mentioned above.

The difference is a real argument about how the canon was settled and by whom. Protestants say the Jewish canon should decide the Old Testament. Catholics say the Church's canon was settled in the early centuries and that it included these books.

## What's at stake

If the list of books is open, then everything built on Scripture is shaky. If it is settled, it was settled by a Church with authority to settle it. The Catholic and Protestant answers differ on who had that authority.

## How to live it

- **Read one of the disputed books.** Wisdom 3 and 2 Maccabees 7 are both good places to start.
- **Notice how the New Testament quotes the Old.** Ask which version it uses.
- **Buy a Bible with the deuterocanonical books,** such as the Douay-Rheims or a study edition.
- **Find out which books your own Bible leaves out,** and why.
