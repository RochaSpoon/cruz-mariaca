## The short version

The Church is the People God gathers, the Body of Christ, and in her own words "the sacrament of salvation" (CCC 752, 780). Catholics believe Christ founded her on the apostles.

## What the Church teaches

The word "church" means the liturgical assembly, the local community and the whole community of believers. The Catechism says these three meanings are "inseparable" (CCC 752).

The Church is "born primarily of Christ's total self-giving for our salvation," anticipated in the Eucharist and fulfilled on the cross (CCC 766).

She is at the same time a visible "society structured with hierarchical organs" and "the mystical body of Christ" (CCC 771).

The Church calls herself a sacrament: "the sign and the instrument of the communion of God and men" (CCC 780). She is Christ's instrument, not Christ's replacement (CCC 776).

The Second Vatican Council states that the Church of Christ "subsists in the Catholic Church," which is governed by the successor of Peter and the bishops in communion with him. It adds that "many elements of sanctification and of truth are found outside its visible confines" (CCC 816, 870).

## Scripture

> "And I say to thee: That thou art Peter; and upon this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it."
*Matthew 16:18 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "And he hath subjected all things under his feet, and hath made him head over all the church, Which is his body, and the fulness of him who is filled all in all."
*Ephesians 1:22-23 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Now you are the body of Christ, and members of member."
*1 Corinthians 12:27 (Douay-Rheims)*

Protestants and Catholics read Matthew 16:18 differently. Many Protestants read the "rock" as Peter's faith, and Catholics as Peter himself. Both agree Christ builds the Church.

## Why this matters today

A common view is that the Church is a human institution run by people who disagree with each other, and that faith is a private matter. The Church answers that Christ did not leave a book alone but a body he sustains on earth (CCC 771).

The scandals are real. The Catechism says the Church "still includes sinners" and calls her "the sinless one made up of sinners" (CCC 867).

## What's at stake

If the Church is just an organization, you can leave it the way you leave a club. If she is Christ's body, leaving her is leaving where he said he would be found.

## How to live it

- **Separate Christ from his people** when you are angry. The Church says her holiness comes from him, not from her members (CCC 811).
- **Find a parish** and go for a month before deciding.
- **Learn what she actually claims.** Most arguments are with a caricature.
- **Be honest about the scandals.** The Church is.
