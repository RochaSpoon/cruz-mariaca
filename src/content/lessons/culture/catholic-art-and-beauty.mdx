## The short version

The Church holds that beauty is a way to God. The Catechism says "truth is beautiful in itself," and that sacred art should evoke "the transcendent mystery of God" (CCC 2500-2502).

## What the Church teaches

"The practice of goodness is accompanied by spontaneous spiritual joy and moral beauty. Likewise, truth carries with it the joy and splendor of spiritual beauty" (CCC 2500).

Because man is made in God's image, he "expresses the truth of his relationship with God the Creator by the beauty of his artistic works." Art is "a freely given superabundance of the human being's inner riches" (CCC 2501).

"Sacred art is true and beautiful when its form corresponds to its particular vocation: evoking and glorifying, in faith and adoration, the transcendent mystery of God" (CCC 2502).

The Catechism says bishops "should see to the promotion of sacred art, old and new" (CCC 2503).

John Paul II wrote a Letter to Artists in 1999. He wrote that "the theme of beauty is decisive for a discourse on art," and that in perceiving that all he had created was good, God "saw that it was beautiful as well" (Letter to Artists 3).

## Scripture

> "And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: Behold, I have called by name Beseleel the son of Uri the son of Hur of the tribe of Juda, And I have filled him with the spirit of God, with wisdom and understanding, and knowledge in all manner of work. To devise whatsoever may be artificially made of gold, and silver, and brass, Of marble, and precious stones, and variety of wood."
*Exodus 31:1-5 (Douay-Rheims)*

God says he has filled Bezalel with his spirit to make works of art for the tabernacle. This is one of the places in the Bible where craft is called a gift of God.

## Why this matters today

Many people feel more at home in a gallery or a concert hall than in a church. The Church's answer is that beauty has always been one of her ways of speaking.

Critics say the Church spent fortunes on buildings that should have gone to the poor. The Catechism teaches both love of the poor (CCC 2443) and the promotion of sacred art (CCC 2503).

## What's at stake

If beauty has nothing to do with truth, worship can be ugly and nobody loses anything. The Church thinks that is not true.

## How to live it

- **Go to a beautiful church** when it is quiet and stay for ten minutes.
- **Look at an icon or painting** of one Gospel scene for as long as you would look at a screen.
- **Make something** and offer it up.
- **Read the Letter to Artists.** It is short and written to everyone, not just painters.
