## The short version

Many of the holidays we know come from the Church's calendar. Christmas and Easter are the center of that calendar, and saints' days are on it (CCC 1163-1173).

## What the Church teaches

The Church year celebrates "the saving work of her divine Spouse in a sacred commemoration on certain days throughout the course of the year" (CCC 1163).

Easter is "the Feast of feasts" (CCC 1169). The cycle of feasts around the Incarnation, "Annunciation, Christmas, Epiphany," commemorates "the beginning of our salvation" (CCC 1171).

The Church also keeps days for Mary (CCC 1172) and for the martyrs and other saints (CCC 1173).

The Code of Canon Law says Ash Wednesday and Good Friday are days of fasting and abstinence, and that Lent is a season of penance (can. 1250-1251). Mardi Gras, "Fat Tuesday," is the day before Ash Wednesday, which is where the name comes from.

All Saints' Day is on November 1 (GIRM 346). The evening before it is All Hallows' Eve, which became Halloween.

## Scripture

> "Let no man therefore judge you in meat or in drink, or in respect of a festival day, or of the new moon, or of the sabbaths, Which are a shadow of things to come, but the body is of Christ."
*Colossians 2:16-17 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul tells the Colossians not to let anyone judge them over feasts. The Church reads him as saying feasts are shadows of what is in Christ, not that feasts are bad.

## Why this matters today

A lot of holidays have been emptied of their meaning. People celebrate Christmas without Christ, and Easter with only a bunny.

Some Christians avoid holidays with old roots out of caution. The Church says the feasts have a real Christian meaning (CCC 1163-1173).

## What's at stake

If a holiday is just a day off, it changes nothing. If it points at something, it gives the year a shape.

## How to live it

- **Keep Advent before Christmas** and the twelve days after.
- **Go to Mass on a feast day** for a saint whose name you carry.
- **Explain one holiday's real origin** to a friend this year.
- **Do not look down on people who just enjoy the day.** Start with what you share.
