## The short version

John Paul II asked historians to reconstruct the Inquisition "as precise as possible" before judging it. The Church's own theologians say to avoid both apologetics and "unwarranted laying of blame" (ITC, Memory and Reconciliation, 2000).

## What the Church teaches

The ITC document says: "Both an apologetics that seeks to justify everything and an unwarranted laying of blame, based on historically untenable attributions of responsibility, must be avoided" (Memory and Reconciliation, 2000).

It quotes John Paul II on the Inquisition: the Church "may not intend to perform an act of natural ethics, which the request for pardon is, without first being exactly informed concerning the situation of that time" (Memory and Reconciliation, 2000).

He also said the Magisterium may not "rely on images of the past steered by public opinion, since these are frequently highly charged with passionate emotion which impedes serene and objective diagnosis" (Memory and Reconciliation, 2000).

The first step, he said, is asking historians "to offer help toward a reconstruction, as precise as possible, of the events, of the customs, of the mentality of the time" (Memory and Reconciliation, 2000).

## Scripture

> "And when his disciples James and John had seen this, they said: Lord, wilt thou that we command fire to come down from heaven, and consume them? And turning, he rebuked them, saying: You know not of what spirit you are. The Son of man came not to destroy souls, but to save. And they went into another town."
*Luke 9:54-56 (Douay-Rheims)*

The disciples ask to call down fire on a village. Jesus rebukes them and says He came to save lives, not destroy them.

## Why this matters today

This lesson does not give a body count or a verdict. Those need historians, and the sources I have fetched do not contain them.

What the Church says plainly is that using coercion against conscience in matters of faith is contrary to the Gospel. For that, see Vatican II's declaration on religious liberty.

## What's at stake

The Church has asked pardon for sins of its members while asking that facts be established first.

## How to live it

- **Be** wary of both the black legend and the defensive legend.
- **Read** specialist historians.
