## The short version

Vatican II (1962-1965) produced four constitutions. Sacrosanctum Concilium says the liturgy is "the summit toward which the activity of the Church is directed" and that "the use of the Latin language is to be preserved in the Latin rites" (SC 10, 36).

## What the Church teaches

Sacrosanctum Concilium 10: "the liturgy is the summit toward which the activity of the Church is directed; at the same time it is the font from which all her power flows."

Sacrosanctum Concilium 14 asks for "fully conscious, and active participation in liturgical celebrations," which it calls "their right and duty by reason of their baptism."

Sacrosanctum Concilium 36 §1: "Particular law remaining in force, the use of the Latin language is to be preserved in the Latin rites." §2 allows the mother tongue to be extended, "in the first place to the readings and directives, and to some of the prayers and chants."

Dei Verbum 10: "Sacred tradition and Sacred Scripture form one sacred deposit of the word of God, committed to the Church."

Gaudium et Spes 1: "The joys and the hopes, the griefs and the anxieties of the men of this age, especially those who are poor or in any way afflicted, these are the joys and hopes, the griefs and anxieties of the followers of Christ."

## Scripture

> "Going therefore, teach ye all nations; baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and behold I am with you all days, even to the consummation of the world."
*Matthew 28:19-20 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus sends the apostles to all nations. The council read its mission in this light.

## Why this matters today

Debates about the council are often about what it required and what was done in its name. The best check is to read the texts.

The council did not abolish Latin in its text. How it was applied is a separate question.

## What's at stake

The documents are public and short enough to read. The Church asks its members to receive them.

## How to live it

- **Read** Sacrosanctum Concilium 1-14.
- **Check** a claim about "what Vatican II said" against the text.
