## The short version

Diligence is doing your duties with love, even the dull ones. Paul tells believers to do whatever they do from the heart, as for the Lord (Colossians 3:23), and the Catechism says work is a duty (CCC 2427).

## What the Church teaches

Paul says that whatever you do, you should do it from the heart, as for the Lord and not for men (Colossians 3:23).

The Catechism says "work is a duty," and that it "honors the Creator's gifts and the talents received from him" (CCC 2427).

Proverbs tells the sluggard to go to the ant and consider her ways and be wise (Proverbs 6:6-11).

The Catechism describes acedia as refusing "the joy that comes from God" and being "repelled by divine goodness" (CCC 2094).

Rest is part of the rhythm of life too (CCC 2184-2185).

## Scripture

> "Whatsoever you do, do it from the heart, as to the Lord, and not to men: Knowing that you shall receive of the Lord the reward of inheritance. Serve ye the Lord Christ."
*Colossians 3:23-24 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Go to the ant, O sluggard, and consider her ways, and learn wisdom: Which, although she hath no guide, nor master, nor captain, Provideth her meat for herself in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest."
*Proverbs 6:6-8 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul gives the motive for work as for the Lord. Proverbs gives a simple picture of preparation.

## Why this matters today

Sloth often looks like busyness without purpose, or screens in place of tasks. The cure is a small start.

If low motivation is persistent, it could be a health issue. Talk to a doctor.

## What's at stake

If you never do what you should, life drifts.

## How to live it

- **Start** the thing you are avoiding for ten minutes.
- **Offer** the boring task to God.
- **Keep** a simple routine.
- **Rest** on purpose.
