## The short version

Envy is sadness at another person's good and the desire to have it, even unjustly (CCC 2539). It is not just wanting what someone has. It's being upset that they have it. In its worst form, envy wishes them harm.

## What the Church teaches

The Catechism calls envy a capital sin and says that when it wishes grave harm on a neighbor, it is a mortal sin (CCC 2539). St. Augustine called it "the diabolical sin." St. Gregory the Great taught that from envy are born hatred, detraction, calumny, joy at another's misfortune, and displeasure at his prosperity (CCC 2539, citing *Moralia* XXXI.45).

## Scripture

> "But by the envy of the devil, death came into the world."
*Wisdom 2:24 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Soundness of heart is the life of the flesh: but envy is the rottenness of the bones."
*Proverbs 14:30 (Douay-Rheims)*

Cain killed Abel because God accepted Abel's offering and not his (Genesis 4). The first murder in the Bible was an envy murder. The chief priests handed Jesus over "out of envy" (Matthew 27:18).

## The Fathers and saints

> "Would you like to see God glorified by you? Then rejoice in your brother's progress and you will give glory to God."
*St. John Chrysostom, Homilies on Romans 7.3 (quoted in CCC 2540)*

## Why this hits hard today

Social media is an envy machine. You see everyone's highlight reel and compare it to your behind-the-scenes. Studies keep linking heavy social media use with anxiety and depression in teens, and comparison is a big part of it. Envy shows up as: hating on someone who's succeeding, secretly enjoying when a "perfect" person fails, gossiping to bring someone down, or quitting things because someone else is better.

## What's at stake

Envy makes you miserable and blind. It can't enjoy anything, because someone always has more. It leads straight to detraction, calumny, and hatred, which can be grave sins. Left alone, it turns a person into a Cain.

## How to fight it

- **Name it.** "I'm envious of ___." Confess it.
- **Pray for the person by name,** specifically for more of the blessing you envy.
- **Congratulate them** out loud and mean it.
- **Count your gifts.** Gratitude starves envy.
- **Cut the feed.** If an account makes you envious every time, unfollow it.
- **Remember the goal.** You aren't competing with them for heaven. There's room.
