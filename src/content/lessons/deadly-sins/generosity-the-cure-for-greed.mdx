## The short version

Giving is an act of trust in God. Paul says God loves a cheerful giver (2 Corinthians 9:6-7), and the Catechism says "the goods we possess are not ours, but theirs" (CCC 2446).

## What the Church teaches

Paul says that the one who sows sparingly will reap sparingly, and that God loves a cheerful giver (2 Corinthians 9:6-7).

Paul recalls the Lord's words that it is more blessed to give than to receive (Acts 20:35).

Jesus praises the poor widow who gave two small coins out of her need, more than the rich who gave from their abundance (Luke 21:1-4).

"Love for the poor is incompatible with immoderate love of riches or their selfish use" (CCC 2445).

The Catechism says the Church's love for the poor "is a part of her constant tradition" (CCC 2444).

## Scripture

> "Now this I say: He who soweth sparingly, shall also reap sparingly: and he who soweth in blessings, shall also reap blessings. Every one as he hath determined in his heart, not with sadness, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver."
*2 Corinthians 9:6-7 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "And looking on, he saw the rich men cast their gifts into the treasury. And he saw also a certain poor widow casting in two brass mites. And he said: Verily I say to you, that this poor widow hath cast in more than they all: For all these have of their abundance cast into the offerings of God: but she of her want, hath cast in all the living that she had."
*Luke 21:1-4 (Douay-Rheims)*

Both passages place generosity in the heart. The gift is not measured by size.

## Why this matters today

Many people feel they do not have enough to give. The widow shows that the gift is measured by what it costs you.

Greed is not only about rich people. Anyone can hold on tightly.

## What's at stake

What you cannot give, you do not own. It owns you.

## How to live it

- **Give** before you spend.
- **Give** in secret.
- **Give** time as well as money.
- **Pray** for the poor by name.
