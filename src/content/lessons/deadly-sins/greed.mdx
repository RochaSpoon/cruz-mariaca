## The short version

Greed (avarice) is a disordered love of money and possessions. It's not about how much you have. A poor person can be greedy and a rich person can be generous. It's about whether money serves you or you serve it.

## What the Church teaches

The Tenth Commandment forbids greed and the desire to amass earthly goods without limit (CCC 2536). The goods of creation are meant for everyone (CCC 2402-2403). Giving to the poor is not optional charity for nice people. It is justice (CCC 2446).

## Scripture

> "For the desire of money is the root of all evils."
*1 Timothy 6:10 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "No man can serve two masters... You cannot serve God and mammon."
*Matthew 6:24 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Thou fool, this night do they require thy soul of thee: and whose shall those things be which thou hast provided?"
*Luke 12:20 (Douay-Rheims)*

The rich young man walked away sad because he had great possessions (Mark 10:22). He's the only person in the Gospels who was personally invited to follow Jesus and said no.

## The Fathers and saints

> "Not to enable the poor to share in our goods is to steal from them and deprive them of life. The goods we possess are not ours, but theirs."
*St. John Chrysostom, Homily on Lazarus 2.5 (quoted in CCC 2446)*

St. Basil the Great preached that the bread you hoard belongs to the hungry, and the cloak in your closet belongs to the naked (*Homily on Luke 12:18*, "I will tear down my barns"). [VERIFY translation]

## Why this hits hard today

Hustle culture, crypto get-rich-quick, sports betting apps, "passive income," luxury influencer content. A lot of it promises that the right amount of money will finally make you happy. It doesn't. Greed also hides in respectable places: never giving, always "saving for later," thinking your money is only yours.

Note: Wanting to earn well, build a business, and provide for your family is good. Work and profit aren't sins. The question is whether God and people come first.

## What's at stake

The rich man in Luke 16 went to hell, and Scripture lists the greedy among those who "shall not possess the kingdom of God" (1 Corinthians 6:10). Greed also leads to theft, fraud, cheating, and exploiting workers, which the Church calls sins that cry to heaven (CCC 1867).

## How to fight it

- **Give first.** Set aside a percentage before you spend anything. The biblical tithe is 10 percent.
- **Give to real people.** Not only causes. Buy someone a meal.
- **Ask before buying:** "Do I need this, or do I want to feel something?"
- **Pay people fairly and on time** (James 5:4).
- **Pray with Luke 12:15-21** (the rich fool).
