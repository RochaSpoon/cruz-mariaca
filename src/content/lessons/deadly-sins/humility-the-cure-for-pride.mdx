## The short version

Humility is the virtue against pride. The Catechism says "humility is the foundation of prayer" (CCC 2559) and tells the baptized to "train himself to live in humility" (CCC 2540).

## What the Church teaches

"He who humbles himself will be exalted; humility is the foundation of prayer" (CCC 2559).

"Envy often comes from pride; the baptized person should train himself to live in humility" (CCC 2540).

Jesus says to learn from him, because he is meek and humble of heart, and that his followers will find rest for their souls (Matthew 11:29).

Paul says to have among yourselves the mind that was in Christ Jesus, who emptied himself and took the form of a servant (Philippians 2:5-8).

The Catechism lists pride first among the capital sins (CCC 1866).

## Scripture

> "For let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus: Who being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: But emptied himself, taking the form of a servant, being made in the likeness of men, and in habit found as a man. He humbled himself, becoming obedient unto death, even to the death of the cross."
*Philippians 2:5-8 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul uses Christ as the pattern. The Church reads humility as truth about yourself before God.

## Why this matters today

Humility is often confused with low self-esteem. The Church's version is accurate self-knowledge: gifts are real, and they are gifts.

You cannot grow in humility by announcing it. Practice it by serving.

## What's at stake

Pride blocks prayer and love. Humility opens both.

## How to live it

- **Thank** someone who helped you.
- **Take** the lowest seat once.
- **Admit** a mistake without excuses.
- **Serve** without being seen.
