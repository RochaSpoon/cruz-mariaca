## The short version

Lust is "disordered desire for or inordinate enjoyment of sexual pleasure" (CCC 2351). It means wanting sexual pleasure for its own sake, cut off from love, marriage, and life. Sex is good. God made it. Lust takes something holy and uses it, and uses people with it.

## What the Church teaches

Sexual pleasure is morally disordered when sought for itself, isolated from its procreative and unitive purposes (CCC 2351). The Church teaches that sexual acts belong only in marriage between a man and a woman. The sins against chastity include lust, masturbation, fornication, pornography, prostitution, and rape (CCC 2351-2356), as well as homosexual acts (CCC 2357), adultery, and others (CCC 2380-2391). Each of these is grave matter.

## Scripture

> "But I say to you, that whosoever shall look on a woman to lust after her, hath already committed adultery with her in his heart. And if thy right eye scandalize thee, pluck it out and cast it from thee. For it is expedient for thee that one of thy members should perish, rather than that thy whole body be cast into hell."
*Matthew 5:28-29 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Fly fornication... Or know you not, that your members are the temple of the Holy Ghost, who is in you, whom you have from God; and you are not your own? For you are bought with a great price. Glorify and bear God in your body."
*1 Corinthians 6:18-20 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus doesn't literally want you to cut out your eye. He's saying: cut out whatever leads you to sin, no matter what it costs, because hell is worse.

## The Fathers and saints

> "Give me chastity and continency, only not yet."
*St. Augustine, Confessions VIII.7.17 (Pusey translation)*

Augustine's honest prayer from his years of sexual sin. He wanted to be free, but not yet. He later called his habit a chain he forged himself (*Confessions* VIII.5.10). He broke free, became a bishop, and is now a Doctor of the Church.

St. Thomas Aquinas, following St. Gregory, lists what he calls the "daughters" of lust, what it does to you over time: blindness of mind, thoughtlessness, inconstancy, rashness, self-love, hatred of God, love of this world, and despair of the world to come (*ST* II-II, q. 153, a. 5). In other words, lust makes you stupid, unstable, selfish, and eventually indifferent to heaven.

St. John Paul II taught that the opposite of love is not hate but using. Lust reduces another person to an object for my satisfaction (*Love and Responsibility*; *Theology of the Body*).

## Why this hits hard today

Lust is the water we swim in: porn on every phone, sexual content in shows and music, hookup apps, sexting, thirst traps. Teens are exposed before they're old enough to understand it. Almost nobody tells young people that chastity is possible, let alone good.

## What's at stake

Sins against chastity are grave matter. Committed with full knowledge and consent, they are mortal sins. Scripture says fornicators, adulterers, and the impure "shall not obtain the kingdom of God" (Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9-10). On earth, lust trains you to use people, damages your capacity for real love, and wounds your future marriage before it starts.

## How to fight it

- **Guard your eyes.** Job "made a covenant with [his] eyes" (Job 31:1). Look away fast.
- **Cut off the source.** Accountability software, no phone in the bedroom, unfollow accounts. This is "pluck it out."
- **Confess often.** Weekly if you need to. Grace is real.
- **Tell someone.** A priest, a parent, a trusted friend. Lust grows in secret.
- **Fill the space.** Exercise, sleep, real friendships, real work. Lust attacks the bored and lonely.
- **Pray to Mary** and St. Joseph, guardians of purity.

## If you've fallen

Get up and go to Confession. You are not your sin. Augustine was worse, and he's a saint. "And such some of you were; but you are washed, but you are sanctified, but you are justified in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ" (1 Corinthians 6:11).
