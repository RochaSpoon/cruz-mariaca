## The short version

Pride is the disordered love of your own excellence. It says, "I am the measure. I decide what's good. I don't need anyone, including God." It is the first sin in history (the fall of Satan) and the first sin of humanity (Adam and Eve wanting to "be as gods," Genesis 3:5).

## What the Church teaches

Pride is listed first among the capital sins (CCC 1866). They're called capital because other sins come from them. St. Gregory the Great called pride the queen of the vices, and the root the other seven grow from (*Moralia* XXXI.45). St. Thomas Aquinas teaches that pride is the most grievous sin by its nature, because it is a direct turning away from God (*ST* II-II, q. 162, a. 6).

## Scripture

> "Pride is the beginning of all sin."
*Ecclesiasticus 10:15 (Douay-Rheims; Sirach 10:13 in modern Bibles)*

> "Pride goeth before destruction: and the spirit is lifted up before a fall."
*Proverbs 16:18 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble."
*James 4:6 (Douay-Rheims)*

## The Fathers and saints

> "And what is pride but the craving for undue exaltation?"
*St. Augustine, City of God XIV.13 (Dods translation)*

Augustine says two loves built two cities. The earthly city is built by love of self, even to contempt of God. The City of God is built by love of God, even to contempt of self (*City of God* XIV.28).

> "Pride leads to every other vice: it is the complete anti-God state of mind."
*C.S. Lewis (Anglican), Mere Christianity, Book III, ch. 8*

## Why this hits hard today

Our culture runs on pride and calls it self-esteem. "Follow your heart." "Be your own person." "Your truth." Social media rewards you for curating an image of yourself and measuring your worth in likes. The hardest part about pride is that the proud person almost never thinks he is proud. He thinks he's right.

Signs of pride: you can't admit you were wrong. You need credit. You compare yourself constantly. You can't take correction. You think the rules (including God's) are for other people. You won't go to Confession because "I'm a good person."

## What's at stake

Pride makes repentance impossible, because you can't repent of what you won't admit. That's why it's so deadly. Satan, the proudest creature, is fixed forever in refusing to serve. Pride left unchecked ends the same way.

## How to fight it

- **Practice humility on purpose.** Humility is the truth about yourself: everything good you have is a gift (1 Corinthians 4:7).
- **Say "I was wrong" out loud** when you were. Don't add "but."
- **Do hidden good.** Help someone and tell no one (Matthew 6:3-4).
- **Accept correction** without defending yourself first.
- **Pray the Litany of Humility** (attributed to Cardinal Rafael Merry del Val).
- **Go to Confession.** Kneeling and naming your sins is the most humbling thing a Catholic does.
