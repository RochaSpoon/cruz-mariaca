## The short version

Being good can become another way to look down on others. Jesus told the parable of the Pharisee and the tax collector to those "who trusted in themselves as just, and despised others" (Luke 18:9-14).

## What the Church teaches

Luke introduces the parable as spoken to some who trusted in themselves that they were just, and despised others (Luke 18:9).

The Pharisee thanks God that he is not like other men. The tax collector will not even lift his eyes, and asks God to be merciful to him, a sinner (Luke 18:11-13).

Jesus says the tax collector went home justified, and that everyone who exalts himself will be humbled (Luke 18:14).

The Catechism lists two kinds of presumption: trusting in one's own capacities "hoping to be able to save himself without help from on high," and presuming on God's mercy (CCC 2092).

In Matthew 23 Jesus condemns those who do their works to be seen and who say but do not do (Matthew 23:3-5).

## Scripture

> "And to some who trusted in themselves as just, and despised others, he spoke also this parable: Two men went up into the temple to pray: the one a Pharisee, and the other a publican. The Pharisee standing, prayed thus with himself: O God, I give thee thanks that I am not as the rest of men, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, as also is this publican. I fast twice in a week: I give tithes of all that I possess. And the publican, standing afar off, would not so much as lift up his eyes towards heaven; but struck his breast, saying: O God, be merciful to me a sinner. I say to you, this man went down into his house justified rather than the other: because every one that exalteth himself, shall be humbled: and he that humbleth himself, shall be exalted."
*Luke 18:9-14 (Douay-Rheims)*

The parable is aimed at people of faith. It is a warning to anyone who is religious.

## Why this matters today

Religious people can look down on others and call it standards. Non-religious people can do the same.

The remedy is not to lower your standards. It is to remember that you also need mercy.

## What's at stake

A person can keep every rule and still miss the point.

## How to live it

- **Pray** the tax collector's prayer.
- **Notice** when you feel superior.
- **Befriend** someone you would normally look down on.
- **Go to Confession** and tell the truth.
