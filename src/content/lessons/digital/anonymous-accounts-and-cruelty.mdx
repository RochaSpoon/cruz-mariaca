## The short version

Nothing is hidden from God, so anonymity does not change what is right. Jesus says that what is whispered will be heard in the light (Luke 12:2-3), and the Catechism forbids what injures a neighbor's reputation (CCC 2477).

## What the Church teaches

Jesus says that nothing is covered that shall not be revealed, and that what you have spoken in darkness shall be heard in the light (Luke 12:2-3).

Hebrews says that no creature is invisible in God's sight, and that all things are naked and open to his eyes (Hebrews 4:13).

"Respect for the reputation of persons forbids every attitude and word likely to cause them unjust injury" (CCC 2477).

Calumny and detraction "offend against the virtues of justice and charity" (CCC 2479).

Responsibility is shared. We answer for sins we take part in "by ordering, advising, praising, or approving them" (CCC 1868).

## Scripture

> "For there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed: nor hidden, that shall not be known. For whatsoever things you have spoken in darkness, shall be published in the light: and that which you have spoken in the ear in the chambers, shall be preached on the housetops."
*Luke 12:2-3 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Neither is there any creature invisible in his sight: but all things are naked and open to his eyes, to whom our speech is."
*Hebrews 4:13 (Douay-Rheims)*

Both passages say that nothing is hidden. The Church takes them as a reminder that what you do unseen is still seen.

## Why this matters today

Anonymous accounts let people say what they would never say to a face. That is the point of them, and the problem.

Cruelty online has driven some people to despair. If someone you know is being targeted, tell an adult.

## What's at stake

Who you are when no one is watching is who you are.

## How to live it

- **Ask** whether you would say it with your name on it.
- **Do not pile on.**
- **Delete** a cruel post and apologize.
- **Report** abuse.
