## The short version

Greed dressed up as investing. Paul warns that those who want to be rich fall into a snare (1 Timothy 6:9-10), and Proverbs says wealth gotten hastily will dwindle (Proverbs 13:11). The Catechism forbids "the desire to amass earthly goods without limit" (CCC 2536).

## What the Church teaches

"The tenth commandment forbids greed and the desire to amass earthly goods without limit. It forbids avarice arising from a passion for riches and their attendant power" (CCC 2536).

Paul says "they that will become rich, fall into temptation, and into the snare of the devil," and that the desire of money is the root of all evils (1 Timothy 6:9-10).

Proverbs says that wealth gotten in haste will be diminished, but that whoever gathers little by little will increase it (Proverbs 13:11).

The Catechism condemns fraud and "forcing up prices by taking advantage of the ignorance or hardship of another" (CCC 2409).

Games of chance and wagers can become "an enslavement" and unacceptable when they deprive someone of what they need (CCC 2413).

## Scripture

> "Substance got in haste shall be diminished: but that which by little and little is gathered with the hand shall increase."
*Proverbs 13:11 (Douay-Rheims)*

The proverb is old, practical advice. The Church says wealth should come from honest work.

## Why this matters today

The Church does not teach that any particular investment is a sin. Investing is allowed, and prudence is a virtue (CCC 1806).

Schemes that promise fast, large returns, recruit friends or play on fear of missing out are warning signs. Do not invest what you cannot afford to lose.

## What's at stake

The people who lose most in a scheme are the ones who can least afford it.

## How to live it

- **Ask who** profits if you buy.
- **Do not invest** money you need.
- **Do not recruit** friends into a scheme.
- **Talk to a trusted adult** before big decisions.
