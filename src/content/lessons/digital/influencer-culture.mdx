## The short version

Chasing fame can become a false god. Jesus tells his disciples not to do good deeds to be seen by men (Matthew 6:1-4), and the Catechism calls idolatry "divinizing what is not God" (CCC 2113).

## What the Church teaches

Jesus says, "Take heed that you do not your justice before men, to be seen by them" (Matthew 6:1), and tells his hearers to give alms in secret.

He asks the leaders how they can believe when they receive glory from one another and do not seek the glory that comes from God alone (John 5:44).

"Idolatry consists in divinizing what is not God. Man commits idolatry whenever he honors and reveres a creature in place of God, whether this be... power, pleasure, race, ancestors, the state, money" (CCC 2113).

"Boasting or bragging is an offense against truth" (CCC 2481).

Pope Leo XIV writes: "When business models thrive on human weakness, the person is treated as a means rather than as an end" (Magnifica Humanitas 170).

## Scripture

> "Take heed that you do not your justice before men, to be seen by them: otherwise you shall not have a reward of your Father who is in heaven. Therefore when thou dost an almsdeed, sound not a trumpet before thee, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, that they may be honoured by men. Amen I say to you, they have received their reward. But when thou dost alms, let not thy left hand know what thy right hand doth. That thy alms may be in secret, and thy Father who seeth in secret will repay thee."
*Matthew 6:1-4 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus is not forbidding public good deeds. He is warning about doing them for the audience.

## Why this matters today

Many young people say they want to be influencers. There is nothing wrong with talent or work. The danger is making attention your goal.

Followers and likes can be a drug. The question is whether your worth depends on them.

## What's at stake

If you live for applause, you will do whatever the crowd wants.

## How to live it

- **Do something good** and tell no one.
- **Hide your like counts** for a month.
- **Ask who you are performing for.**
- **Follow people** who make you better, not just busier.
