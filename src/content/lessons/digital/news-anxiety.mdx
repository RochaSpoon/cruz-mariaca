## The short version

You can be informed without losing peace. Paul says not to be anxious but to bring everything to God in prayer (Philippians 4:6-7), and Jesus says each day has trouble enough (Matthew 6:34). The Catechism asks for "moderation and discipline" with the media (CCC 2496).

## What the Church teaches

Paul says to have no anxiety about anything, but in every need to make your requests known to God with thanksgiving, and that the peace of God will guard your hearts (Philippians 4:6-7).

Jesus says not to be anxious about tomorrow, since each day has its own trouble (Matthew 6:34).

The Catechism says media users "should practice moderation and discipline" (CCC 2496), and that "society has a right to information based on truth" (CCC 2494).

Pope Leo XIV warns of "an incessant flow of information" that can replace "research, reflection and discernment" (Magnifica Humanitas 146).

The same text asks for "rhythms that incorporate silence, in-depth study, reading and judicious analysis" (Magnifica Humanitas 146).

## Scripture

> "Be nothing solicitous; but in every thing, by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your petitions be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasseth all understanding, keep your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus."
*Philippians 4:6-7 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Be not therefore solicitous for tomorrow; for the morrow will be solicitous for itself. Sufficient for the day is the evil thereof."
*Matthew 6:34 (Douay-Rheims)*

Neither verse tells you to ignore the world. They tell you not to be ruled by worry.

## Why this matters today

Constant alerts keep many people on edge. That is not the same as being informed.

If anxiety is taking over your life, a doctor or counselor can help. This lesson is not medical advice.

## What's at stake

Fear makes it harder to act well. Peace makes it easier.

## How to live it

- **Pick one or two times** a day to read the news.
- **Choose sources** that give facts.
- **Pray** for what you read.
- **Do one concrete thing** for someone near you.
