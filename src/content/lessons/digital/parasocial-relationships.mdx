## The short version

Parasocial attachments are one-sided bonds with streamers and celebrities. The Church says real persons are known "as a face not merely as a function" (Magnifica Humanitas 114), and Proverbs says to guard the heart (Proverbs 4:23).

## What the Church teaches

Pope Leo XIV writes: "The quality of a civilization is measured not by the power of its means, but by the care it is able to offer, by its ability to recognize the other as a face not merely as a function" (Magnifica Humanitas 114).

He gives examples of care learned in life: "Reading stories to a child, offering company to an elderly person and arranging a home so that it is welcoming" (Magnifica Humanitas 114).

The Catechism says "the human person needs to live in society. Society is not for him an extraneous addition but a requirement of his nature. Through the exchange with others, mutual service and dialogue with his brethren, man develops his potential" (CCC 1879).

Proverbs says to keep your heart with all diligence, because life issues out of it (Proverbs 4:23).

## Scripture

> "With all watchfulness keep thy heart, because life issueth out from it."
*Proverbs 4:23 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims reads "With all watchfulness keep thy heart, because life issueth out from it."

## Why this matters today

A streamer talks to you daily and knows nothing about you. That feels like friendship and is not mutual.

Enjoying a creator is fine. The concern is when it replaces real people, or when you spend money to feel close.

## What's at stake

Real friends know you and need you. A stream only needs your attention.

## How to live it

- **Reach out** to a real friend this week.
- **Notice** how much time and money you give to a stranger.
- **Keep it enjoyable** and keep it in proportion.
- **Pray** for the person you follow.
