## The short version

The Catechism says media users "should practice moderation and discipline," and Magnifica Humanitas warns that platforms are designed to capture "users' time and attention" (CCC 2496; Magnifica Humanitas 170).

## What the Church teaches

"The means of social communication (especially the mass media) can give rise to a certain passivity among users, making them less than vigilant consumers of what is said or shown. Users should practice moderation and discipline in their approach to the mass media" (CCC 2496).

Vatican II said the same: "Those who make use of the media of communications, especially the young, should take steps to accustom themselves to moderation and self-control in their regard" (Inter Mirifica 10).

Pope Leo XIV writes that "platforms and services are often designed to capture users' time and attention, exploiting their vulnerabilities and weakening their inner freedom" (Magnifica Humanitas 170).

He also says early and unsupervised exposure to digital devices and social media can affect "sleep, attention span, control of emotions and relationships" (Magnifica Humanitas 141).

Paul says that all things are lawful for him, but that he will not be brought under the power of any of them (1 Corinthians 6:12).

## Scripture

> "All things are lawful to me, but all things are not expedient. All things are lawful to me, but I will not be brought under the power of any."
*1 Corinthians 6:12 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul's rule is that nothing should master him. It is the Church's standard for any habit.

## Why this matters today

Many people check their phones without thinking. That is by design, as Pope Leo XIV says (Magnifica Humanitas 170).

This lesson makes no medical claims about addiction. If your use is out of control, a doctor or counselor can help.

## What's at stake

Your attention is what you give to God and to other people. If it is captured, it is not free to give.

## How to fight it

- **Turn off notifications** that are not from people.
- **Leave the phone outside the bedroom.**
- **Set a daily limit** and use the tool that enforces it.
- **Spend the first ten minutes** of the day without it.
