## The short version

The Church welcomes technology that serves people. Vatican II said the media can be "of great service to mankind" and help spread the Kingdom of God (Inter Mirifica 2; CCC 2493-2495).

## What the Church teaches

"The Church recognizes that these media, if properly utilized, can be of great service to mankind, since they greatly contribute to men's entertainment and instruction as well as to the spread and support of the Kingdom of God" (Inter Mirifica 2).

The Council says the Church "considers it one of its duties to announce the Good News of salvation also with the help of the media of social communication" (Inter Mirifica 3).

The Catechism says that the media "play a major role in information, cultural promotion, and formation" and "the information provided by the media is at the service of the common good" (CCC 2493-2494).

"Science and technology are precious resources when placed at the service of man and promote his integral development for the benefit of all" (CCC 2293).

"It is an illusion to claim moral neutrality in scientific research and its applications" (CCC 2294).

## Scripture

> "Going therefore, teach ye all nations; baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and behold I am with you all days, even to the consummation of the world."
*Matthew 28:19-20 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Church reads the great commission as including every means of communication available.

## Why this matters today

Good uses are everywhere: learning, prayer apps, Mass online for the sick, connecting with friends and family, and helping the poor.

The same tools can be used badly. The Church's standard is truth and charity (CCC 2495).

## What's at stake

Technology is neither good nor evil by itself. How you use it is the question.

## How to live it

- **Use it to learn** and to pray.
- **Share** good things.
- **Check** facts before sharing.
- **Use it to call** someone who is alone.
