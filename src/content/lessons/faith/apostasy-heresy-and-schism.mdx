## The short version

The Church distinguishes heresy (obstinately denying a truth that must be believed), apostasy (total repudiation of the faith) and schism (refusing communion with the Pope or the Church) (CCC 2089).

## What the Church teaches

"Incredulity is the neglect of revealed truth or the willful refusal to assent to it" (CCC 2089).

"Heresy is the obstinate post-baptismal denial of some truth which must be believed with divine and catholic faith, or it is likewise an obstinate doubt concerning the same; apostasy is the total repudiation of the Christian faith; schism is the refusal of submission to the Roman Pontiff or of communion with the members of the Church subject to him" (CCC 2089).

Doubt is different. "Involuntary doubt refers to hesitation in believing, difficulty in overcoming objections connected with the faith, or also anxiety aroused by its obscurity" (CCC 2088).

The Catechism teaches that "one cannot charge with the sin of the separation those who at present are born into these communities" that resulted from past separations (CCC 818).

## Scripture

> "But though we, or an angel from heaven, preach a gospel to you besides that which we have preached to you, let him be anathema. As we said before, so now I say again: If any one preach to you a gospel, besides that which you have received, let him be anathema."
*Galatians 1:8-9 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul warns the Galatians against another gospel. The Church uses this to say that the faith received from the apostles is not to be altered.

## Why this matters today

People use "heretic" as an insult. The Church defines it narrowly: an obstinate denial after baptism (CCC 2089).

Someone raised outside the Catholic Church is not a heretic in that sense. The Catechism says the sin of separation is not charged to them (CCC 818).

## What's at stake

The terms matter because they describe what a person actually did. Calling people names helps no one.

## How to live it

- **Use the words carefully.**
- **Ask what the person actually believes** before you label them.
- **If you are drifting,** talk to a priest or a friend you trust.
- **Remember the difference** between doubt and denial (CCC 2088).
