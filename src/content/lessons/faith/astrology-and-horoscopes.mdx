## The short version

The Catechism says "all forms of divination are to be rejected," and names horoscopes and astrology among them (CCC 2116).

## What the Church teaches

"All forms of divination are to be rejected: recourse to Satan or demons, conjuring up the dead or other practices falsely supposed to 'unveil' the future. Consulting horoscopes, astrology, palm reading, interpretation of omens and lots, the phenomena of clairvoyance, and recourse to mediums all conceal a desire for power over time, history, and, in the last analysis, other human beings" (CCC 2116).

"God can reveal the future to his prophets or to other saints. Still, a sound Christian attitude consists in putting oneself confidently into the hands of Providence for whatever concerns the future, and giving up all unhealthy curiosity about it" (CCC 2115).

The Catechism treats this under the first commandment, since it puts something else in God's place as the guide of your life (CCC 2112-2113).

## Scripture

> "Thou hast failed in the multitude or thy counsels: let now the astrologers stand and save thee, they that gazed at the stars, and counted the months, that from them they might tell the things that shall come to thee. Behold they are as stubble, fire hath burnt them, they shall not deliver themselves from the power of the flames: there are no coals wherewith they may be warmed, nor fire, that they may sit thereat."
*Isaiah 47:13-14 (Douay-Rheims)*

Isaiah mocks the astrologers of Babylon, saying they cannot even save themselves. The Church reads this as part of the Bible's steady refusal of divination.

## Why this matters today

Many people treat their sign as harmless fun. The Catechism takes it more seriously, because it aims at a "desire for power" over the future (CCC 2116).

Even those who say they do not believe in it check it. That habit trains you to look anywhere but at God for direction.

## What's at stake

If the stars decide, then you are not free and God is not Father. The Church holds that you are free and that the future is in God's hands (CCC 2115).

## How to live it

- **Delete the horoscope app.**
- **Ask what you are looking for** when you check it: reassurance, control, or direction.
- **Take that to prayer** and to a person who knows you.
- **Be kind to friends who like it.** Explain gently.
