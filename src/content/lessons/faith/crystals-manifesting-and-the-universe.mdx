## The short version

Crystals, manifesting and "the universe" are New Age practices. The Church's concern is that they put a force or a technique where God belongs, and she warns against magic (CCC 2111, 2113, 2117).

## What the Church teaches

The Catechism's test is who or what is trusted. Idolatry "consists in divinizing what is not God," and includes honoring "power, pleasure... money, etc." in place of him (CCC 2113).

It defines superstition as attributing "an importance in some way magical" to practices (CCC 2111).

And it condemns "all practices of magic or sorcery, by which one attempts to tame occult powers, so as to place them at one's service and have a supernatural power over others" (CCC 2117).

The Christian alternative is trust in Providence: "putting oneself confidently into the hands of Providence for whatever concerns the future" (CCC 2115).

Prayer is different from manifesting. In prayer you ask a person who loves you. The Catechism describes it as an encounter, not a technique (CCC 2560).

## Scripture

> "Be not solicitous therefore, saying, What shall we eat: or what shall we drink, or wherewith shall we be clothed? For after all these things do the heathens seek. For your Father knoweth that you have need of all these things. Seek ye therefore first the kingdom of God, and his justice, and all these things shall be added unto you."
*Matthew 6:31-33 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus tells his hearers not to be anxious about food and clothing, and to seek first the kingdom of God. The rest "shall be added."

## Why this matters today

Many people use crystals as décor or comfort and nothing more. The Church's concern starts when a stone or a ritual is expected to produce an effect.

Manifesting says that your thoughts and the universe will give you what you want. The Church says to ask God, and to accept that the answer may be no (CCC 2115).

## What's at stake

If the universe is a vending machine for your desires, you stay at the center. The Church says you are not.

## How to live it

- **Ask who you are asking** when you make a request.
- **Pray instead,** and tell God plainly what you want.
- **Keep gratitude,** and drop the rituals.
- **Explain gently** to friends who find peace in it.
