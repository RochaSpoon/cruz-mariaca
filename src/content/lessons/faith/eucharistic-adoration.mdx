## The short version

Eucharistic adoration is spending time in prayer before the Blessed Sacrament. The Church says she has always offered adoration to the Eucharist, "not only during Mass, but also outside of it" (CCC 1378).

## What the Church teaches

"The Catholic Church has always offered and still offers to the sacrament of the Eucharist the cult of adoration, not only during Mass, but also outside of it, reserving the consecrated hosts with the utmost care, exposing them to the solemn veneration of the faithful" (CCC 1378).

The tabernacle was first for taking the Eucharist to the sick. As faith in the real presence deepened, "the Church became conscious of the meaning of silent adoration of the Lord present under the Eucharistic species" (CCC 1379).

"It is highly fitting that Christ should have wanted to remain present to his Church in this unique way" (CCC 1380).

The Catechism quotes Cyril: "Do not doubt whether this is true, but rather receive the words of the Savior in faith, for since he is the truth, he cannot lie" (CCC 1381).

## Scripture

> "And he cometh to his disciples, and findeth them asleep, and he saith to Peter: What? Could you not watch one hour with me? Watch ye, and pray that ye enter not into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh weak."
*Matthew 26:40-41 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus asks Peter, "Could you not watch one hour with me?" Many Catholics read this as an invitation to the holy hour.

## Why this matters today

For many people it looks like staring at a piece of bread. Catholics believe that the Eucharist is Christ himself (CCC 1374), which is why it makes sense to sit with him.

Not every Christian tradition practices adoration this way. For Catholics it is a devotion, not a requirement.

## What's at stake

If the Eucharist is a symbol, adoration is odd. If it is Christ, the odd thing would be never visiting.

## How to live it

- **Go for ten minutes.** Sit and say nothing if you like.
- **Bring a short Gospel passage** to read.
- **Tell him what is on your mind.**
- **Stay when it is dull.**
