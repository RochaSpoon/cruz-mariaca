## The short version

Sundays are the primary holy day of obligation, and canon law lists others, including Christmas, Mary, Mother of God, and All Saints. Catholics are obliged to attend Mass on these days (can. 1246-1247).

## What the Church teaches

"Sunday, on which by apostolic tradition the paschal mystery is celebrated, must be observed in the universal Church as the primordial holy day of obligation" (can. 1246 §1).

The list also includes the Nativity of the Lord, the Epiphany, the Ascension, the Body and Blood of Christ, Holy Mary the Mother of God, her Immaculate Conception, her Assumption, Saint Joseph, Saints Peter and Paul, and All Saints (can. 1246 §1).

The conference of bishops, with the approval of the Apostolic See, can suppress some of these days or move them to a Sunday (can. 1246 §2). So the list varies from country to country.

"On Sundays and other holy days of obligation, the faithful are obliged to participate in the Mass" (can. 1247). The obligation can be fulfilled on the evening before (can. 1248; CCC 2180).

The Catechism says those excused for a serious reason, such as illness or care of infants, are not bound, and that "those who deliberately fail in this obligation commit a grave sin" (CCC 2181).

## Scripture

> "And let us consider one another, to provoke unto charity and to good works: Not forsaking our assembly, as some are accustomed; but comforting one another, and so much the more as you see the day approaching."
*Hebrews 10:24-25 (Douay-Rheims)*

Hebrews tells believers not to forsake their assembly. The Catechism quotes it for the Sunday gathering (CCC 2178).

## Why this matters today

Many Catholics are not sure which days apply where they live. Check your own country's list.

Some people see the obligation as a rule imposed from outside. The Catechism sees it as the minimum of a life of prayer (CCC 2041).

## What's at stake

If there is no obligation, the Mass depends on mood. The Church thinks a weekly gathering is worth protecting.

## How to live it

- **Look up your country's list** and put the dates in your calendar.
- **Go on the vigil** if the day is hard.
- **Plan around Mass** rather than fitting it in.
- **If you cannot go,** the Catechism says to pray at home (CCC 2183).
