## The short version

The Church asks Catholics to prepare for Mass and to show respect in clothing and posture. The Catechism says gestures and clothing ought to convey "the respect, solemnity, and joy of this moment" (CCC 1387).

## What the Church teaches

"To prepare for worthy reception of this sacrament, the faithful should observe the fast required in their Church. Bodily demeanor (gestures, clothing) ought to convey the respect, solemnity, and joy of this moment when Christ becomes our guest" (CCC 1387).

The General Instruction of the Roman Missal says the faithful stand from the beginning of the Entrance Chant until the end of the Collect, for the Gospel, during the Creed and the Universal Prayer, and from the invitation to pray over the offerings until the end of Mass, "except at the places indicated" (GIRM 43).

In the dioceses of the United States, "they should kneel beginning after the singing or recitation of the Sanctus (Holy, Holy, Holy) until after the Amen of the Eucharistic Prayer" (GIRM 43).

The faithful sit during the readings before the Gospel, the psalm, the homily and the preparation of the gifts (GIRM 43).

"Anyone aware of having sinned mortally must not receive communion without having received absolution" (CCC 1415).

## Scripture

> "Keep thy foot, when thou goest into the house of God, and draw nigh to hear. For much better is obedience, than the victims of fools, who know not what evil they do."
*Ecclesiastes 4:17 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims numbers this verse 4:17. Other translations number it 5:1. It tells the worshiper to guard his steps when going to the house of God.

## Why this matters today

Many people dress for church as they would for the couch. The Church does not give a dress code. The Catechism says clothing ought to show respect (CCC 1387).

Phones and chatter are a bigger problem than clothes. The Mass is the action of the whole assembly, and attention is part of taking part.

## What's at stake

How you act at Mass shows what you believe is happening there.

## How to live it

- **Arrive a few minutes early** and be quiet.
- **Silence your phone.**
- **Follow the posture of the congregation.**
- **Dress as if you are going to meet someone important.**
