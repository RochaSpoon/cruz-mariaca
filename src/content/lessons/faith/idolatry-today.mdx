## The short version

The Catechism says idolatry "consists in divinizing what is not God," whether power, pleasure, money or anything else (CCC 2113).

## What the Church teaches

"Idolatry not only refers to false pagan worship. It remains a constant temptation to faith. Idolatry consists in divinizing what is not God" (CCC 2113).

"Man commits idolatry whenever he honors and reveres a creature in place of God, whether this be gods or demons (for example, satanism), power, pleasure, race, ancestors, the state, money, etc." (CCC 2113).

Jesus says, "You cannot serve God and mammon" (CCC 2113).

"Human life finds its unity in the adoration of the one God." The commandment "integrates man and saves him from an endless disintegration" (CCC 2114).

The Catechism also says that "Many martyrs died for not adoring 'the Beast,'" refusing even to feign this worship (CCC 2113).

## Scripture

> "Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, lust, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is the service of idols."
*Colossians 3:5 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "No man can serve two masters. For either he will hate the one, and love the other: or he will sustain the one, and despise the other. You cannot serve God and mammon."
*Matthew 6:24 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says covetousness "is the service of idols." The Church takes this to mean that anything we crave above God takes his place.

## Why this matters today

Most idols now are good things made ultimate: money, a career, a relationship, a cause, a screen. The Catechism includes "power, pleasure... the state, money" (CCC 2113).

The commandment does not ban loving these things. It bans putting them in God's place.

## What's at stake

An idol promises what only God can give, and it cannot deliver. The Catechism says idolatry leads to disintegration, and the unity of a life comes from adoring one God (CCC 2114).

## How to live it

- **Ask what you would not give up** if God asked.
- **Fast from one thing** for a week and see what it does.
- **Give something away** that has a hold on you.
- **Pray the first line of the Creed** when you catch yourself serving something else.
