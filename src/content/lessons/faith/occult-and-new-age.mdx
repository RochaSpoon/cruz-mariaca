## The short version

Horoscopes, tarot, palm reading, Ouija boards, séances, "manifesting," spells, and channeling spirits all try to get knowledge or power from somewhere other than God. The Catechism calls them sins against the First Commandment. Some are grave. And Scripture is clear that real spiritual forces can be involved.

## What the Church teaches

"All forms of divination are to be rejected: recourse to Satan or demons, conjuring up the dead or other practices falsely supposed to 'unveil' the future. Consulting horoscopes, astrology, palm reading, interpretation of omens and lots, the phenomena of clairvoyance, and recourse to mediums all conceal a desire for power over time, history, and, in the last analysis, other human beings" (CCC 2116).

"All practices of magic or sorcery, by which one attempts to tame occult powers, so as to place them at one's service and have a supernatural power over others... are gravely contrary to the virtue of religion" (CCC 2117).

## Scripture

> "Neither let there be found among you any one... that consulteth soothsayers, or observeth dreams and omens, neither let there be any wizard, Nor charmer, nor any one that consulteth pythonic spirits, or fortune tellers, or that seeketh the truth from the dead. For the Lord abhorreth all these things."
*Deuteronomy 18:10-12 (Douay-Rheims)*

King Saul consulted a medium the night before he died (1 Samuel 28). St. Paul cast a spirit of divination out of a slave girl who told fortunes (Acts 16:16-18). In Ephesus, new Christians burned their books of magic, worth 50,000 pieces of silver (Acts 19:19).

## Why this hits hard today

"WitchTok" has billions of views. Tarot and astrology are marketed as self-care. Crystals, manifestation, and "the universe" stand in for God in a lot of wellness culture. Ouija boards are sold as toys. It all feels harmless and aesthetic. Exorcists consistently say that the occult is one of the most common ways people get into spiritual trouble.

## What's at stake

Deliberately seeking contact with spirits or using magic is grave matter. Even "for fun" or "just to see," you're inviting forces that are not God. Sorcerers are listed among those whose "portion" is "the pool burning with fire and brimstone" (Apocalypse 21:8).

## What to do

- **Get rid of it.** Tarot decks, Ouija boards, spell books. Destroy them, like the Ephesians did.
- **Confess it.** Name each practice.
- **Tell a priest** if you've had contact with spirits or have strange experiences since. Don't be embarrassed. Priests take it seriously.
- **Pray the St. Michael prayer** daily.
- **Use real sacramentals.** Holy water, a blessed crucifix, a blessed medal, the Rosary.
