## The short version

Sacrilege is profaning or treating unworthily the sacraments, liturgical actions, or persons, things and places consecrated to God (CCC 2120).

## What the Church teaches

"Sacrilege consists in profaning or treating unworthily the sacraments and other liturgical actions, as well as persons, things, or places consecrated to God" (CCC 2120).

"Sacrilege is a grave sin especially when committed against the Eucharist, for in this sacrament the true Body of Christ is made substantially present for us" (CCC 2120).

The Catechism lists it with tempting God and simony as the main sins of irreligion (CCC 2118).

Receiving communion while aware of mortal sin without absolution is one example the Church warns about (CCC 1415).

Paul says that whoever eats and drinks "unworthily" is guilty of the body and blood of the Lord (1 Corinthians 11:27).

## Scripture

> "Therefore whosoever shall eat this bread, or drink the chalice of the Lord unworthily, shall be guilty of the body and of the blood of the Lord. But let a man prove himself: and so let him eat of that bread, and drink of the chalice. For he that eateth and drinketh unworthily, eateth and drinketh judgment to himself, not discerning the body of the Lord."
*1 Corinthians 11:27-29 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Church takes this passage as the basis for her teaching on receiving communion with a clear conscience (CCC 1385).

## Why this matters today

Most of us will never desecrate a church. The more common issue is carelessness: treating the Eucharist as a snack, or going to communion without thinking.

The Church treats this as a reason to prepare and to be honest, not as a reason to stay away from the sacraments out of fear.

## What's at stake

If sacred things are ordinary, nothing is sacred. The Church asks for reverence because she believes Christ is really present.

## How to live it

- **Prepare for Communion** with a moment of prayer.
- **Go to Confession** before Communion if you are aware of serious sin (CCC 1415).
- **Treat churches and sacred objects with respect.**
- **Do not stay away from Mass out of fear.** Talk to a priest.
