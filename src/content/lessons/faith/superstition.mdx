## The short version

Superstition is "the deviation of religious feeling." It includes attributing a magical importance to religious practices, as if prayer worked by its mere performance (CCC 2111).

## What the Church teaches

"Superstition is the deviation of religious feeling and of the practices this feeling imposes. It can even affect the worship we offer the true God, e.g., when one attributes an importance in some way magical to certain practices otherwise lawful or necessary" (CCC 2111).

"To attribute the efficacy of prayers or of sacramental signs to their mere external performance, apart from the interior dispositions that they demand, is to fall into superstition" (CCC 2111).

The sacraments act by the power of Christ, but their fruits "depend on the disposition of the one who receives them" (CCC 1128).

The first commandment's list of irreligion includes tempting God, sacrilege and simony (CCC 2118).

## Scripture

> "And when you are praying, speak not much, as the heathens. For they think that in their much speaking they may be heard. Be not you therefore like to them, for your Father knoweth what is needful for you, before you ask him."
*Matthew 6:7-8 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus says the pagans think they will be heard for their many words, and tells his disciples not to be like them.

## Why this matters today

Many people carry a lucky charm, forward a prayer chain with a threat attached, or treat a medal as a guarantee. The Catechism treats the idea of magical efficacy as a deviation (CCC 2111).

Religious objects are not bad. Medals, statues and holy water are sacramentals, and the Church says they dispose us to grace. They do not work by themselves (CCC 1667).

## What's at stake

If a prayer is a spell, God is a machine. The Church says prayer is a relationship and the objects are signs (CCC 2111).

## How to live it

- **Ask what you expect** from a medal or a novena before you use it.
- **Do not send chain messages that threaten bad luck.**
- **Keep devotion tied to the Mass and the sacraments.**
- **Pray without a formula** at least once a day.
