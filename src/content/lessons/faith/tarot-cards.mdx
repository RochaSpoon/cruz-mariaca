## The short version

The Church treats tarot as divination, and the Catechism says all forms of divination are to be rejected (CCC 2116).

## What the Church teaches

"All forms of divination are to be rejected," including "interpretation of omens and lots" and "recourse to mediums." Such practices "conceal a desire for power over time, history, and, in the last analysis, other human beings" (CCC 2116).

Tarot is used to try to learn the future or the hidden state of someone's life. That puts it in the group of practices the Catechism lists.

The remedy the Church gives is trust: "putting oneself confidently into the hands of Providence for whatever concerns the future" (CCC 2115).

The Catechism also condemns magic: attempts "to tame occult powers, so as to place them at one's service" are "gravely contrary to the virtue of religion" (CCC 2117).

## Scripture

> "Neither let there be found among you any one that shall expiate his son or daughter, making them to pass through the fire: or that consulteth soothsayers, or observeth dreams and omens, neither let there be any wizard, Nor charmer, nor any one that consulteth pythonic spirits, or fortune tellers, or that seeketh the truth from the dead. For the Lord abhorreth all these things, and for these abominations he will destroy them at thy coming."
*Deuteronomy 18:10-12 (Douay-Rheims)*

Moses lists soothsayers, observers of dreams and omens, and those who consult "pythonic spirits" among the things Israel must not do.

## Why this matters today

Tarot is sold as self-reflection or art, and many people use it that way. The Catechism's concern is divination, and the intent matters.

That is the point to be honest about. If the cards are being used to learn the future or ask a spirit, the Church says no. If they are used as a prompt for thought, it is a different question.

## What's at stake

If you hand your decisions to a deck, you stop deciding. The Church says to ask God, and to ask people who know you.

## How to live it

- **Ask why you want a reading** before you take one.
- **Bring the question to prayer** and to a trusted person.
- **Do not dismiss friends who use the cards.** Talk about what they are looking for.
- **Get rid of decks** you have used for divination.
