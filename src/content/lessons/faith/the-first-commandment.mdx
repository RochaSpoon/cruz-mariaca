## The short version

The first commandment is "You shall not go after other gods." The Catechism says it embraces faith, hope and charity (CCC 2084-2086).

## What the Church teaches

God introduces the commandments by recalling what he has done: "I brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage." The first word "contains the first commandment of the Law": "You shall fear the LORD your God; you shall serve him... You shall not go after other gods" (CCC 2084).

"God's first call and just demand is that man accept him and worship him" (CCC 2084).

"The first commandment embraces faith, hope, and charity" (CCC 2086).

"Our moral life has its source in faith in God who reveals his love to us" (CCC 2087).

The commandment also requires us "to nourish and protect our faith with prudence and vigilance, and to reject everything that is opposed to it" (CCC 2088).

## Scripture

> "I am the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. Thou shalt not have strange gods before me. Thou shalt not make to thyself a graven thing, nor the likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or in the earth beneath, nor of those things that are in the waters under the earth. Thou shalt not adore them, nor serve them: I am the Lord thy God, mighty, jealous, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me: And shewing mercy unto thousands to them that love me, and keep my commandments."
*Exodus 20:2-6 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jews, Protestants and Catholics count the commandments slightly differently, but all of them put this one first in substance: God alone is to be worshiped.

## Why this matters today

Few people bow to carved idols now. The Catechism says idolatry is "a constant temptation to faith" (CCC 2113). The next lessons look at the forms it takes.

Some people think the first commandment is just a rule about religion. The Catechism says the whole moral life flows from it (CCC 2087).

## What's at stake

If something else comes first in your life, God is second whatever you say. The commandment is about what actually rules you.

## How to fight it

- **Ask what you really put first.** Look at your time and your money.
- **Start the day with God** before the phone.
- **Learn the Creed.** It answers who God is.
- **Read Exodus 20** slowly.
