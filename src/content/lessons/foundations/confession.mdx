## The short version

Jesus gave his apostles the power to forgive sins. That power passed to bishops and priests. Confession is how mortal sins committed after Baptism are ordinarily forgiven. It is also where you get the grace to stop.

## What the Church teaches

After the Resurrection, Jesus breathed on the apostles and said:

> "Receive ye the Holy Ghost. Whose sins you shall forgive, they are forgiven them; and whose sins you shall retain, they are retained."
*John 20:22-23 (Douay-Rheims)*

A priest cannot retain or forgive what he never hears. So Christ built in confession out loud. The Council of Trent taught that all mortal sins must be confessed by kind and number (CCC 1456). Catholics must confess serious sins at least once a year (CCC 1457; can. 989).

What the priest hears is protected by the seal. A priest may never reveal it, for any reason, under penalty of automatic excommunication (can. 983, 1388). Priests have died rather than break it, like St. John Nepomucene.

## Why it matters

People say, "I confess straight to God." You should, every night. But Jesus chose a human channel on purpose. You hear the words "I absolve you" out loud. You know you're forgiven, instead of guessing. And humility kills the pride that hides sin.

## How to go (step by step)

1. **Examine your conscience.** Go through the Ten Commandments. Write it down if it helps. Many parishes and apps have examination guides.
2. **Go in.** Behind a screen or face to face, your choice.
3. **Start:** "Bless me, Father, for I have sinned. It has been [time] since my last confession." If it's your first time or it's been years, say so. Priests are glad to help.
4. **Confess.** Say your sins plainly, mortal sins with kind and roughly how many times. Do not explain them away, and do not tell long stories.
5. **Listen.** The priest may give advice. He will give a penance.
6. **Act of Contrition.** A simple version: "My God, I am sorry for my sins with all my heart. In choosing to do wrong and failing to do good, I have sinned against you whom I should love above all things. I firmly intend, with your help, to do penance, to sin no more, and to avoid whatever leads me to sin. Our Savior Jesus Christ suffered and died for us. In his name, my God, have mercy."
7. **Absolution.** The priest forgives you in Christ's name.
8. **Do your penance** right away if you can.

## What's at stake

Deliberately hiding a mortal sin in Confession makes the confession invalid and adds the sin of sacrilege. Avoiding Confession for years while living in grave sin is gambling with your soul. You do not know when you will die.

## If you're afraid

Every priest has heard it all. Your sin is not special to him. What is rare is courage, and he respects it. Jesus' parable of the prodigal son (Luke 15) shows the father running to meet the son before the son even finishes his speech.
