## The short version

The Catechism says sin repeats and reinforces itself, and that grace, the sacraments and effort can break a habit. Augustine described his own chains in his Confessions.

## What the Church teaches

"Sin creates a proclivity to sin; it engenders vice by repetition of the same acts. This results in perverse inclinations which cloud conscience and corrupt the concrete judgment of good and evil. Thus sin tends to reproduce itself and reinforce itself, but it cannot destroy the moral sense at its root" (CCC 1865).

Augustine wrote of his own struggle: "Of a perverse will was lust made; and lust indulged in became custom; and custom not resisted became necessity" (Confessions VIII.5.10).

Paul says sin must not reign in the mortal body, and promises that sin will not have dominion over those who are under grace and not under the law (Romans 6:12-14).

The Catechism says that that human virtues acquired by education and repeated effort are purified and raised up by divine grace (CCC 1810). A good habit can replace a bad one.

Confession "helps us form our conscience, fight against evil tendencies, let ourselves be healed by Christ" (CCC 1458).

## Scripture

> "Let no sin therefore reign in your mortal body, so as to obey the lusts thereof. Neither yield ye your members as instruments of iniquity unto sin; but present yourselves to God, as those that are alive from the dead, and your members as instruments of justice unto God. For sin shall not have dominion over you; for you are not under the law, but under grace."
*Romans 6:12-14 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says sin will not have dominion over you because you are under grace, not law.

## Why this matters today

Many habits, such as porn, drink, rage and screens, run deep. Some also involve addiction, which is not only a moral matter. Seek medical and counseling help where it applies.

The Church's contribution is not the whole answer, and does not replace help from professionals. It is a way of seeing that you can change, and that you are not alone.

## What's at stake

If the chain is permanent, there is no point in fighting. The Church says it is breakable, and grace is real.

## How to live it

- **Go to Confession** regularly, and tell the truth about the pattern.
- **Tell one person** you trust.
- **Change your surroundings** so that the habit is harder.
- **See a doctor or counselor** if addiction is involved.
