## The short version

Remember you must die is an old Christian habit. The Catechism says remembering our mortality "lends urgency to our lives" and helps us bring them to fulfillment (CCC 1007).

## What the Church teaches

"Death is the end of earthly life. Our lives are measured by time, in the course of which we change, grow old and, as with all living beings on earth, death seems like the normal end of life. That aspect of death lends urgency to our lives: remembering our mortality helps us realize that we have only a limited time in which to bring our lives to fulfillment" (CCC 1007).

For Christians, "death is transformed by Christ." His obedience "has transformed the curse of death into a blessing" (CCC 1009).

Paul says, "For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain" (CCC 1010).

"Death is the end of man's earthly pilgrimage." We will not return to other earthly lives; "it is appointed for men to die once" (CCC 1013).

"The Church encourages us to prepare ourselves for the hour of our death." In the Hail Mary we ask Mary to pray for us "at the hour of our death" (CCC 1014).

## Scripture

But God said to him: Thou fool, this night do they require thy soul of thee: and whose shall those things be which thou hast provided? So is he that layeth up treasure for himself, and is not rich towards God."
*Luke 12:19-21 (Douay-Rheims)*

The first verse is Psalm 89 in the Douay-Rheims. The second is the parable of the rich fool, who plans for years he does not have.

## Why this matters today

People spend a great deal of energy ignoring death. The Church's approach is to look at it, and then live better.

Some traditions, including some Eastern ones, speak of rebirth after death. The Catechism says there is no reincarnation (CCC 1013).

## What's at stake

If you never think of your death, you will put off what matters. The Church says time is the gift you have.

## How to live it

- **Ask what you would do** if you knew you had a year.
- **Do one thing on that list** this month.
- **Pray the Hail Mary** with the last line in mind.
- **Write down** what you want people to know.
