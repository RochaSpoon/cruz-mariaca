## The short version

Scrupulosity is obsessive fear of sin. It is a real burden, and the Church's answer is to trust God and a confessor and not to judge every thought as sin. If it feels like illness, a doctor can help as well as a priest.

## What the Church teaches

The Catechism says conscience "is a judgment of reason" (CCC 1778). It is not a feeling of dread.

Mortal sin requires "grave matter," "full knowledge" and "deliberate consent" (CCC 1857). A passing thought you did not choose does not meet those conditions.

Venial sin does not break the covenant with God. It "is humanly reparable" (CCC 1863).

Even when ignorance or error is involved, the Catechism says the moral subject is not blamed if the ignorance is "invincible" (CCC 1793).

The Catechism says "the education of the conscience guarantees freedom and engenders peace of heart" (CCC 1784).

The Church also asks us to be guided by others: the faithful are "aided by the witness or advice of others" (CCC 1785).

## Scripture

> "Come to me, all you that labour, and are burdened, and I will refresh you. Take up my yoke upon you, and learn of me, because I am meek, and humble of heart: and you shall find rest to your souls. For my yoke is sweet and my burden light."
*Matthew 11:28-30 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus says his yoke is sweet and his burden light. The Church takes this as a sign that a life of faith is not meant to be lived in constant terror.

## Why this matters today

Scrupulosity can look like holiness from the outside, and can feel to the person like the only honest response to sin. It often comes with anxiety that is not about God.

If your fear is out of proportion or will not stop, tell a priest and also a doctor or counselor. This lesson is not medical advice.

## What's at stake

A conscience driven by fear cannot hear God. The Church's goal is peace of heart (CCC 1784).

## How to live it

- **Tell one confessor** the whole thing, and follow his guidance.
- **Do not repeat confessions** of the same thing.
- **See a doctor** if the fear controls your day.
- **Pray the psalms** that speak of trust.
