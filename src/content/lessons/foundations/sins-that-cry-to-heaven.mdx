## The short version

The Church's catechetical tradition lists four "sins that cry to heaven": murder (the blood of Abel), the sin of the Sodomites, oppression of the poor and the foreigner, and defrauding the worker of his wages (CCC 1867).

## What the Church teaches

"The catechetical tradition also recalls that there are 'sins that cry to heaven.' The blood of Abel; the sin of the Sodomites; the cry of the people oppressed in Egypt; the cry of the foreigner, the widow, and the orphan; injustice to the wage earner" (CCC 1867).

The first is Cain's murder of Abel, where God says "the voice of thy brother's blood crieth to me from the earth" (Genesis 4:10, Douay-Rheims).

God tells Moses, "I have seen the affliction of my people in Egypt, and I have heard their cry" (Exodus 3:7, Douay-Rheims).

James says the wages kept back by fraud from the laborers cry out, and that their cry has reached the ears of the Lord of hosts (James 5:4).

The common thread is that these sins harm people who cannot defend themselves, and God hears them.

## Scripture

> "And he said to him: What hast thou done? the voice of thy brother's blood crieth to me from the earth."
*Genesis 4:10 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Behold the hire of the labourers, who have reaped down your fields, which by fraud has been kept back by you, crieth: and the cry of them hath entered into the ears of the Lord of Sabaoth."
*James 5:4 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Catechism lists the sin of the Sodomites with the others. The Bible texts for the other three are quoted here.

## Why this matters today

Wage theft, trafficking, and the abuse of migrants and the vulnerable are current forms. The Church treats them as grave because the victims are defenseless.

The list also includes the sin of the Sodomites, which the Catechism treats elsewhere, in the context of chastity (CCC 2357).

## What's at stake

If no one hears the poor, then no one acts. The Church says God does.

## How to live it

- **Pay fair wages** if you hire anyone.
- **Welcome the stranger.**
- **Speak up** when the powerless are harmed.
- **Pray the psalms** of the poor.
