## The short version

The Catechism says "prayer is a battle," against ourselves and "against the wiles of the tempter" (CCC 2725). The Church's weapons are prayer, the sacraments and fasting.

## What the Church teaches

"Prayer is both a gift of grace and a determined response on our part. It always presupposes effort. The great figures of prayer... all teach us this: prayer is a battle. Against whom? Against ourselves and against the wiles of the tempter who does all he can to turn man away from prayer" (CCC 2725).

"Such a battle and such a victory become possible only through prayer. It is by his prayer that Jesus vanquishes the tempter" (CCC 2849).

Paul tells the Ephesians to put on "the armor of God" to stand against the devil's schemes (Ephesians 6:11).

The devil's power is "not infinite" (CCC 395). "The reason the Son of God appeared was to destroy the works of the devil" (CCC 394).

The sacraments, especially the Eucharist and Penance, are how grace comes to us (CCC 1999).

## Scripture

> "Finally, brethren, be strengthened in the Lord, and in the might of his power. Put you on the armour of God, that you may be able to stand against the deceits of the devil. For our wrestling is not against flesh and blood; but against principalities and powers, against the rulers of the world of this darkness, against the spirits of wickedness in the high places. Therefore take unto you the armour of God, that you may be able to resist in the evil day, and to stand in all things perfect."
*Ephesians 6:10-13 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says "our wrestling is not against flesh and blood," and tells believers to take up the armor of God that they may resist.

## Why this matters today

Spiritual warfare can become a sensational subject. The Church's teaching is plain: pray, use the sacraments, keep watch, and do not seek the dramatic.

If you feel under attack in a way that scares you, talk to a priest, and also to a doctor if there is any possibility of illness.

## What's at stake

If there is no battle, you can drift. If the whole of life is battle, you will wear out. The Church's advice is to keep watch and stay close to Christ.

## How to live it

- **Pray daily,** even briefly.
- **Go to Mass and Confession.**
- **Fast occasionally.**
- **Do not go looking** for demons.
