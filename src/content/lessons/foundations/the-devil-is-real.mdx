## The short version

The Church teaches that Satan is a real fallen angel, made good by God, whose power is real but not infinite (CCC 391-395).

## What the Church teaches

"Scripture and the Church's Tradition see in this being a fallen angel, called 'Satan' or the 'devil.' The Church teaches that Satan was at first a good angel, made by God" (CCC 391).

The fall "consists in the free choice of these created spirits, who radically and irrevocably rejected God and his reign" (CCC 392).

"It is the irrevocable character of their choice, and not a defect in the infinite divine mercy, that makes the angels' sin unforgivable" (CCC 393).

Jesus calls him "a murderer from the beginning." "The reason the Son of God appeared was to destroy the works of the devil" (CCC 394).

"The power of Satan is, nonetheless, not infinite. He is only a creature, powerful from the fact that he is pure spirit, but still a creature. He cannot prevent the building up of God's reign" (CCC 395).

## Scripture

> "Be sober and watch: because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, goeth about seeking whom he may devour. Whom resist ye, strong in faith: knowing that the same affliction befalls your brethren who are in the world."
*1 Peter 5:8-9 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Put you on the armour of God, that you may be able to stand against the deceits of the devil. For our wrestling is not against flesh and blood; but against principalities and powers, against the rulers of the world of this darkness, against the spirits of wickedness in the high places."
*Ephesians 6:11-12 (Douay-Rheims)*

Peter says the devil goes about like a roaring lion, and tells believers to resist him, "strong in faith." Paul says our wrestling is against spiritual forces.

## Why this matters today

Many people treat the devil as a symbol. The Church says he is a person, a fallen angel (CCC 391).

Others see the devil behind everything. The Catechism says his power is limited, and he cannot stop God's reign (CCC 395).

## What's at stake

If the devil is not real, temptation is only psychology. If he is everywhere, you will live in fear. The Church says he is real, and defeated.

## How to live it

- **Take temptation seriously** and pray.
- **Do not play with the occult** (CCC 2116-2117).
- **Use the sacraments.**
- **Do not panic.** The Catechism says his power is limited (CCC 395).
