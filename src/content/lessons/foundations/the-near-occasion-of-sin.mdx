## The short version

Avoiding the people, places and things that reliably lead you into sin is part of real repentance. Jesus' hard saying about the eye and the hand makes the same point (Matthew 5:29-30).

## What the Church teaches

Contrition includes "the resolution not to sin again" (CCC 1451). A resolution that keeps going back to the same occasions is not a firm one.

Jesus says that if your right eye is an occasion of sin you should pluck it out, because it is better to lose one part than for the whole body to be lost (Matthew 5:29-30).

The Catechism says to "discern between being tempted and consenting to temptation," and notes that "Lead us not into temptation" implies "a decision of the heart" (CCC 2847-2848).

It also says sin creates a "proclivity to sin," and repetition produces vice (CCC 1865).

Prayer and vigilance are the means. The Catechism says Christ "urges us to vigilance of the heart" (CCC 2849).

## Scripture

> "And if thy right eye scandalize thee, pluck it out and cast it from thee. For it is expedient for thee that one of thy members should perish, rather than that thy whole body be cast into hell. And if thy right hand scandalize thee, cut it off, and cast it from thee: for it is expedient for thee that one of thy members should perish, rather than that thy whole body be cast into hell."
*Matthew 5:29-30 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus is speaking in strong terms. The Church reads this as a call to take radical steps against what leads to sin, not as a call to self-harm.

## Why this matters today

Many people say they cannot avoid a place or a person. Sometimes that is true. Often it is a way of staying close to what we say we are fighting.

Phones are the near occasion for many people now. Distance and rules help.

## What's at stake

Willpower fails in the presence of the thing you are trying to resist. The Church's advice is to stay out of reach.

## How to live it

- **Name your triggers** in confession.
- **Change one thing** in your environment this week.
- **Tell someone** who can hold you to it.
- **Pray** when you cannot avoid it.
