## The short version

Venial sin does not break your friendship with God, but it weakens charity and, if deliberate and unrepented, "disposes us little by little to commit mortal sin" (CCC 1863).

## What the Church teaches

"One commits venial sin when, in a less serious matter, he does not observe the standard prescribed by the moral law, or when he disobeys the moral law in a grave matter, but without full knowledge or without complete consent" (CCC 1862).

"Venial sin weakens charity; it manifests a disordered affection for created goods; it impedes the soul's progress in the exercise of the virtues and the practice of the moral good; it merits temporal punishment" (CCC 1863).

"Deliberate and unrepented venial sin disposes us little by little to commit mortal sin" (CCC 1863).

Still, venial sin "does not set us in direct opposition to the will and friendship of God; it does not break the covenant with God." It does not deprive the sinner of sanctifying grace (CCC 1863).

Confession of everyday faults is, "without being strictly necessary," nevertheless "strongly recommended by the Church" (CCC 1458).

## Scripture

> "A workman that is a drunkard shall not be rich: and he that contemneth small things, shall fall by little and little."
*Ecclesiasticus 19:1 (Douay-Rheims)*

Ecclesiasticus is the Douay-Rheims name for Sirach. The verse says that whoever despises small things will fall by little and little.

## Why this matters today

Many people ask what they can get away with. The Church does not hand out permissions. She says small sins make large ones easier.

Others become anxious over every small fault. The Church says venial sin does not break your friendship with God (CCC 1863).

## What's at stake

Small sins feel harmless because they are small. The Church says the direction they take you is what matters.

## How to live it

- **Pick one small habit** to drop.
- **Confess venial sins** regularly (CCC 1458).
- **Do not turn it into scruples.** See the lesson on scrupulosity.
- **Ask where small compromises are heading.**
