## The short version

This is the hardest case. The Church teaches that the child is innocent, and that rape is "gravely against justice and charity" (CCC 2270, 2356). The woman deserves care without blame.

## What the Church teaches

"Rape is the forcible violation of the sexual intimacy of another person. It does injury to justice and charity. Rape deeply wounds the respect, freedom, and physical and moral integrity to which every person has a right. It causes grave damage that can mark the victim for life. It is always an intrinsically evil act" (CCC 2356).

"Human life must be respected and protected absolutely from the moment of conception" (CCC 2270), and "direct abortion... is gravely contrary to the moral law" (CCC 2271).

The Church says the child, though conceived in violence, is an innocent human being (CCC 2261).

The Church does not tell the woman she has done wrong by being the victim. She asks for care and support for both mother and child.

Evangelium Vitae 99 speaks to women who are in this position as well as to those who have had abortions: the Church knows "the many factors" and does not doubt that decisions can be "painful and even shattering."

## Why this matters today

This case is often used to challenge the Church's whole teaching. The Church does not avoid it.

Adoption, practical support and counseling are real options. The Church asks the community to provide them.

## What's at stake

Two people were harmed. The Church says neither should be treated as disposable.

## How to live it

- **Believe** survivors and help them get care.
- **Support** pregnancy centers and agencies that help with money, housing and counseling.
- **Do not use** this case to score points.
- **Pray** for the mother and the child.
