## The short version

Abortion is the deliberate killing of a human being in the womb. Human life begins at conception. That's biology, not religion. The Church teaches that direct abortion is always gravely wrong, whatever the reason. Christians have taught this since the first century, when Rome accepted abortion and infanticide.

## What the Church teaches

"Human life must be respected and protected absolutely from the moment of conception" (CCC 2270). "Since the first century the Church has affirmed the moral evil of every procured abortion. This teaching has not changed and remains unchangeable" (CCC 2271).

Pope John Paul II solemnly declared that "direct abortion, that is, abortion willed as an end or as a means, always constitutes a grave moral disorder, since it is the deliberate killing of an innocent human being" (*Evangelium Vitae* 62).

The Second Vatican Council called abortion and infanticide "unspeakable crimes" (*Gaudium et Spes* 51).

Anyone who procures a completed abortion incurs automatic excommunication (can. 1398; CCC 2272). That applies to the mother, and also to those who directly and necessarily cooperate. The penalty doesn't apply if the person was under 16, acted out of grave fear, or didn't know about the penalty. And it can be lifted. In the U.S. and many countries, any priest can now absolve it in Confession.

## Answering common arguments

- **"It's just a clump of cells."** You were a clump of cells too. A human embryo is a distinct, living, whole human organism with its own DNA. Size and development don't decide who counts.
- **"My body, my choice."** The child has a different body, a different blood type, often a different sex. The mother's body is involved, but it isn't the only body.
- **"What about rape?"** Rape is a horrific crime and the woman deserves every support. But the child didn't commit it. Killing the child doesn't undo the assault, and the child shouldn't get the death penalty for the father's crime.
- **"What about the mother's life?"** The Church allows treatment necessary to save the mother's life even if the child dies as an unintended side effect (for example, removing a cancerous uterus, or treating an ectopic pregnancy by removing the damaged tube). What's forbidden is directly killing the child as the means.

## Why this hits hard today

Since *Dobbs* (2022) overturned *Roe v. Wade*, abortion law in the U.S. is decided state by state. In California, abortion is legal and protected in the state constitution. Medication abortion (the abortion pill) made up about 63% of abortions in the U.S. health care system in 2023, according to the Guttmacher Institute. California's Proposition 1, approved in November 2022, added a right to reproductive freedom to the state constitution. Many women who have abortions say they felt they had no other choice. That's a failure of all of us.

## What's at stake

Abortion takes an innocent life. It's grave matter, and it carries excommunication. Those who promote it, pay for it, or pressure someone into it share in the guilt. That includes boyfriends and parents who push.

There is also hope. John Paul II wrote to women who have had abortions: what happened was terribly wrong, but don't give in to discouragement and don't lose hope. The Father of mercies is waiting to give you forgiveness and peace in the Sacrament of Reconciliation (*Evangelium Vitae* 99, paraphrased).

## What to do

- **If you've been involved in an abortion,** go to Confession. Project Rachel and Rachel's Vineyard offer confidential healing for mothers, fathers, and families.
- **If you or someone you know is pregnant and scared,** pregnancy resource centers and Walking with Moms in Need (a USCCB initiative) can help with housing, money, medical care, and support.
- **Be pro-life all the way.** Help mothers. Support adoption. Show up.
- **Learn the arguments,** and make them with charity.
