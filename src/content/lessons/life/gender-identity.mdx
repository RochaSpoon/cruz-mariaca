## The short version

The Church teaches that you are a unity of body and soul. Your body is not a costume over the "real you." It is you. Being male or female is part of how God made you, and it's a gift to receive, not a problem to fix. People who experience gender dysphoria deserve real compassion and care. Changing the body to reject its sex is not the answer.

## What the Church teaches

"Everyone, man and woman, should acknowledge and accept his sexual identity" (CCC 2333). The human body shares in the dignity of the image of God, and the soul and body together form a single nature (CCC 364-365). Apart from strictly therapeutic medical reasons, directly intended amputations, mutilations, and sterilizations of innocent persons are against the moral law (CCC 2297).

*Dignitas Infinita* (2024) says that every person, regardless of sexual orientation, "ought to be respected in his or her dignity and treated with consideration" (no. 55). It says that desiring a personal self-determination apart from the truth that human life is a gift "amounts to a concession to the age-old temptation to make oneself God" (no. 57). And it says that any sex-change intervention, as a rule, "risks threatening the unique dignity the person has received from the moment of conception," while not excluding care for genital abnormalities evident at birth or that develop later (no. 60).

## Why this hits hard today

In a few years, gender identity went from fringe theory to school curriculum. The number of teens identifying as transgender has risen sharply. Several European countries (including the UK, Sweden, and Finland) have pulled back on puberty blockers for minors after medical reviews found weak evidence for them. Meanwhile many kids who question their gender are struggling with real pain, anxiety, and depression.

## What's at stake

At the deepest level: whether God made you, or you make yourself. That's the oldest temptation in the book (Genesis 3:5). Practically: irreversible medical procedures on young bodies, and kids who are hurting not getting the care they need.

## What to do

- **If you're struggling with your sense of self:** Talk to a priest and to your parents, and find a counselor who respects your faith. You are not a mistake.
- **Treat everyone with charity.** Never mock or bully. Christ doesn't.
- **Learn the arguments,** especially the medical evidence from Europe.
