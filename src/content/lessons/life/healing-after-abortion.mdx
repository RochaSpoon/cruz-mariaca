## The short version

The Church says to those who have had an abortion: "do not give in to discouragement and do not lose hope." Forgiveness and peace are available in the Sacrament of Reconciliation (Evangelium Vitae 99).

## What the Church teaches

John Paul II writes: "I would now like to say a special word to women who have had an abortion. The Church is aware of the many factors which may have influenced your decision, and she does not doubt that in many cases it was a painful and even shattering decision" (Evangelium Vitae 99).

"The wound in your heart may not yet have healed. Certainly what happened was and remains terribly wrong. But do not give in to discouragement and do not lose hope. Try rather to understand what happened and face it honestly" (Evangelium Vitae 99).

"The Father of mercies is ready to give you his forgiveness and his peace in the Sacrament of Reconciliation. To the same Father and his mercy you can with sure hope entrust your child" (Evangelium Vitae 99).

The Catechism says the Church "attaches the canonical penalty of excommunication to this crime against human life" (CCC 2272). A person who is unsure of their situation should speak to a priest, who can help.

Isaiah says though sins be as scarlet they shall be made white as snow (Isaiah 1:18).

## Scripture

> "And then come, and accuse me, saith the Lord: if your sins be as scarlet, they shall be made as white as snow: and if they be red as crimson, they shall be white as wool."
*Isaiah 1:18 (Douay-Rheims)*

The promise is for anyone who comes back.

## Why this matters today

Fathers, grandparents and medical workers may also carry pain. The Church's call to mercy is for them too.

Grief and guilt can be heavy. A counselor can help alongside a priest. This lesson is not therapy.

## What's at stake

Shame keeps people away. Mercy brings them back.

## How to live it

- **Go to** Confession, and ask for a priest you trust.
- **Find** a counselor or a support group.
- **Name** your child, and pray for him or her.
- **Tell** one person.
