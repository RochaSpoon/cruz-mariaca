## The short version

The Church says IVF separates conception from the marital act and usually involves the destruction or freezing of embryos. She asks for compassion for couples who suffer from infertility (CCC 2376-2377; Dignitas Personae 14-19).

## What the Church teaches

"Techniques involving only the married couple (homologous artificial insemination and fertilization) are perhaps less reprehensible, yet remain morally unacceptable. They dissociate the sexual act from the procreative act" (CCC 2377).

"A child is not something owed to one, but is a gift... In this area, only the child possesses genuine rights" (CCC 2378).

Dignitas Personae says "it is ethically unacceptable to dissociate procreation from the integrally personal context of the conjugal act" (Dignitas Personae 16).

It notes that IVF "very frequently involves the deliberate destruction of embryos" (Dignitas Personae 14), and it takes up the many frozen embryos already in existence (Dignitas Personae 19).

John Paul II notes that these techniques "have a high rate of failure," and the embryo is "exposed to the risk of death" (Evangelium Vitae 14).

The Church does not blame couples. The Catechism says "Couples who discover that they are sterile suffer greatly" (CCC 2374).

## Scripture

> "And Rachel, seeing herself without children, envied her sister, and said to her husband: Give me children, otherwise I shall die. And Jacob being angry with her, answered: Am I as God, who hath deprived thee of the fruit of thy womb?"
*Genesis 30:1-2 (Douay-Rheims)*

Rachel's cry for children is one of the Bible's oldest accounts of infertility.

## Why this matters today

IVF has given many families children, and the Church says each of these children is a gift and a person with full dignity (CCC 2378).

The Church's objection is to the method and to what happens to embryos, not to the children or their parents.

## What's at stake

The wish for a child is good. The Church says the means matter, and that no embryo is disposable.

## How to live it

- **Learn** the reasons before you decide.
- **Ask** clinics what happens to extra embryos.
- **Look** at the options in the next lesson.
- **Be kind** to couples who choose differently.
