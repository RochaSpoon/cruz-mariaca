## The short version

The Church allows "lawful self-defense" when "all peace efforts have failed," under strict conditions. The Catechism lists four conditions at once (CCC 2308-2309).

## What the Church teaches

"All citizens and all governments are obliged to work for the avoidance of war. However, 'as long as the danger of war persists and there is no international authority with the necessary competence and power, governments cannot be denied the right of lawful self-defense, once all peace efforts have failed'" (CCC 2308).

The conditions for legitimate defense by military force "require rigorous consideration." At the same time: the damage must be "lasting, grave, and certain"; all other means must have proved impractical or ineffective; there must be serious prospects of success; and the use of arms must not produce evils greater than the evil to be eliminated (CCC 2309).

"The mere fact that war has regrettably broken out does not mean that everything becomes licit between the warring parties" (CCC 2312).

"Non-combatants, wounded soldiers, and prisoners must be respected and treated humanely" (CCC 2313). "Blind obedience does not suffice to excuse those who carry them out," speaking of actions deliberately contrary to the law of nations (CCC 2313).

"Every act of war directed to the indiscriminate destruction of whole cities or vast areas with their inhabitants is a crime against God and man" (CCC 2314).

## Scripture

> "For he is God's minister to thee, for good. But if thou do that which is evil, fear: for he beareth not the sword in vain. For he is God's minister: an avenger to execute wrath upon him that doth evil."
*Romans 13:4 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says the ruler does not bear the sword in vain. The Church reads this together with the limits above.

## Why this matters today

Some Christians are pacifists, and the Church respects those who refuse to bear arms for reasons of conscience (CCC 2311). Others serve with honor (CCC 2310).

The theory is a set of tests. Many modern wars fail them.

## What's at stake

War is among the gravest human acts. The Church asks for strict conditions so it is never light.

## How to live it

- **Learn** the four conditions.
- **Pray** for peace and for soldiers.
- **Question** the case for war.
- **Honor** conscience.
