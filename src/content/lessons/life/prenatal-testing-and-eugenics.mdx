## The short version

Testing to prepare is fine. John Paul II says prenatal diagnosis "presents no moral objections" if it identifies treatment, but becomes "eugenic abortion" when it is used to eliminate children with a disability (Evangelium Vitae 14).

## What the Church teaches

"Prenatal diagnosis, which presents no moral objections if carried out in order to identify the medical treatment which may be needed by the child in the womb, all too often becomes an opportunity for proposing and procuring an abortion" (Evangelium Vitae 14).

"This is eugenic abortion, justified in public opinion on the basis of a mentality... which accepts life only under certain conditions and rejects it when it is affected by any limitation, handicap or illness" (Evangelium Vitae 14).

Dignitas Personae says that pre-implantation diagnosis "is directed toward the qualitative selection and consequent destruction of embryos, which constitutes an act of abortion" (Dignitas Personae 22).

Every human being has the same dignity, whatever their abilities (CCC 1700, 1935).

The Church supports parents who receive a hard diagnosis, with practical and spiritual help (CCC 2208).

## Scripture

> "For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast protected me from my mother's womb. I will praise thee, for thou art fearfully magnified: wonderful are thy works, and my soul knoweth right well. My bone is not hidden from thee, which thou hast made in secret: and my substance in the lower parts of the earth. Thy eyes did see my imperfect being, and in thy book all shall be written: days shall be formed, and no one in them."
*Psalms 138:13-16 (Douay-Rheims)*

The psalmist says God formed him in the womb. The verse is numbered 138 in the Douay-Rheims and 139 in other Bibles.

## Why this matters today

A positive test can be frightening, and parents often feel pressure to end the pregnancy. The Church asks the community to support them, not to leave them alone.

Knowing in advance can help a family prepare. The question is what the knowledge is used for.

## What's at stake

If a child's worth depends on passing a test, no one is safe.

## How to live it

- **Ask** what a test is for before you take it.
- **Find** support groups for the condition.
- **Be** a friend to a family with a disabled child.
- **Never** shame a parent for a hard choice.
