## The short version

Protecting unborn children and helping their mothers are the same work. Jesus says what you do for the least you do for him, and James says pure religion is to visit orphans and widows in their trouble (Matthew 25:35-40; James 1:27).

## What the Church teaches

Jesus says that whatever you did for one of the least of his brethren, you did for him (Matthew 25:40).

James says that religion that is pure and undefiled is to visit orphans and widows in their tribulation (James 1:27).

The Catechism says the Church's love for the poor "is a part of her constant tradition" (CCC 2444), and that the works of mercy include feeding the hungry and sheltering the homeless (CCC 2447).

John Paul II calls for "a new feminism" that rejects "male domination" and supports women in culture (Evangelium Vitae 99).

"Society should permit each of its members to fulfill his vocation" (CCC 1907), and the family is "the original cell of social life" (CCC 2207).

## Scripture

> "Religion clean and undefiled before God and the Father, is this: to visit the fatherless and widows in their tribulation: and to keep one's self unspotted from this world."
*James 1:27 (Douay-Rheims)*

James puts care for the vulnerable at the center of true religion.

## Why this matters today

Critics say the movement cares about birth and not what follows. The Church's teaching asks for care before, during and after.

What helps most is practical: housing, diapers, rides, a job, a friend.

## What's at stake

Being against abortion means little if a mother is left alone with the cost.

## How to live it

- **Volunteer** at a pregnancy center or a shelter.
- **Give** diapers, money or a spare room.
- **Speak** kindly to a woman in crisis.
- **Support** policies that help families.
