## The short version

Every human being shares the same dignity. The Catechism says "the equality of men rests essentially on their dignity as persons" and that sinful inequalities are "in open contradiction of the Gospel" (CCC 1935, 1938).

## What the Church teaches

"The equality of men rests essentially on their dignity as persons and the rights that flow from it" (CCC 1935).

"There exist also sinful inequalities that affect millions of men and women. These are in open contradiction of the Gospel" (CCC 1938).

Paul tells the Athenians that God made from one man every nation of mankind to live on the whole face of the earth (Acts 17:26).

Paul tells the Galatians that in Christ there is neither Jew nor Greek, neither slave nor free, neither male nor female, for all are one in Christ Jesus (Galatians 3:28).

"Everyone should look upon his neighbor (without any exception) as 'another self'" (CCC 1931).

## Scripture

> "And hath made of one, all mankind, to dwell upon the whole face of the earth, determining appointed times, and the limits of their habitation."
*Acts 17:26 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "For you are all the children of God by faith, in Christ Jesus. For as many of you as have been baptized in Christ, have put on Christ. There is neither Jew nor Greek: there is neither bond nor free: there is neither male nor female. For you are all one in Christ Jesus."
*Galatians 3:26-28 (Douay-Rheims)*

Acts says all peoples come from one origin. Galatians says that in Christ the dividing lines no longer separate believers.

## Why this matters today

Racism can be personal or built into systems. The Catechism treats social structures that deny equality as sinful (CCC 1938).

Christians and the Church have a record on this, both good and bad. The Church says the Gospel condemns it.

## What's at stake

If some people count less, none of us is safe.

## How to live it

- **Listen** to people who experience it.
- **Speak up** when you hear it.
- **Ask** where your own prejudices are.
- **Befriend** someone different.
