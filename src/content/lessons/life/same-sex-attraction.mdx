## The short version

The Church makes a distinction most people miss: attraction is not the same thing as acts. Having same-sex attraction is not a sin. Homosexual acts are. Every person, whatever their attractions, is called to chastity, and every person deserves respect.

## What the Church teaches

The Church holds that "homosexual acts are intrinsically disordered. They are contrary to the natural law. They close the sexual act to the gift of life... Under no circumstances can they be approved" (CCC 2357).

The next paragraph says the inclination itself is objectively disordered, and that for most people it is a trial. They "must be accepted with respect, compassion, and sensitivity. Every sign of unjust discrimination in their regard should be avoided" (CCC 2358).

"Homosexual persons are called to chastity," and through self-mastery, friendship, prayer, and grace, "they can and should gradually and resolutely approach Christian perfection" (CCC 2359).

## Scripture

> "Have ye not read, that he who made man from the beginning, made them male and female? And he said: For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife, and they two shall be in one flesh."
*Matthew 19:4-5 (Douay-Rheims)*

St. Paul lists those who practice homosexual acts among those who "shall not possess the kingdom of God," right alongside fornicators, adulterers, thieves, the greedy, and drunkards. Then he says:

> "And such some of you were; but you are washed, but you are sanctified, but you are justified in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ."
*1 Corinthians 6:11 (Douay-Rheims)*

## Why this hits hard today

This is the most contested moral teaching in the West right now. Same-sex marriage is legal in the U.S. Disagreeing is often called hate. Some Christian denominations have changed their teaching. Catholics with same-sex attraction often feel caught between a culture that says "be yourself" and a Church they love.

About *Fiducia Supplicans* (2023): it allowed priests to give brief, spontaneous, non-liturgical blessings to people in irregular situations, including same-sex couples, as individuals asking for God's help. It explicitly said this does not approve the relationship and does not change doctrine on marriage. Many bishops' conferences, especially in Africa, said they would not apply it. The teaching in CCC 2357-2359 stands.

## What's at stake

Homosexual acts are grave matter, like any sex outside marriage. The call to chastity is hard, and the Church doesn't pretend it isn't. It's a cross. But it's the same call every unmarried person gets, and Christ promises grace to carry it.

The Church also forbids hatred, mockery, and unjust discrimination. Bullying, slurs, and contempt are sins too.

## What to do

- **If you experience same-sex attraction:** You are not your attractions. You are a son or daughter of God. Courage International is a Church-approved apostolate that offers support groups and spiritual friendship. Find a good confessor and spiritual director.
- **If a friend comes out to you:** Listen first. Love them. Don't stop being their friend. Be honest about what you believe if they ask, kindly and without lecturing.
- **Never use slurs or mock anyone.**
