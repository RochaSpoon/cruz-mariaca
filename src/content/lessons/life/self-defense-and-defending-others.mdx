## The short version

The Church teaches that legitimate defense is not murder. "Someone who defends his life is not guilty of murder even if he is forced to deal his aggressor a lethal blow," and defense of others can be a grave duty (CCC 2263-2265).

## What the Church teaches

"The legitimate defense of persons and societies is not an exception to the prohibition against the murder of the innocent that constitutes intentional killing" (CCC 2263).

"The act of self-defense can have a double effect: the preservation of one's own life; and the killing of the aggressor.... The one is intended, the other is not" (CCC 2263).

"Love toward oneself remains a fundamental principle of morality. Therefore it is legitimate to insist on respect for one's own right to life" (CCC 2264).

"Legitimate defense can be not only a right but a grave duty for someone responsible for another's life" (CCC 2265).

Aquinas states the principle: the act of self-defense "may have two effects, one is the saving of one's life, the other is the slaying of the aggressor," and only the first may be intended (ST II-II, q.64, a.7).

## Scripture

> "If a thief be found breaking open a house or undermining it, and be wounded so as to die: he that slew him shall not be guilty of blood. But if he did this when the sun is risen, he hath committed murder, and he shall die. If he have not wherewith to make restitution for the theft, he shall be sold."
*Exodus 22:2-3 (Douay-Rheims)*

Exodus says that if a thief is killed while breaking in at night, no guilt of blood falls on the defender. The Church builds on this with limits.

## Why this matters today

The Church does not say that you may use whatever force you like. The force must be proportionate to the threat.

Fear is not the same as a real threat, and a prepared person should know the law.

## What's at stake

A person may protect life, theirs and others'. The Church also insists that life is never to be taken lightly.

## How to live it

- **Avoid** conflict where you can.
- **Know** your local law.
- **Use** only what you need.
- **Go to Confession** if you have hurt someone, even in defense.
