## The short version

Your body is good and you are loved. Paul says your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19), and the Catechism says life and health are gifts to be cared for (CCC 2288). If you are in danger, call or text 988 in the US.

## What the Church teaches

Paul says your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19).

"Life and physical health are precious gifts entrusted to us by God. We must take reasonable care of them" (CCC 2288).

The Catechism says "the human body shares in the dignity of 'the image of God'" (CCC 364).

The psalmist says he is fearfully and wonderfully made (Psalm 138:14, Douay-Rheims numbering).

The Church does not blame people in anguish. Of suicide, she says "grave psychological disturbances, anguish, or grave fear of hardship, suffering, or torture can diminish the responsibility" (CCC 2282).

This lesson is not medical advice. A doctor, therapist or crisis line is the right place to start.

## Scripture

> "Or know you not, that your members are the temple of the Holy Ghost, who is in you, whom you have from God; and you are not your own? For you are bought with a great price. Glorify and bear God in your body."
*1 Corinthians 6:19-20 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says you were bought at a great price, so glorify God in your body.

## Why this matters today

Many people who hurt themselves hide it out of shame. The Church's message is that you are loved and can be helped.

If you are in danger, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.

## What's at stake

Hiding it makes it worse. Telling one person is the first step.

## How to live it

- **Tell** one trusted person.
- **Call or text 988** if you are in crisis.
- **See** a doctor or therapist.
- **Remove** what you use to hurt yourself.
