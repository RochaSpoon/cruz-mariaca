## The short version

The Church says suicide is gravely contrary to love of self, and also that grave suffering can lessen responsibility, and that we "should not despair of the eternal salvation" of those who die by suicide (CCC 2281-2283). If you are in danger, call or text 988 in the US.

## What the Church teaches

"Everyone is responsible for his life before God who has given it to him. It is God who remains the sovereign Master of life... We are stewards, not owners, of the life God has entrusted to us" (CCC 2280).

"Suicide contradicts the natural inclination of the human being to preserve and perpetuate his life" (CCC 2281).

"Grave psychological disturbances, anguish, or grave fear of hardship, suffering, or torture can diminish the responsibility of the one committing suicide" (CCC 2282).

"We should not despair of the eternal salvation of persons who have taken their own lives. By ways known to him alone, God can provide the opportunity for salutary repentance. The Church prays for persons who have taken their own lives" (CCC 2283).

The psalmist says the Lord is near to those who are broken of heart (Psalm 33:19, Douay-Rheims numbering).

## Scripture

> "The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a contrite heart: and he will save the humble of spirit."
*Psalms 33:19 (Douay-Rheims)*

This is Psalm 33 in the Douay-Rheims and 34 in other Bibles.

## Why this matters today

If you are thinking of ending your life, you are not alone, and this is not a test of faith. Call or text 988 in the United States, call emergency services, or tell someone near you now.

If someone you love died this way, the Church says you need not despair about them.

## What's at stake

The pain is real and it can pass. The Church says your life is a gift, and that help is available.

## How to live it

- **Call or text 988** (US).
- **Tell** one person today.
- **See** a doctor or counselor.
- **Pray** the psalm.
