## The short version

The Church rejects surrogacy. The Catechism calls the use of a surrogate "gravely immoral," and Dignitas Infinita says it "violates the dignity of the child" and of the woman (CCC 2376; Dignitas Infinita 48-49).

## What the Church teaches

"Techniques that entail the dissociation of husband and wife, by the intrusion of a person other than the couple (donation of sperm or ovum, surrogate uterus), are gravely immoral" (CCC 2376).

Donum Vitae says that "surrogate motherhood represents an objective failure to meet the obligations of maternal love, of conjugal fidelity and of responsible motherhood; it offends the dignity and the right of the child to be conceived, carried in the womb, brought into the world and brought up by his own parents" (Donum Vitae II.A.3).

Dignitas Infinita (2024) says the Church "takes a stand against the practice of surrogacy, through which the immensely worthy child becomes a mere object" (Dignitas Infinita 48).

It quotes Pope Francis: surrogacy is "a grave violation of the dignity of the woman and the child, based on the exploitation of situations of the mother's material needs. A child is always a gift and never the basis of a commercial contract" (Dignitas Infinita 48).

## Scripture

> "Behold the inheritance of the Lord are children: the reward, the fruit of the womb."
*Psalms 126:3 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims numbers this psalm 126. It says that children are the inheritance of the Lord.

## Why this matters today

Surrogacy is often presented as an act of generosity. The Church's concern is that the child becomes a product and the woman's body a service, especially when she is poor.

The child born through surrogacy has full dignity and is loved by God.

## What's at stake

If a child can be ordered, then children are goods. The Church says a child is always a gift.

## How to live it

- **Learn** the reasons.
- **Consider** adoption.
- **Be kind** to families formed this way.
- **Support** women who are pressured by poverty.
