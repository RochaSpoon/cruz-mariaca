## The short version

The Church holds that abortion by any method, including a pill, is gravely wrong, and that the Church also offers forgiveness and healing (CCC 2270-2272; Evangelium Vitae 58, 99).

## What the Church teaches

"Human life must be respected and protected absolutely from the moment of conception" (CCC 2270).

"Since the first century the Church has affirmed the moral evil of every procured abortion. This teaching has not changed and remains unchangeable. Direct abortion, that is to say, abortion willed either as an end or a means, is gravely contrary to the moral law" (CCC 2271).

"Formal cooperation in an abortion constitutes a grave offense" (CCC 2272).

John Paul II calls procured abortion "particularly serious and deplorable" (Evangelium Vitae 58).

He also writes to women who have had abortions: "The Church is aware of the many factors which may have influenced your decision... Certainly what happened was and remains terribly wrong. But do not give in to discouragement and do not lose hope" (Evangelium Vitae 99).

"The Father of mercies is ready to give you his forgiveness and his peace in the Sacrament of Reconciliation" (Evangelium Vitae 99).

## Scripture

Isaiah says that though sins be red as scarlet, they shall be made white as snow.

## Why this matters today

A pill makes abortion feel private and easy. The moral question the Church asks is the same as for any other method: whether a human life is being ended.

If you or someone you know has taken one, the Church's message is forgiveness and help, not condemnation.

## What's at stake

The Church says a child's life is at stake, and also that the mother is not lost.

## How to live it

- **Be** gentle with anyone who has had an abortion.
- **Offer** practical help to women in crisis.
- **Tell** anyone who regrets it that mercy is real.
- **Pray.**
