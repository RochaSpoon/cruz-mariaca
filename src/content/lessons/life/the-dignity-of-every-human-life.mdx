## The short version

The Church teaches that every human life is sacred from its beginning to its natural end, because it involves the creative action of God (CCC 2258).

## What the Church teaches

"Human life is sacred because from its beginning it involves the creative action of God and it remains for ever in a special relationship with the Creator, who is its sole end. God alone is the Lord of life from its beginning until its end: no one can under any circumstance claim for himself the right directly to destroy an innocent human being" (CCC 2258).

"The dignity of the human person is rooted in his creation in the image and likeness of God" (CCC 1700).

After Cain kills Abel, God says "What have you done? The voice of your brother's blood is crying to me from the ground" (CCC 2259; Genesis 4:10).

"The deliberate murder of an innocent person is gravely contrary to the dignity of the human being, to the golden rule, and to the holiness of the Creator" (CCC 2261).

The Catechism teaches that human life must be respected "from the moment of conception" (CCC 2270).

## Scripture

> "And he said: Let us make man to our image and likeness: and let him have dominion over the fishes of the sea, and the fowls of the air, and the beasts, and the whole earth, and every creeping creature that moveth upon the earth. And God created man to his own image: to the image of God he created him: male and female he created them."
*Genesis 1:26-27 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "For I will require the blood of your lives at the hand of every beast, and at the hand of man, at the hand of every man, and of his brother, will I require the life of man. Whosoever shall shed man's blood, his blood shall be shed: for man was made to the image of God."
*Genesis 9:5-6 (Douay-Rheims)*

Genesis 1 says man is made in God's image, and Genesis 9 forbids shedding human blood because of it.

## Why this matters today

Most disputes about life, such as abortion, euthanasia and embryo research, come back to whether every human being has dignity just by existing. The Church says yes.

That claim is meant to protect the weakest first: the unborn, the dying, the disabled.

## What's at stake

If dignity depends on ability, age or usefulness, then it can be taken away. The Church says it cannot.

## How to fight it

- **Treat** every person you meet as made in God's image.
- **Help** a mother, a sick person, a refugee.
- **Learn** the reasons, not only the slogans.
- **Speak** with gentleness.
