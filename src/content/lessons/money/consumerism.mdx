## The short version

Jesus says that "a man's life doth not consist in the abundance of things which he possesseth" (Luke 12:15). The Catechism says love of the poor "is incompatible with immoderate love of riches" (CCC 2445).

## What the Church teaches

Jesus says, "Take heed and beware of all covetousness: for a man's life doth not consist in the abundance of things which he possesseth" (Luke 12:15, Douay-Rheims).

The Letter to the Hebrews says to be content with what you have: "Let your manners be without covetousness, contented with such things as you have" (Hebrews 13:5).

"Love for the poor is incompatible with immoderate love of riches or their selfish use" (CCC 2445).

The Catechism teaches that "those who hold goods for use and consumption should use them with moderation, reserving the better part for guests, for the sick and the poor" (CCC 2405).

Temperance "moderates the attraction of pleasures and provides balance in the use of created goods" (CCC 1809).

## Scripture

> "And he said to them: Take heed and beware of all covetousness; for a man's life doth not consist in the abundance of things which he possesseth."
*Luke 12:15 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Let your manners be without covetousness, contented with such things as you have; for he hath said: I will not leave thee, neither will I forsake thee."
*Hebrews 13:5 (Douay-Rheims)*

Both verses warn against greed and call for contentment.

## Why this matters today

Advertising's whole business is making you feel you lack something. The Church's counter is contentment and giving.

The Church does not ban owning things. It teaches moderation and generosity (CCC 2405).

## What's at stake

If you are what you buy, you are always one purchase short.

## How to live it

- **Wait a week** before a purchase you want.
- **Give away** one thing for each new one.
- **Unfollow** accounts that make you want things.
- **Keep a gratitude list.**
