## The short version

Paying workers fairly is a moral duty. The Catechism says "a just wage is the legitimate fruit of work. To refuse or withhold it can be a grave injustice" (CCC 2434).

## What the Church teaches

"A just wage is the legitimate fruit of work. To refuse or withhold it can be a grave injustice. In determining fair pay both the needs and the contributions of each person must be taken into account" (CCC 2434).

"Remuneration for work should guarantee man the opportunity to provide a dignified livelihood for himself and his family on the material, social, cultural and spiritual level, taking into account the role and the productivity of each, the state of the business, and the common good" (CCC 2434).

"Agreement between the parties is not sufficient to justify morally the amount to be received in wages" (CCC 2434).

Leo XIII wrote in Rerum Novarum (1891) that "wages ought not to be insufficient to support a frugal and well-behaved wage-earner," even where the worker has agreed to less (Rerum Novarum 45).

James says the wages of the laborers "kept back by fraud" cry out to the Lord (James 5:4).

## Scripture

> "Behold the hire of the labourers, who have reaped down your fields, which by fraud has been kept back by you, crieth: and the cry of them hath entered into the ears of the Lord of Sabaoth."
*James 5:4 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Catechism lists defrauding the wage earner among the sins that cry to heaven (CCC 1867).

## Why this matters today

Wage theft is real: unpaid overtime, withheld tips, misclassified workers. The Catechism treats it as grave.

The Church does not give a number. It gives principles: needs, contribution, the state of the business and the common good (CCC 2434).

## What's at stake

If wages are only what the market bears, the weakest pay the price. The Church says the person comes first.

## How to live it

- **If you hire,** pay on time and in full.
- **Know your rights** as a worker.
- **Tip well** where tips are part of someone's wage.
- **Ask where your goods come from.**
