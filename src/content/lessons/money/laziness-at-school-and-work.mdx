## The short version

Diligence is part of the Christian duty to work. The Catechism says work is a duty, quoting Paul: "if any one will not work, let him not eat" (CCC 2427).

## What the Church teaches

"Work is a duty: 'If any one will not work, let him not eat.' Work honors the Creator's gifts and the talents received from him" (CCC 2427).

Paul says this to the Thessalonians about those who live in idleness (2 Thessalonians 3:10).

Proverbs tells the sluggard to consider the ant (Proverbs 6:6-11).

The Catechism names sloth among the capital sins (CCC 1866) and acedia as a refusal of the joy that comes from God (CCC 2094).

Rest is not laziness. The Church asks for Sunday rest for "appropriate relaxation of mind and body" (CCC 2185).

## Scripture

> "Go to the ant, O sluggard, and consider her ways, and learn wisdom: Which, although she hath no guide, nor master, nor captain, Provideth her meat for herself in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest. How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou rise out of thy sleep? Thou wilt sleep a little, thou wilt slumber a little, thou wilt fold thy hands a little to sleep: And want shall come upon thee, as a traveller, and poverty as a man armed. But if thou be diligent, thy harvest shall come as a fountain, and want shall flee far from thee."
*Proverbs 6:6-11 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "For also when we were with you, this we declared to you: that, if any man will not work, neither let him eat."
*2 Thessalonians 3:10 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Proverbs passage is a sharp warning. The Thessalonians verse is about those who refuse to work, not those who cannot.

## Why this matters today

Students and workers both feel the pull of minimum effort. The Church asks you to use the talents you were given.

Some tiredness is illness, not laziness, and some low motivation is depression. Seek help if that fits.

## What's at stake

Wasted talent is a loss to you and to everyone who would have benefited.

## How to live it

- **Start the task** you are avoiding for ten minutes.
- **Offer it up.**
- **Keep a schedule.**
- **Rest on purpose,** and call it rest.
