## The short version

The Catechism says "the faithful also have the duty of providing for the material needs of the Church, each according to his abilities" (CCC 2043). It does not set a percentage.

## What the Church teaches

The fifth duty listed under the precepts of the Church is: "The faithful also have the duty of providing for the material needs of the Church, each according to his abilities" (CCC 2043).

The Old Testament command to tithe is in Malachi: "Bring all the tithes into the storehouse" (Malachi 3:10).

The Catechism notes that the Old Testament contained "the obligation to tithe" among measures that served the poor (CCC 2449).

The Church does not prescribe ten percent. The measure is "according to his abilities" (CCC 2043).

## Scripture

> "Bring all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in my house, and try me in this, saith the Lord: if I open not unto you the flood-gates of heaven, and pour you out a blessing even to abundance."
*Malachias 3:10 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims calls the book Malachias. Many Protestant churches teach tithing as a rule. The Catholic Church does not set a percentage.

## Why this matters today

Many Catholics give a few dollars on Sunday. The Church asks you to give in proportion to what you have.

Giving is also trust. It is a way of saying that your money belongs to God.

## What's at stake

If you do not support your parish, it will struggle. If you give only what you will not miss, you miss the point.

## How to live it

- **Pick a percentage** and give it first.
- **Give to the parish** and to the poor.
- **Use envelopes or automatic giving.**
- **Ask to see the budget.**
