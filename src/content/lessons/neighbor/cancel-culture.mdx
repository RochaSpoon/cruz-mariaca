## The short version

The Church's answer to public shaming is neither to ignore wrong nor to pile on. Jesus did not condemn the woman caught in sin, and told her to sin no more (John 8:1-11). Paul says to restore the sinner gently (Galatians 6:1).

## What the Church teaches

Jesus tells the accusers that whoever is without sin should cast the first stone, and then tells the woman that he does not condemn her and that she should go and sin no more (John 8:7-11).

Paul says that if someone is caught in a fault, those who are spiritual should restore him gently, watching themselves lest they also be tempted (Galatians 6:1).

The Catechism says that detraction and calumny destroy reputation, and that "every offense committed against justice and truth entails the duty of reparation" (CCC 2479, 2487).

It also says the faithful should "interpret insofar as possible his neighbor's thoughts, words, and deeds in a favorable way" (CCC 2478).

Justice is a virtue (CCC 1807). Wrongs should be named, and the guilty can be held to account. The Church also has a place for repentance and restoration.

## Scripture

But they hearing this, went out one by one, beginning at the eldest. And Jesus alone remained, and the woman standing in the midst. Then Jesus lifting up himself, said to her: Woman, where are they that accused thee? Hath no man condemned thee? Who said: No man, Lord. And Jesus said: Neither will I condemn thee. Go, and now sin no more."
*John 8:3-11 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus does not say the woman did nothing wrong. He says to go and sin no more.

## Why this matters today

Public shaming can be a form of justice when institutions fail. It can also destroy people over a mistake, with no way back.

The Church does not take a side in every dispute. She asks for truth, mercy and a path to repair.

## What's at stake

If there is no mercy, no one dares to repent. If there is no justice, victims are ignored.

## How to live it

- **Check the facts** before you share.
- **Speak to** the person first, if you can.
- **Hold** wrongdoers accountable and leave the door to repentance open.
- **Remember** your own record.
