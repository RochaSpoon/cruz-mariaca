## The short version

The Catechism calls charity "friendship and communion" (CCC 1829), and Jesus says "greater love than this no man hath, that a man lay down his life for his friends" (John 15:13).

## What the Church teaches

"Charity... is friendship and communion" (CCC 1829).

Jesus tells his disciples that he no longer calls them servants but friends (John 15:15).

The Catechism says solidarity can be called "friendship" or "social charity" (CCC 1939).

Chastity "blossoms in friendship," and the Catechism says it shows the disciple how to follow him "who has chosen us as his friends" (CCC 2347).

Ecclesiasticus praises a faithful friend as a strong defense and a treasure (Ecclesiasticus 6:14-17).

## Scripture

> "Greater love than this no man hath, that a man lay down his life for his friends. You are my friends, if you do the things that I command you. I will not now call you servants: for the servant knoweth not what his lord doth. But I have called you friends: because all things whatsoever I have heard of my Father, I have made known to you."
*John 15:13-15 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "A faithful friend is a strong defence: and he that hath found him, hath found a treasure. Nothing can be compared to a faithful friend, and no weight of gold and silver is able to countervail the goodness of his fidelity. A faithful friend is the medicine of life and immortality: and they that fear the Lord, shall find him. He that feareth God, shall likewise have good friendship: because according to him shall his friend be."
*Ecclesiasticus 6:14-17 (Douay-Rheims)*

Ecclesiasticus is the Douay-Rheims name for Sirach. It has a long section on friendship.

## Why this matters today

Many people have many contacts and few friends. The Church describes friendship as something that costs and lasts.

Real friends tell you the truth and stay when it is hard.

## What's at stake

Friends shape who you become.

## How to live it

- **Reach out** to one old friend.
- **Be a friend** you would want.
- **Choose** friends who make you better.
- **Pray** for your friends.
