## The short version

"Deliberate hatred is contrary to charity." Hatred is a sin when you deliberately wish someone evil, and a grave sin when you desire them grave harm (CCC 2303).

## What the Church teaches

"Deliberate hatred is contrary to charity. Hatred of the neighbor is a sin when one deliberately wishes him evil. Hatred of the neighbor is a grave sin when one deliberately desires him grave harm" (CCC 2303).

Jesus says: "Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you, so that you may be sons of your Father who is in heaven" (CCC 2303).

Our Lord "denounced murderous anger and hatred as immoral" (CCC 2302).

John says that whoever hates his brother is a murderer (1 John 3:15).

## Scripture

> "Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer. And you know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in himself."
*1 John 3:15 (Douay-Rheims)*

John is blunt. The Church reads it as a warning that hatred is the root of murder, even when no blow is struck.

## Why this matters today

Hatred is easy to feed online. It can feel like moral energy.

Anger at injustice is not hatred of a person. The Church distinguishes them (CCC 2302).

## What's at stake

Hatred hurts the one who carries it.

## How to live it

- **Pray for** the person by name.
- **Do something small** that is good for them.
- **Step away** from places that feed it.
- **Go to Confession.**
