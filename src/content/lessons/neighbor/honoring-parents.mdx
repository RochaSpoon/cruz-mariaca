## The short version

God commands you to honor your parents. While you live at home, that means obeying them in everything that isn't sinful. As an adult, it means respect, gratitude, and caring for them when they're old. It doesn't mean doing something wrong because they told you to.

## What the Church teaches

"As long as a child lives at home with his parents, the child should obey his parents in all that they ask of him when it is for his good or that of the family" (CCC 2217). But "if they are convinced in conscience that it would be morally wrong to obey a particular order, they must not do so" (CCC 2217).

Respect for parents comes from gratitude to the people who gave you life, love, and work (CCC 2215). Adult children owe their parents material and moral support in old age, sickness, loneliness, or distress (CCC 2218).

Parents have duties too: to educate their children in faith and virtue and to treat them as children of God (CCC 2221-2231). Ephesians 6:4 tells fathers not to "provoke your children to anger."

## Scripture

> "Children, obey your parents in the Lord: for this is just. Honour thy father and thy mother, which is the first commandment with a promise: That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest be long lived upon earth."
*Ephesians 6:1-3 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus, who is God, "was subject to" Mary and Joseph (Luke 2:51). Sirach 3 says that honoring your father atones for sins, and whoever honors his mother "is as one that layeth up a treasure."

## Why this hits hard today

Pop culture often plays parents as clueless, and teens as the smart ones. Disrespect, eye rolls, silent treatment, sneaking around, and lying about where you are get treated as normal teenage behavior. Many families are also broken, and some parents really are harmful. If a parent abuses you, you don't owe them obedience in that. Tell a trusted adult.

## What's at stake

Contempt for parents, serious disobedience, cursing them, or abandoning them in need can be grave sins. Scripture repeatedly links honoring parents to blessing, and dishonoring them to curse (Sirach 3; Proverbs 20:20).

## How to live it

- **Obey the first time,** without arguing, when it's not sinful.
- **Tell them where you are.** Don't make them guess.
- **Thank them** for specific things.
- **Disagree respectfully.** You can argue your case calmly.
- **Pray for them by name** every day.
