## The short version

The Church's picture of manhood is strength in service. Paul tells husbands to love as Christ loved the Church, and tells all Christians to stand firm in the faith and be strong (Ephesians 5:25; 1 Corinthians 16:13).

## What the Church teaches

"Each of the two sexes is an image of the power and tenderness of God, with equal dignity though in a different way" (CCC 2335).

The Catechism lists fortitude, "firmness in difficulties and constancy in the pursuit of the good" (CCC 1808).

"Whoever would be great among you must be your servant," Jesus says, and "those who exercise authority should do so as a service" (CCC 2235).

Paul says to husbands: love your wives as Christ loved the Church and gave himself for her (Ephesians 5:25).

Paul also tells the Corinthians to watch, stand firm in the faith, act like men and be strong (1 Corinthians 16:13).

## Scripture

> "Husbands, love your wives, as Christ also loved the church, and delivered himself up for it:"
*Ephesians 5:25 (Douay-Rheims)*

The model of strength in the Bible is Christ giving himself.

## Why this matters today

Many young men feel pulled between swagger and softness. The Church's picture includes both strength and tenderness in the same person (CCC 2335).

Domination, contempt for women and hiding feelings are not the Catholic ideal.

## What's at stake

A man who has power and no service is dangerous.

## How to live it

- **Use** your strength to protect.
- **Learn** to say sorry.
- **Be** faithful.
- **Find** men who show it.
