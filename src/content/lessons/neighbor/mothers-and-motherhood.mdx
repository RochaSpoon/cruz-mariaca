## The short version

John Paul II's Mulieris Dignitatem looks to Mary as the model for women. The Church says man and woman are "equally" persons created in God's image (CCC 2334).

## What the Church teaches

"In creating men 'male and female,' God gives man and woman an equal personal dignity" (CCC 2334).

"Each of the two sexes is an image of the power and tenderness of God, with equal dignity though in a different way" (CCC 2335).

John Paul II wrote Mulieris Dignitatem in 1988 on the dignity and vocation of women, and begins its biblical reflection from Genesis 1:27: God created man in his own image, male and female (Mulieris Dignitatem 6).

He also wrote that in Mary's case the Annunciation dialogue shows her free consent: the event "is clearly interpersonal in character: it is a dialogue" (Mulieris Dignitatem 5).

Mary's yes shows both receptivity and strength: "Let it be done to me according to your word" (CCC 494).

## Scripture

> "And Mary said: Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it done to me according to thy word. And the angel departed from her."
*Luke 1:38 (Douay-Rheims)*

Mary says "Behold the handmaid of the Lord." The Church reads this as free consent, not as passivity.

## Why this matters today

Mothers carry a great deal and often feel unseen. The Church honors motherhood and also says a woman's dignity does not depend on it (CCC 2334-2335).

Many women do not become mothers and are not less. The Church honors them too (CCC 2231).

## What's at stake

Honor for mothers is hollow if it excuses neglect.

## How to live it

- **Thank** a mother in your life.
- **Help** with the real work.
- **Pray** the Magnificat.
- **Read** Mulieris Dignitatem 5-7.
