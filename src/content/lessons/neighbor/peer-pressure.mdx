## The short version

Paul says "be not conformed to this world," and Exodus says not to follow a crowd to do evil (Romans 12:2; Exodus 23:2). The Catechism lists bad example as a source of moral error (CCC 1792).

## What the Church teaches

Paul says not to be conformed to this world, but to be transformed by the renewal of the mind (Romans 12:2).

Exodus says not to follow the crowd in doing evil (Exodus 23:2).

The Catechism lists "bad example given by others" among the sources of error of judgment in moral conduct (CCC 1792).

It says the one who gives scandal "becomes his neighbor's tempter" and that scandal can be caused by "fashion or opinion" (CCC 2284, 2286).

"A human being must always obey the certain judgment of his conscience" (CCC 1790). Conscience must be formed (CCC 1783).

## Scripture

> "And be not conformed to this world; but be reformed in the newness of your mind, that you may prove what is the good, and the acceptable, and the perfect will of God."
*Romans 12:2 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Thou shalt not follow the multitude to do evil: neither shalt thou yield in judgment, to the opinion of the most part, to stray from the truth."
*Exodus 23:2 (Douay-Rheims)*

Both verses say to stand apart from the crowd when the crowd is wrong.

## Why this matters today

The pressure is as strong as it has ever been, now with likes and group chats.

You are not required to be rude. A calm no, a change of subject or leaving is enough.

## What's at stake

If you follow the crowd, you give them your conscience.

## How to live it

- **Decide** your limits before you are in the room.
- **Have** one friend who will back you.
- **Say no** without a speech.
- **Leave** if you need to.
