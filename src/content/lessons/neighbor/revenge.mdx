## The short version

Getting even is not yours to do. The Catechism says "to desire vengeance in order to do evil to someone who should be punished is illicit," and Paul tells Christians not to avenge themselves (CCC 2302; Romans 12:19).

## What the Church teaches

"Anger is a desire for revenge. 'To desire vengeance in order to do evil to someone who should be punished is illicit,' but it is praiseworthy to impose restitution 'to correct vices and maintain justice'" (CCC 2302).

"If anger reaches the point of a deliberate desire to kill or seriously wound a neighbor, it is gravely against charity; it is a mortal sin" (CCC 2302).

Paul tells the Romans to repay no one evil for evil, to live at peace, and to leave vengeance to God (Romans 12:17-19).

Leviticus says: you shall not seek revenge nor bear a grudge against your people, but love your neighbor as yourself (Leviticus 19:18).

Legitimate authorities can punish wrongdoers. That is justice, and it is not the same as private revenge (CCC 2266).

## Scripture

> "To no man rendering evil for evil. Providing good things, not only in the sight of God, but also in the sight of all men. If it be possible, as much as is in you, have peace with all men. Revenge not yourselves, my dearly beloved; but give place unto wrath, for it is written: Revenge is mine, I will repay, saith the Lord. But if thy enemy be hungry, give him to eat; if he thirst, give him to drink. For, doing this, thou shalt heap coals of fire upon his head. Be not overcome by evil, but overcome evil by good."
*Romans 12:17-21 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul ends with the line "be not overcome by evil, but overcome evil by good."

## Why this matters today

Revenge now takes the form of leaking messages, posting photos or ghosting. The Church's standard is the same.

Seeking justice through proper channels is not revenge. Reporting a crime is not revenge.

## What's at stake

Revenge hands the story to the person who hurt you.

## How to live it

- **Wait** before you act.
- **Report** serious wrongs to the proper authority.
- **Pray** for the person who hurt you.
- **Do a good turn** for them if you can.
