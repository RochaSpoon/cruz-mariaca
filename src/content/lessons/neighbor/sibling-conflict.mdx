## The short version

Brothers fight in Genesis, and the Bible also shows reconciliation. The Catechism says filial respect "concerns relationships between brothers and sisters," and Psalm 132 says it is good for brothers to dwell together in unity (CCC 2219).

## What the Church teaches

"Filial respect promotes harmony in all of family life; it also concerns relationships between brothers and sisters. Respect toward parents fills the home with light and warmth" (CCC 2219).

"Each and everyone should be generous and tireless in forgiving one another for offenses, quarrels, injustices, and neglect. Mutual affection suggests this. The charity of Christ demands it" (CCC 2227).

Genesis 4 tells the story of Cain and Abel, which ends in murder. Genesis 45 tells how Joseph forgives the brothers who sold him.

The psalmist says how good and pleasant it is for brothers to dwell together in unity (Psalm 132:1, in the Douay-Rheims numbering).

"The family is the original cell of social life" (CCC 2207).

## Scripture

Be not afraid, and let it not seem to you a hard case that you sold me into these countries: for God sent me before you into Egypt for your preservation. For it is two years since the famine began to be upon the land, and five years more remain, wherein there can be neither ploughing nor reaping. And God sent me before, that you may be preserved upon the earth, and may have food to live. Not by your counsel was I sent hither, but by the will of God: who hath made me as it were a father to Pharao, and lord of his whole house, and governor in all the land of Egypt."
*Genesis 45:4-8 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Behold how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell in unity."
*Psalms 132:1 (Douay-Rheims)*

Joseph tells his brothers that God sent him ahead to preserve life. The Church reads this as forgiveness that sees beyond the hurt.

## Why this matters today

Sibling quarrels are normal and can be deep. The Catechism asks for forgiveness that is "generous and tireless" (CCC 2227).

If the conflict is abuse, tell an adult. That is a different matter.

## What's at stake

Peace at home is the first place peace is practiced.

## How to live it

- **Apologize first** if you can.
- **Do not keep score.**
- **Pray** for your sibling.
- **Call** one you have not spoken to.
