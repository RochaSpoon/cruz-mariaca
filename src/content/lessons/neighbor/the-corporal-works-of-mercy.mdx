## The short version

The corporal works of mercy are feeding the hungry, sheltering the homeless, clothing the naked, visiting the sick and imprisoned, and burying the dead (CCC 2447).

## What the Church teaches

"The works of mercy are charitable actions by which we come to the aid of our neighbor in his spiritual and bodily necessities" (CCC 2447).

"The corporal works of mercy consist especially in feeding the hungry, sheltering the homeless, clothing the naked, visiting the sick and imprisoned, and burying the dead" (CCC 2447).

"It is by what they have done for the poor that Jesus Christ will recognize his chosen ones" (CCC 2443).

Jesus says that at the judgment he will say that he was hungry and they gave him to eat, and that whatever they did for one of the least of his brethren they did for him (Matthew 25:35-40).

"The bodies of the dead must be treated with respect and charity, in faith and hope of the Resurrection. The burial of the dead is a corporal work of mercy" (CCC 2300).

## Scripture

> "Then shall the king say to them that shall be on his right hand: Come, ye blessed of my Father, possess you the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world. For I was hungry, and you gave me to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me to drink; I was a stranger, and you took me in: Naked, and you covered me: sick, and you visited me: I was in prison, and you came to me. Then shall the just answer him, saying: Lord, when did we see thee hungry, and fed thee; thirsty, and gave thee drink? And when did we see thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and covered thee? Or when did we see thee sick or in prison, and came to thee? And the king answering, shall say to them: Amen I say to you, as long as you did it to one of these my least brethren, you did it to me."
*Matthew 25:34-40 (Douay-Rheims)*

The list in Matthew 25 has six items. The Church adds burying the dead from the Book of Tobias (Tobias 1:20).

## Why this matters today

The works are practical and local: a meal, a coat, a visit. You do not need a program.

They are not extras. Matthew 25 makes them the measure of the judgment.

## What's at stake

You will meet Christ in the person who needs you, or you will miss him.

## How to live it

- **Pick one** and do it this week.
- **Visit** someone sick or in prison.
- **Give** food or clothing.
- **Go to a funeral** for someone you hardly knew.
