## The short version

The Catechism says man and woman have "equal personal dignity" (CCC 2334). John Paul II's Letter to Women (1995) says thank you to women in every vocation, and asks that their dignity be respected (Letter to Women 6).

## What the Church teaches

"In creating men 'male and female,' God gives man and woman an equal personal dignity" (CCC 2334). "Man is a person, man and woman equally so, since both were created in the image and likeness of the personal God" (CCC 2334).

"Everyone, man and woman, should acknowledge and accept his sexual identity" (CCC 2333).

John Paul II's Letter to Women (1995) offers thanks to women as mothers, wives, daughters, sisters and workers: "Thank you, women who work! You are present and active in every area of life" (Letter to Women 2).

He asks "that everyone, and in a special way States and international institutions, should make every effort to ensure that women regain full respect for their dignity and role" (Letter to Women 6).

Mulieris Dignitatem (1988) grounds this in Genesis 1:27: "male and female he created them" (Mulieris Dignitatem 6).

## Scripture

> "And God created man to his own image: to the image of God he created him: male and female he created them. And God blessed them, saying: Increase and multiply, and fill the earth, and subdue it, and rule over the fishes of the sea, and the fowls of the air, and all living creatures that move upon the earth."
*Genesis 1:27-28 (Douay-Rheims)*

Both man and woman are made in God's image.

## Why this matters today

The Church is often accused of oppressing women. The documents above speak plainly about equal dignity, and also say Christians have sometimes failed to live it.

The Church holds that women and men are equal in dignity and different. She does not say they are the same.

## What's at stake

If dignity depends on usefulness, no one is safe. The Church says it is given by God.

## How to live it

- **Treat** women as full persons.
- **Listen.**
- **Say** thank you.
- **Read** the Letter to Women.
