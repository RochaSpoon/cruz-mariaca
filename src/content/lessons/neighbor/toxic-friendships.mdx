## The short version

Proverbs and Paul both warn that friends shape you. "Evil communications corrupt good manners" (1 Corinthians 15:33). Charity does not mean staying where you are harmed.

## What the Church teaches

Proverbs says that whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, and a companion of fools will suffer harm (Proverbs 13:20).

Paul tells the Corinthians that bad company ruins good character (1 Corinthians 15:33).

The Catechism lists bad example among the sources of error and sin, and says "scandal can be provoked by... fashion or opinion" (CCC 1792, 2286).

Charity requires wishing a person good. It does not require staying in a relationship that harms you (CCC 1822-1825).

Where a friendship involves abuse, tell a trusted adult or authority. Sin can be shared among friends (CCC 1868-1869).

## Scripture

> "He that walketh with the wise, shall be wise: a friend of fools shall become like to them."
*Proverbs 13:20 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Be not seduced: Evil communications corrupt good manners."
*1 Corinthians 15:33 (Douay-Rheims)*

Both verses are about influence. They are not instructions to drop people at the first fault.

## Why this matters today

Some friendships keep you stuck, or pull you into things you do not want. Naming that is not unkind.

If you fear for your safety, talk to someone at once. This lesson is not counseling.

## What's at stake

Your friends write much of your story.

## How to live it

- **Ask** how you are different after you spend time with them.
- **Set** a clear boundary and keep it.
- **Keep praying** for them.
- **Find** new friends who share your values.
