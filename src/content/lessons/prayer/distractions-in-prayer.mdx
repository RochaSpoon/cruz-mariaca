## The short version

Everyone gets distracted at prayer. The Catechism says the remedy is to turn your heart back to God, not to fight the distraction itself (CCC 2729).

## What the Church teaches

A distraction shows what your heart is attached to (CCC 2729). If you keep thinking about work, that is where your heart is. Offer it, then return.

Jesus told Martha she was anxious about many things, and that Mary had chosen the better part (Luke 10:41-42). The point is not that work is bad. It is that attention is limited.

You will lose the thread many times. Each time you come back, you have prayed.

## Scripture

> "And the Lord answering, said to her: Martha, Martha, thou art careful, and art troubled about many things: But one thing is necessary. Mary hath chosen the best part, which shall not be taken away from her."
*Luke 10:41-42 (Douay-Rheims)*

## Why this matters today

Phones have made this worse. You may have a hard time sitting five minutes without reaching for one.

## What's at stake

If you treat distraction as proof you cannot pray, you will stop. If you treat it as normal, you can stay.

## How to live it

- **Put the phone in another room.** Not on the table.
- **Keep a notepad** and write down tasks that pop up. Then let them go.
- **Say the distraction to God:** "I am worried about this. Take it."
- **Use words** if silence is too noisy. Pray a Psalm out loud.
