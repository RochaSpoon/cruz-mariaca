## The short version

The book of Revelation (called the Apocalypse in the Douay-Rheims) shows worship in heaven, with an altar, incense, a Lamb who was slain, and people singing "Holy, holy, holy." Catholics see the Mass as joining that worship (CCC 1137-1139).

## What the Church teaches

Isaiah sees the Lord on a throne with seraphim crying "Holy, holy, holy" (Isaiah 6:1-3). Revelation echoes it (Apocalypse 4:8). At Mass, the Sanctus does the same.

In Revelation 5, a Lamb who looks as if slain stands before the throne, and the elders hold bowls of incense, which are the prayers of the saints (Apocalypse 5:6-8). The Church hears Calvary, the altar and the intercession of the saints in that scene.

The Catechism says the Church on earth takes part in the liturgy of heaven (CCC 1137-1139). That is a teaching. Reading the Mass line by line into Revelation is a theological opinion, one that scholars of different traditions treat very differently.

## Scripture

> "And the four living creatures had each of them six wings; and round about and within they are full of eyes. And they rested not day and night, saying: Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty, who was, and who is, and who is to come."
*Apocalypse 4:8 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "And I saw: and behold in the midst of the throne and of the four living creatures, and in the midst of the ancients, a Lamb standing as it were slain, having seven horns and seven eyes: which are the seven Spirits of God, sent forth into all the earth."
*Apocalypse 5:6 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "And when he had opened the book, the four living creatures, and the four and twenty ancients fell down before the Lamb, having every one of them harps, and golden vials full of odours, which are the prayers of saints:"
*Apocalypse 5:8 (Douay-Rheims)*

Not everyone reads these chapters as liturgy. Many Protestant scholars see worship in them but do not link it to the Mass.

## Why this matters today

If Mass feels small, boring or like a chore, this is a useful corrective. The Church says something much bigger is going on than what the room looks like.

## What's at stake

This is not a matter of salvation. It is a lens. It is worth using if it helps you pay attention, and worth dropping if it starts to sound like a code.

## How to live it

- **Read Apocalypse 4 and 5 once, in one sitting.** Do not hunt for symbols. Just notice the worship.
- **At the Sanctus,** picture the voices around you as the end of something larger.
- **Hold the opinion lightly.** Other Christians read it differently, and some of them read it very well.
