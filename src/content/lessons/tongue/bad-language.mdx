## The short version

Swearing is about what comes out of you. Jesus says what comes out of the mouth comes from the heart (Matthew 15:18). Crude language, sexual jokes, cursing at people, and constant profanity are sins against charity and purity of speech. Most of the time they're venial, but they're still sins, and they shape who you become.

## What Scripture teaches

> "Let no evil speech proceed from your mouth; but that which is good, to the edification of faith, that it may administer grace to the hearers."
*Ephesians 4:29 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "But now put you also all away: anger, indignation, malice, blasphemy, filthy speech out of your mouth."
*Colossians 3:8 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "But the tongue no man can tame, an unquiet evil, full of deadly poison. By it we bless God and the Father: and by it we curse men, who are made after the likeness of God. Out of the same mouth proceedeth blessing and cursing. My brethren, these things ought not so to be."
*James 3:8-10 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh."
*Matthew 12:34 (Douay-Rheims)*

## Three different things

1. **Vulgar words** (the usual swear words). Using them out of habit or frustration is generally a venial sin against temperance and charity of speech.
2. **Cursing someone** ("go to hell," "damn you"). Wishing damnation or serious harm on a person, if meant, is a sin against charity and can be grave.
3. **Obscene talk** (sexual jokes, describing people sexually, crude humor). This is a sin against chastity. It can be grave if it's seriously impure or leads others into sin (Ephesians 5:3-4; CCC 2284).

## Why this hits hard today

Profanity is normal in music, games, sports, and school hallways. Many people swear in every other sentence without noticing. Group chats and locker rooms are full of sexual "jokes" about classmates. Online, people curse at strangers they'd never insult in person.

## What's at stake

Jesus says you'll give an account for every careless word (Matthew 12:36). Habitual swearing makes your speech coarse, which makes your thoughts coarse. Crude talk about others, especially sexual, can do real harm to their reputation and dignity. And Christians who swear constantly give terrible witness: people notice when your words don't match your faith.

## How to fight it

- **Notice.** For one day, count every time you swear. It'll surprise you.
- **Pick replacement words.** Say them on purpose until they're automatic.
- **Fix the inputs.** What you listen to all day comes out of your mouth.
- **Walk out of crude conversations.** Or change the subject. You don't have to preach.
- **Speak words that build up.** Ephesians 4:29 isn't just "don't swear." It's "say good things."
