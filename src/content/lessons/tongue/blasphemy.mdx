## The short version

Blasphemy is speaking against God in hatred, reproach or defiance, or misusing his name. The Catechism calls it "directly opposed to the second commandment" (CCC 2148).

## What the Church teaches

"Blasphemy is directly opposed to the second commandment. It consists in uttering against God - inwardly or outwardly - words of hatred, reproach, or defiance; in speaking ill of God; in failing in respect toward him in one's speech; in misusing God's name" (CCC 2148).

The prohibition "extends to language against Christ's Church, the saints, and sacred things" (CCC 2148).

"Oaths which misuse God's name, though without the intention of blasphemy, show lack of respect for the Lord" (CCC 2149).

The Law in Leviticus 24:16 treated it as a capital offense. The Church does not apply this penalty, and treats the matter as one of grave sin and reverence.

Jesus speaks of a sin against the Holy Spirit. The Catechism reads this as a refusal to repent, not as a single word (CCC 1864).

## Scripture

> "And he that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, dying let him die: all the multitude shall stone him, whether he be a native or a stranger. He that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, dying let him die."
*Leviticus 24:16 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "Therefore I say to you: Every sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven men, but the blasphemy of the Spirit shall not be forgiven. And whosoever shall speak a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but he that shall speak against the Holy Ghost, it shall not be forgiven him, neither in this world, nor in the world to come."
*Matthew 12:31-32 (Douay-Rheims)*

Leviticus gives the penalty under the old covenant for blaspheming the name of the Lord. Jesus' words in Matthew 12 are about something else: hardness of heart.

## Why this matters today

Using God's name as an exclamation is so common that most people do not hear it. The Catechism treats misusing the name as a lack of respect (CCC 2149).

Mocking someone else's faith is a different matter, though also an offense against charity.

## What's at stake

The name stands for a person. How you use it shows what you think of him.

## How to live it

- **Drop the exclamation.**
- **Say the name in prayer.**
- **If you have blasphemed in anger,** confess it.
- **Speak respectfully** about other people's sacred things.
