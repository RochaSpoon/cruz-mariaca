## The short version

Creating or sharing fake images or videos of real people is lying and harms their reputation. The Catechism says a lie is speaking "with the intention of deceiving" and requires reparation (CCC 2477, 2482, 2487).

## What the Church teaches

"A lie consists in speaking a falsehood with the intention of deceiving" (CCC 2482). The Catechism also says to lie is "to speak or act against the truth in order to lead into error someone who has the right to know the truth" (CCC 2483). A fabricated image or video is an act against the truth.

A person is guilty of calumny who "by remarks contrary to the truth, harms the reputation of others and gives occasion for false judgments concerning them" (CCC 2477).

"Lying is destructive of society; it undermines trust among men and tears apart the fabric of social relationships" (CCC 2486).

Anyone who spreads it shares responsibility, since we are responsible for sins we cooperate in "by ordering, advising, praising, or approving them" (CCC 1868).

Every offense against justice and truth "entails the duty of reparation, even if its author has been forgiven" (CCC 2487).

The Catechism also condemns systems that "systematically falsify the truth" (CCC 2499).

## Scripture

> "Lie not one to another: stripping yourselves of the old man with his deeds,"
*Colossians 3:9 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul says to lie not one to another, since you have put off the old man.

## Why this matters today

Technology can now produce convincing fake images, voices and video. Fakes meant to humiliate, such as sexual images of a real person, do especially deep harm to the person shown.

If you were deceived and shared something false, you can still correct it. That is part of repair (CCC 2487).

## What's at stake

If no one can trust what they see, truth becomes a matter of power. The Church holds that people have a right to know the truth.

## How to live it

- **Do not make** fake images of real people.
- **Do not forward** unverified media.
- **Report** fakes that target someone.
- **If you were fooled,** say so and correct it.
