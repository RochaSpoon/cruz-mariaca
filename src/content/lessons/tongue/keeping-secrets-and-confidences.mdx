## The short version

The right to know the truth "is not unconditional." The Church holds that the seal of confession is inviolable, that professional secrets must be kept, and that there are exceptions when keeping a secret would cause very grave harm (CCC 2488-2491).

## What the Church teaches

"The right to the communication of the truth is not unconditional." We must judge "whether or not it is appropriate to reveal the truth to someone who asks for it" (CCC 2488).

"The good and safety of others, respect for privacy, and the common good are sufficient reasons for being silent about what ought not be known" (CCC 2489).

"The secret of the sacrament of reconciliation is sacred, and cannot be violated under any pretext" (CCC 2490).

"Professional secrets - for example, those of political office holders, soldiers, physicians, and lawyers - or confidential information given under the seal of secrecy must be kept, save in exceptional cases where keeping the secret is bound to cause very grave harm to the one who confided it, to the one who received it or to a third party" (CCC 2491).

"Everyone should observe an appropriate reserve concerning persons' private lives" (CCC 2492).

## Scripture

> "Rehearse not again a wicked and harsh word, and thou shalt not fare the worse. Tell not thy mind to friend or foe: and if there be a sin with thee, disclose it not. For he will hearken to thee, and will watch thee, and as it were defending thy sin he will hate thee, and so will he be with thee always. Hast thou heard a word against thy neighbour? let it die within thee, trusting that it will not burst thee."
*Ecclesiasticus 19:7-10 (Douay-Rheims)*

Ecclesiasticus is the Douay-Rheims name for Sirach. The passage says not to repeat a word you have heard, and that it will be safe with you.

## Why this matters today

Group chats, DMs and screenshots make secrets travel. The Catechism's principle is respect for privacy (CCC 2489, 2492).

If someone tells you they are being hurt or are in danger, the "very grave harm" exception applies, and you should tell a responsible adult (CCC 2491).

## What's at stake

If no one can keep a secret, no one can confide. If every secret is kept, a victim can be left without help.

## How to live it

- **Do not repeat** what was told in confidence.
- **Tell the right person** when someone's safety is at risk.
- **Do not screenshot and share** private messages.
- **Ask before sharing** what someone told you.
