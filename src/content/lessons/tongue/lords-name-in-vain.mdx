## The short version

The Second Commandment forbids every improper use of God's name. That includes casual "OMG," "Jesus Christ!" when you stub your toe, and "God damn it." Using God's name to curse, mock, or vent is a sin. Using it in hatred or contempt is blasphemy, and that's grave.

## What the Church teaches

"The second commandment forbids the abuse of God's name, i.e., every improper use of the names of God, Jesus Christ, but also of the Virgin Mary and all the saints" (CCC 2146).

"Blasphemy is directly opposed to the second commandment. It consists in uttering against God (inwardly or outwardly) words of hatred, reproach, or defiance; in speaking ill of God; in failing in respect toward him in one's speech; in misusing God's name... Blasphemy is in itself a grave sin" (CCC 2148).

False oaths (perjury) are also grave (CCC 2152).

## Scripture

> "Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain: for the Lord will not hold him guiltless that shall take the name of the Lord his God in vain."
*Exodus 20:7 (Douay-Rheims)*

Under the Old Law, blasphemy was punished by death (Leviticus 24:16). The first thing Jesus teaches us to pray is "hallowed be thy name" (Matthew 6:9). And Scripture says every knee shall bow at the name of Jesus (Philippians 2:10).

> "But I say unto you, that every idle word that men shall speak, they shall render an account for it in the day of judgment."
*Matthew 12:36 (Douay-Rheims)*

## How serious is "OMG"?

Casual, thoughtless use of God's name out of habit is usually a venial sin. It's not usually done out of contempt. But it's still a sin, it's an easy one to stop, and it shows the world that God's name means nothing to you. Using God's name in anger ("God damn you") or to mock Him or the faith can be grave. Text "OMG" carries the same meaning.

## Why this hits hard today

"Oh my God" is one of the most common phrases in English. It's in every show, every group chat, every TikTok. Jesus' name is the only religious name commonly used as a swear word. Notice that nobody says "Oh my Buddha" when they drop their phone.

## What's at stake

Small habits shape the heart. If God's name is a filler word, it's hard for it to also be the name you pray to with reverence. Blasphemy, if deliberate and with full knowledge, is a mortal sin.

## How to fight it

- **Replace it.** Pick a substitute and stick with it ("oh my gosh," "wow," "dang").
- **Make reparation.** When you slip, or hear someone else, quietly say "Blessed be the name of Jesus" or a line from the Divine Praises.
- **Use His name on purpose.** Pray the Jesus Prayer: "Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner."
- **Bow your head** at the name of Jesus in the Creed and at Mass. It's an old custom and a good one.
