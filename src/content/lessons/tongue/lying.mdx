## The short version

A lie is saying something false with the intention to deceive (CCC 2482). Every lie is a sin. Small ones are venial. Lies that do serious harm are mortal. "White lies" are still lies.

## What the Church teaches

"Lying is the most direct offense against the truth. To lie is to speak or act against the truth in order to lead someone into error" (CCC 2483). How serious a lie is depends on the truth it distorts, the circumstances, the intentions, and the harm it causes (CCC 2484). A lie becomes a mortal sin when it seriously injures justice or charity (CCC 2484).

You don't have to tell everyone everything, though. You're allowed to keep secrets, stay silent, and refuse to answer when someone has no right to the truth (CCC 2488-2492). Discretion isn't lying.

St. Augustine and St. Thomas Aquinas both teach that lying is always wrong, even to achieve a good end (Augustine, *De Mendacio*; Aquinas, *ST* II-II, q. 110, a. 3). You can't do evil so that good may come.

## Scripture

> "You are of your father the devil... he is a liar, and the father thereof."
*John 8:44 (Douay-Rheims)*

Among the six things the Lord hates: "a lying tongue... a deceitful witness that uttereth lies" (Proverbs 6:16-19).

> "But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, they shall have their portion in the pool burning with fire and brimstone, which is the second death."
*Apocalypse 21:8 (Douay-Rheims)*

## Why this hits hard today

Fake profiles, filtered photos, lying to parents about where you are, lying on résumés, cheating with AI and passing it off as your own work, "I'm fine" when you're not. Deepfakes and AI-generated content make it harder to know what's true at all. Christians should be the people whose word you can trust.

## What's at stake

Lying separates you from Christ, who said "I am the truth" (John 14:6). Habitual lying makes it impossible for people to trust you, and eventually you start believing your own lies. Lies that harm someone's reputation, steal from them, or break a sworn oath are grave.

## How to fight it

- **Catch yourself mid-lie** and correct it: "Actually, that's not true."
- **Stop exaggerating.** It's the gateway lie.
- **If you don't want to answer, say so.** "I'd rather not say" isn't a lie.
- **Confess lies by type:** to parents, to cover up, to hurt someone, etc.
- **Repair damage** caused by your lies (CCC 2487).
