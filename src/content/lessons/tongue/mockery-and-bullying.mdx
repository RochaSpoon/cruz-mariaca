## The short version

Mocking a person made in God's image is a sin against charity. Proverbs says "he that despiseth the poor, reproacheth his Maker" (Proverbs 17:5), and Jesus ties contempt to judgment (Matthew 5:22).

## What the Church teaches

Scripture says that whoever mocks the poor reproaches his Maker (Proverbs 17:5).

Jesus says that whoever calls his brother a fool will be liable to the fire of hell (Matthew 5:22).

The Catechism says "boasting or bragging is an offense against truth. So is irony aimed at disparaging someone by maliciously caricaturing some aspect of his behavior" (CCC 2481).

Respect for reputation "forbids every attitude and word likely to cause them unjust injury" (CCC 2477).

Everyone is made in the image of God (CCC 1700 and 2258 say human life is sacred and dignified from its beginning).

## Scripture

> "He that despiseth the poor, reproacheth his Maker; and he that rejoiceth at another man's ruin, shall not be unpunished."
*Proverbs 17:5 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "But I say to you, that whosoever is angry with his brother, shall be in danger of the judgment. And whosoever shall say to his brother, Raca, shall be in danger of the council. And whosoever shall say, Thou Fool, shall be in danger of hell fire."
*Matthew 5:22 (Douay-Rheims)*

Both verses are about contempt. The first links insult to the Creator, and the second links it to judgment.

## Why this matters today

Bullying now follows people home through their phones. The Church's standard does not change: respect for the person (CCC 2477).

Many bullies were bullied first, and many bystanders joined in only to avoid being next. Sin can be shared out among many people (CCC 1868).

## What's at stake

Mockery can wound a person for years. It also shapes the one who does it.

## How to live it

- **Do not laugh** at the joke at someone's expense.
- **Stand with the target** where you safely can.
- **Tell an adult** when it is serious.
- **Apologize** to anyone you have mocked.
