## The short version

The Church holds that "promises must be kept" (CCC 2410) and that a false oath, perjury, is "a grave lack of respect for the Lord of all speech" (CCC 2152).

## What the Church teaches

"The second commandment forbids false oaths. Taking an oath or swearing is to take God as witness to what one affirms" (CCC 2150).

"A person commits perjury when he makes a promise under oath with no intention of keeping it, or when after promising on oath he does not keep it. Perjury is a grave lack of respect for the Lord of all speech" (CCC 2152).

Jesus says, "Do not swear at all... Let what you say be simply 'Yes' or 'No'" (Matthew 5:34-37; CCC 2153).

The Church "has understood Jesus' words as not excluding oaths made for grave and right reasons (for example, in court)." An oath "cannot be taken unless in truth, in judgment, and in justice" (CCC 2154).

"Promises must be kept and contracts strictly observed to the extent that the commitments made in them are morally just" (CCC 2410).

An oath required by illegitimate authority "may be refused" (CCC 2155).

## Scripture

> "Again you have heard that it was said to them of old, Thou shalt not forswear thyself: but thou shalt perform thy oaths to the Lord. But I say to you not to swear at all, neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God: Nor by the earth, for it is his footstool: nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great king: Neither shalt thou swear by thy head, because thou canst not make one hair white or black. But let your speech be yea, yea: no, no: and that which is over and above these, is of evil."
*Matthew 5:33-37 (Douay-Rheims)*

Jesus tells his hearers to let their speech be yes or no. The tradition reads this as a call to be trustworthy without oaths, while still allowing them in serious cases.

## Why this matters today

Many people treat promises as non-binding. "I will try" is easier than "I will." The Church says a promise creates an obligation (CCC 2410).

Marriage vows, baptismal promises and priestly promises are all oaths or promises of the highest weight.

## What's at stake

Trust depends on promises being kept. A society where nobody keeps their word cannot function.

## How to live it

- **Do not promise** what you may not do.
- **Keep the ones you made,** even small ones.
- **Say yes or no** and mean it.
- **If you cannot keep one,** say so early.
