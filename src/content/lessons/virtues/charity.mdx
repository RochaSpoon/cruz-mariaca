## The short version

Charity is loving God above all things for his own sake, and your neighbor as yourself for the love of God. The Catechism calls it superior to all the virtues (CCC 1822, 1826).

## What the Church teaches

"Charity is the theological virtue by which we love God above all things for his own sake, and our neighbor as ourselves for the love of God" (CCC 1822).

Jesus makes it "the new commandment": "that you love one another as I have loved you" (CCC 1823).

It reaches beyond friends. "The Lord asks us to love as he does, even our enemies, to make ourselves the neighbor of those farthest away, and to love children and the poor as Christ himself" (CCC 1825).

"If I... have not charity, I am nothing." "Charity is superior to all the virtues" (CCC 1826).

Charity is "the form of the virtues." It "articulates and orders them among themselves" (CCC 1827).

The fruits of charity are "joy, peace, and mercy." It "demands beneficence and fraternal correction" (CCC 1829).

## Scripture

> "If I speak with the tongues of men, and of angels, and have not charity, I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal. And if I should have prophecy and should know all mysteries, and all knowledge, and if I should have all faith, so that I could remove mountains, and have not charity, I am nothing. And if I should distribute all my goods to feed the poor, and if I should deliver my body to be burned, and have not charity, it profiteth me nothing. Charity is patient, is kind: charity envieth not, dealeth not perversely; is not puffed up; Is not ambitious, seeketh not her own, is not provoked to anger, thinketh no evil; Rejoiceth not in iniquity, but rejoiceth with the truth; Beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things."
*1 Corinthians 13:1-7 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "And now there remain faith, hope, and charity, these three: but the greatest of these is charity."
*1 Corinthians 13:13 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims uses "charity" where most modern translations say "love." It is the same word.

## Why this matters today

Charity today sounds like donating to a cause. In the Catechism it is a virtue of the heart that includes giving but is bigger than it.

Some people think love means never saying anything hard. The Catechism includes "fraternal correction" in the fruits of charity (CCC 1829).

## What's at stake

Without charity, even good works are empty (CCC 1826). With it, small things count.

## How to live it

- **Do one thing today for someone** who cannot repay you.
- **Pray for an enemy by name.** The Catechism asks it (CCC 1825).
- **Read 1 Corinthians 13** and check yourself against each line.
- **Correct someone gently** when you have to, and never to win.
