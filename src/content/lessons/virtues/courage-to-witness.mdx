## The short version

The Catechism says Christians must "profess" the faith "without equivocation" when witness is required, and that all Christians have an obligation to witness by their lives and words (CCC 2471-2472, 1816).

## What the Church teaches

"Before Pilate, Christ proclaims that he 'has come into the world, to bear witness to the truth.' The Christian is not to 'be ashamed then of testifying to our Lord'" (CCC 2471).

"In situations that require witness to the faith, the Christian must profess it without equivocation, after the example of St. Paul before his judges" (CCC 2471).

The disciple "must not only keep the faith and live on it, but also profess it, confidently bear witness to it, and spread it" (CCC 1816).

"Witness is an act of justice." All Christians "by the example of their lives and the witness of their word, wherever they live, have an obligation" to bear witness (CCC 2472).

Martyrdom is "the supreme witness given to the truth of the faith" (CCC 2473).

## Scripture

> "Every one therefore that shall confess me before men, I will also confess him before my Father who is in heaven. But he that shall deny me before men, I will also deny him before my Father who is in heaven."
*Matthew 10:32-33 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "For God hath not given us the spirit of fear: but of power, and of love, and of sobriety. Be not thou therefore ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner: but labour with the gospel, according to the power of God,"
*2 Timothy 1:7-8 (Douay-Rheims)*

In 2 Timothy, Paul tells his young colleague not to be ashamed of the testimony of the Lord, and says the Lord has not given a spirit of fear.

## Why this matters today

Most of us will never face a court. We will face a classroom, a group chat, or a family dinner where saying you believe costs something small and real.

The Church does not ask you to argue with everyone. The Catechism says to witness "in words and deeds" (CCC 2472), and the deeds count first.

## What's at stake

If faith stays private, it never costs you anything, and it never reaches anyone else.

## How to live it

- **Say grace in public.**
- **Answer honestly** when someone asks what you believe.
- **Keep a clear conscience** (CCC 2471), so your witness is not hypocritical.
- **Be kind.** An unkind witness does harm to the truth.
