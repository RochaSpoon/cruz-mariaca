## The short version

Faith is the theological virtue by which we believe in God and all he has revealed, because he is truth itself (CCC 1814). It is a gift and a free act.

## What the Church teaches

"Faith is the theological virtue by which we believe in God and believe all that he has said and revealed to us, and that Holy Church proposes for our belief, because he is truth itself" (CCC 1814).

"By faith 'man freely commits his entire self to God.' For this reason the believer seeks to know and do God's will" (CCC 1814).

Faith is certain: "It is more certain than all human knowledge because it is founded on the very word of God who cannot lie" (CCC 157).

"Faith seeks understanding." A believer wants to know better the One he believes in (CCC 158).

Faith has to be lived. "Faith apart from works is dead." When it is "deprived of hope and love, faith does not fully unite the believer to Christ" (CCC 1815).

And it is to be shared: the disciple "must not only keep the faith and live on it, but also profess it, confidently bear witness to it, and spread it" (CCC 1816).

## Scripture

> "Now faith is the substance of things to be hoped for, the evidence of things that appear not. For by this the ancients obtained a testimony. By faith we understand that the world was framed by the word of God; that from invisible things visible things might be made."
*Hebrews 11:1-3 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "By faith he that is called Abraham, obeyed to go out into a place which he was to receive for an inheritance; and he went out, not knowing whither he went."
*Hebrews 11:8 (Douay-Rheims)*

Hebrews 11 is the Church's long list of people who trusted God. The Catechism uses Abraham as the model (CCC 145-146).

## Why this matters today

Some think faith means believing without evidence. The Catechism says faith is above reason but is never against it (CCC 159).

Others think it is a feeling. The Church says it is a decision to trust, which can hold when the feeling goes.

## What's at stake

Without faith, God is a theory. With it, he is someone you can obey, question and love.

## How to live it

- **Say the Creed slowly** and mean each line.
- **Ask your doubts out loud,** in prayer or with a friend. Faith seeks understanding.
- **Do one thing this week** that you would only do if God were real.
- **Tell one person** what you believe, without apology.
