## The short version

Obedience in the Church's sense is freely listening and submitting to a word you trust. The Catechism holds up Abraham, Mary and Christ himself as examples (CCC 144, 615).

## What the Church teaches

"To obey (from the Latin ob-audire, to 'hear or listen to') in faith is to submit freely to the word that has been heard, because its truth is guaranteed by God, who is Truth itself" (CCC 144).

"Abraham is the model of such obedience offered us by Sacred Scripture. The Virgin Mary is its most perfect embodiment" (CCC 144).

Christ is the center of it. Through "his obedience unto death," Jesus accomplished our redemption, and "as by one man's disobedience many were made sinners, so by one man's obedience many will be made righteous" (CCC 615).

The Catechism says freedom is "a force for growth and maturity in truth and goodness" and "attains its perfection when directed toward God" (CCC 1731).

Obedience is not the same as doing whatever you are told. The citizen "is obliged in conscience not to follow the directives of civil authorities when they are contrary to the demands of the moral order" (CCC 2242).

## Scripture

> "And whereas indeed he was the Son of God, he learned obedience by the things which he suffered: And being consummated, he became, to all that obey him, the cause of eternal salvation."
*Hebrews 5:8-9 (Douay-Rheims)*

> "He humbled himself, becoming obedient unto death, even to the death of the cross."
*Philippians 2:8 (Douay-Rheims)*

The first verse says Jesus "learned obedience by the things which he suffered." It speaks of his human nature.

## Why this matters today

Obedience has a bad name, and the Church shares part of the worry. It says obedience must be free and must serve what is true (CCC 144, 2242).

Some people say they obey no one. The Catechism says freedom reaches its perfection when it is directed toward God (CCC 1731).

## What's at stake

If you obey everything, you lose your conscience. If you obey nothing, you have no teacher and no direction. The Church's picture is obedience that is free and heard.

## How to live it

- **Ask who you actually listen to** and whether they deserve it.
- **Obey in one small thing** you would normally resist.
- **Read Philippians 2:5-11.**
- **Know where to stop.** Conscience still comes before orders (CCC 2242).
