## The short version

The fruits of the Spirit are the signs of his work in you. The Church's tradition lists twelve: charity, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, generosity, gentleness, faithfulness, modesty, self-control and chastity (CCC 1832).

## What the Church teaches

"The fruits of the Spirit are perfections that the Holy Spirit forms in us as the first fruits of eternal glory" (CCC 1832).

"The tradition of the Church lists twelve of them: 'charity, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, generosity, gentleness, faithfulness, modesty, self-control, chastity'" (CCC 1832).

Paul lists them in Galatians, in contrast to "the works of the flesh" (Galatians 5:19-23).

They are called fruits because they grow. They are not tasks to tick off.

## Scripture

> "But the fruit of the Spirit is, charity, joy, peace, patience, benignity, goodness, longanimity, Mildness, faith, modesty, continency, chastity. Against such there is no law."
*Galatians 5:22-23 (Douay-Rheims)*

The Douay-Rheims lists the twelve in older English: charity, joy, peace, patience, benignity, goodness, longanimity, mildness, faith, modesty, continency, chastity.

## Why this matters today

Many people use the list as a self-help checklist. The Catechism calls the fruits perfections the Spirit forms in us.

If you cannot find these in yourself, the Church would say pray and receive the sacraments. Do not just try harder.

## What's at stake

The fruits are the test. A faith with no joy, peace or patience over time is worth asking questions about.

## How to live it

- **Pick one fruit you lack** and ask for it.
- **Notice which ones show up** when you pray regularly.
- **Ask a friend** which one they see in you.
- **Be patient with growth.** Fruit takes time.
