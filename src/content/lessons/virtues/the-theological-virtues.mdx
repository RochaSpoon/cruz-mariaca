## The short version

Faith, hope and charity are called theological because they relate directly to God and are given by him (CCC 1812-1813).

## What the Church teaches

"The human virtues are rooted in the theological virtues, which adapt man's faculties for participation in the divine nature." They have "the One and Triune God for their origin, motive, and object" (CCC 1812).

They are "infused by God into the souls of the faithful to make them capable of acting as his children" (CCC 1813). We cannot produce them by effort alone.

**Faith** is the virtue by which we believe in God and all that he has revealed. By faith "man freely commits his entire self to God" (CCC 1814).

**Hope** is the virtue by which we desire the kingdom of heaven "placing our trust in Christ's promises and relying not on our own strength, but on the help of the grace of the Holy Spirit" (CCC 1817).

**Charity** is the virtue "by which we love God above all things for his own sake, and our neighbor as ourselves for the love of God" (CCC 1822). "Charity is superior to all the virtues" (CCC 1826).

## Scripture

> "And now there remain faith, hope, and charity, these three: but the greatest of these is charity."
*1 Corinthians 13:13 (Douay-Rheims)*

Paul names the three together here. The Catechism quotes this verse to say the greatest of them is charity (CCC 1826).

## Why this matters today

People use "faith" for a vague feeling. The Church says it is trust in God and belief in what he said (CCC 1814).

Many Protestants and Catholics agree that faith is a gift of God. They differ on how faith, hope and charity work together in a life.

## What's at stake

Without the theological virtues, you can be a decent person and still not be turned toward God. They are the ones that point you at him.

## How to live it

- **Ask for them.** The Catechism says they are given by God (CCC 1813).
- **Practice each once today:** a prayer of trust, a hope said aloud, and one act of love.
- **Read 1 Corinthians 13** and replace "love" with your own name.
- **Do not wait to feel them.**
